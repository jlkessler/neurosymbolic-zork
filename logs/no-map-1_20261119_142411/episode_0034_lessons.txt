LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go up the tree and 'take egg'; +5 was recorded. Do it early, then head straight for the house.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into the Kitchen (+10 recorded again), then west to the Living Room without detours.
3. After 'open case', immediately 'put egg in case'. Previously +5; this episode stalled before depositing. Verify score and inventory afterward.
4. Run inventory before the trap door. Confirm the lantern is carried and lit and the sword is in hand; trap door 'down' recorded +25.
5. Use the sword on the troll, then go east (+5 recorded). Examine new rooms and try room-specific commands for new score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never descend without 'take lantern'. Lighting it while it lies in the room is not carrying it; the dark Cellar led to grue death (-10).
2. Never move in a dark room. Check light and inventory first; the unlit Cellar north move ended in death.
3. Avoid forest wandering, mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle and Attic detours. Once you hold the egg, take the known window route home.
4. Don't open the case and then do something else. Stalling before the deposit left the egg undeposited when the episode ended.
5. Don't attack unexplained objects such as the pile at Entrance Hades; it recorded death. Inspect first and don't repeat zero-score actions like 'touch mirror'.
