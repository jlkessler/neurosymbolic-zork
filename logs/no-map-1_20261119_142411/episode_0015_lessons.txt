LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House, go west, east, then up and take the egg (+5 confirmed). Then head straight to Behind House; skip the house-perimeter wandering.
2. At Behind House, open window then go west; +10 was recorded on that move. Continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, open the case and put the egg in at once; +5 was recorded. Then check the score.
4. Push rug, open trap, down gave +25 here. Take the lantern and light it before descending, because the trap door shuts behind you.
5. Underground, use look after every move and pick one unexplored lit exit. If you find the Gallery art (+4 earlier), carry it up to the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in the dark. North from the dark Cellar caused grue death (-10) this episode. Light the lantern before any underground move.
2. Never jump at East Chasm (death, -10). Never shuttle north/south between Cellar and East Chasm.
3. Don't wander West/North/South House to reach the Living Room with the egg. That cost about 8 steps before the window route was found.
4. Don't use the mailbox as storage for the egg, canary or leaflet. Earlier shuffles scored 0, and taking the leaflet this episode did too.
5. Don't spend turns in the dark on useless actions like dropping the leaflet. Avoid odd egg commands (shake, close, 'take on egg'); an earlier egg broke after one.
