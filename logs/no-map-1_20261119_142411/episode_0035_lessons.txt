LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Up a Tree, 'take egg' recorded +5. Do it early, then go straight to the house with the egg.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then west into the Kitchen recorded +10. Take that route home immediately.
3. From the Kitchen, go west to the Living Room, then 'put egg in case' immediately after 'open case'. Verify score and inventory.
4. Before descending, run inventory. Confirm the lantern is carried and lit and the sword is in hand; only then try the trap door.
5. Once the egg is deposited, explore new rooms and test room-specific commands for new score; log score changes after each action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't wander around North/West/South House and Forest while carrying the egg; this episode lost about nine steps. Go to Behind House.
2. Never stop after 'open case'. Next action must be 'put egg in case'. This episode ended with the egg undeposited.
3. Never descend without 'take lantern'. A lit lantern lying in the room is not carried; dark Cellar moves caused grue death (-10).
4. Avoid wasteful detours: mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle, Attic, and repeated forest loops. Prioritize known score routes and new rooms.
5. Don't turn on the lantern in the lit Living Room as filler. Use steps for deposit, inventory checks, and progress; ask why before acting.
