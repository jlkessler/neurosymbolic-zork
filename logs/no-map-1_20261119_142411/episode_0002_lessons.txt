LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Climb and examine trees, mailbox and other local features early; here, taking the egg in the tree was the only confirmed +5.
2. After a score, retrace the exact inbound path (Up a Tree → Forest Path → North House → West House) instead of improvising a route.
3. Check each exit once per room and keep a written list of tried directions; prefer untried exits over repeating known loops.
4. Verify any score claim against a recorded score delta before repeating an action; take egg gave +5 once, and re-takes gave 0.
5. Use plain verbs (open, take, examine) on listed objects; they gave clear feedback, while odd compound commands gave jokes or damage.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not throw, shake or otherwise roughly handle fragile treasures; 'throw egg at tree' damaged the egg and shaking repeatedly spilled its contents.
2. Do not repeat the open/close/shake/put cycle on the same container more than once; about 30 such steps gave 0 score.
3. Do not loop Forest Path, Clearing and Forest with 'go around' commands; about 60 moves there found no new route or score.
4. Do not use ordinary items as tools on a treasure (leaflet in egg, canary in egg) without a clue; each attempt gave 0 and no new information.
5. Do not keep calling a return-route goal while moving randomly; with no plan back to the House, reread the observation and room descriptions for exit hints.
