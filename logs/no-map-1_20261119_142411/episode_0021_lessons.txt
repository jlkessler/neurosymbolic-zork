LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Before descending: take lantern, check inventory for it, turn it on, then go down. Last episode did this without dying, but skipped the inventory check.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen gave +10 recorded. Repeat early; it is cheap and verified.
3. Push rug, open trap door, down recorded +25 on that move. Repeat only with the lantern confirmed carried and lit.
4. Earlier runs recorded egg +5 and egg in case +5. Do both on the surface, before descending, since the trap door shuts behind you.
5. From Cellar, south crawlway leads to East Chasm, then east to Gallery. After a new room, take an unexplored exit or try a new interaction.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while pitch black or descend without a lit, carried lantern; grue death is the known fatal pattern. Don't assume items are held without checking inventory.
2. Don't oscillate Cellar↔East Chasm. Two north/south cycles gave zero score; after a move, take a new exit instead of reversing.
3. Don't waste turns on aimless put/take, leaflet-in-mailbox, or open/close/shake sack in the dark. Collect items only with a stated purpose.
4. Don't go up to the Attic or other dark rooms without a lit lantern; the Actor spent about eight stalled turns there.
5. Don't descend with surface scoring unfinished. The trap door bars behind you, so bank known points first, and don't re-enter the hostile Troll Room unarmed.
