LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree early; the +5 was confirmed. Then carry it carefully, without 'take on' or other odd commands.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into the Kitchen gave a confirmed +10. Reuse it as the entry route.
3. Open the trophy case and 'put egg in case' gave +2 in the Living Room. Deposit carried valuables there once safe.
4. Take the lantern, push rug, open trap door, turn lantern on, then down gave a confirmed +25. Verify it is carried and lit.
5. Unexplored side rooms can pay: 'take art' in the Gallery gave +4. After any score, run look and pick one lit exit target.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'jump' at a chasm or edge. It caused a death and -10. Avoid risky verbs unless the text confirms they are safe.
2. Do not oscillate north/south between the Cellar and East Chasm; about 20 such moves produced zero score. Pick a new exit.
3. Avoid zero-score house-perimeter detours (mailbox, canary, leaflet, repeated West/South House loops) before banking known score routes.
4. Do not issue malformed or odd commands such as 'take on egg', or 'give thief leaflet'. The egg was damaged and nothing scored.
5. Do not drop and re-take a valuable for no reason. Assume the trap door is barred after descent and plan a lit exploration route.
