LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Opening and examining local objects early (mailbox, tree) found the only confirmed score event; keep checking nearby features before wandering.
2. When feedback says a passage is open or allows entry, use it immediately instead of walking away.
3. After each action, re-read the observation for state changes such as damage or revealed features, and update the plan.
4. Moving or taking scenery objects can reveal new features; follow up by examining the new feature before leaving.
5. Repeat only actions with a recorded score delta; confirm the +5 source before generalizing it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not drop valuables or tools into the grating or other openings just to test for score; they fell out of reach into the darkness and were lost.
2. Do not cycle open/close on the same container (nest, mailbox) more than once; no new information or score came from it.
3. Do not re-take an item already awarded points expecting more points; repeated take/drop of the egg gave 0.
4. Avoid odd or ambiguous commands such as 'take on egg' while carrying a fragile treasure; that handling damaged the egg.
5. Avoid looping Forest/Clearing with 'go around' commands; track tried exits and leave the area after two fruitless passes.
