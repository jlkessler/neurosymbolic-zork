LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen early; west recorded +10 again. Then head straight to the Living Room.
2. Move the rug, open the trap door and descend; this recorded +25 again. The door shuts behind you, so prepare first.
3. Take the sword and lantern before descending. Confirm the lantern is carried and lit before the first underground move.
4. Kill the troll only while carrying the sword; the kill then moving east recorded +5. Fight only with it in hand.
5. Examine and take items in newly reached rooms: Gallery art recorded +4. Try a special room's unusual command, like 'echo', for the bar (+10).

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move blindly in a dark room after arriving. This run's first Cellar move, south, ended in grue death (-10). Check light first.
2. Don't attack or break unexplained objects such as the Entrance Hades pile; 'break pile with sword' recorded -10 and death.
3. Don't open the mailbox or take the leaflet, sack, bottle or Attic early; about nine zero-score moves delayed exploration this run.
4. Don't shuttle between Cellar and East Chasm or repeat 'touch mirror'; neither recorded any score or progress.
5. Don't wander carrying treasure without banking it in the trophy case; test unexplored exits purposefully and stop when rooms recur.
