LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Verified house route: West House north to North House, east to Behind House, open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded). Repeat it directly.
2. Take the sword in the Living Room and keep it; check inventory before going underground so you are armed for the Troll Room.
3. Light the lantern before descending; it worked in the Living Room this run. The trap door shuts behind you, so be ready first.
4. After 'push rug' reveals the trap door, open it and go down (+25 recorded earlier) instead of exploring side rooms.
5. Take visible treasure early (Up a Tree egg +5), then deposit it with an explicit 'put egg in case' after opening the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed: an earlier run recorded -10, a knockout and being thrown back to the Forest.
2. Don't oscillate between rooms (Kitchen↔Attic here, Cellar↔East Chasm earlier). After one zero-score revisit, pick an untried exit or a different goal.
3. Don't spend moves taking and returning junk (leaflet) or giving items to the troll or thief ('give troll painting' recorded -10).
4. Don't drop treasure in the Living Room and leave it; the egg sat on the floor, never deposited or recovered.
5. Don't loop Forest/Clearing with no score; one run burned 500 steps this way. After about 30 zero-score moves, return to the house.
