LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take egg at Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then return via Behind House: open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded), west to Living Room.
2. In Living Room, open case, then put egg in case (+5 recorded) before going underground.
3. Push rug, open trap, down gave +25 on the move. Repeat it only after the lantern is confirmed in inventory and lit.
4. Read each observation after moving. 'Pitch black' or a grue warning means fix light or stop, not explore.
5. Before irreversible steps, run inventory and look. The trap door crashed shut behind the player, so check supplies first.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never descend without 'take lantern' plus an inventory check. Here the lantern was switched on but never taken, and the Cellar was pitch black.
2. Never move in pitch darkness. Cellar 'east' with no light caused the grue death (-10) and ended the run at 35/80.
3. Don't trust internal notes like 'I have the lantern' without inventory evidence; that claim was unsupported in this episode.
4. Don't take risks underground with 45 points banked; dying forfeited all further scoring and cost 10 points.
5. Prior note says Cellar north leads to a hostile Troll Room; don't enter unarmed. Try unexplored Cellar south only with a lit, held lantern.
