LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the lantern, confirm it in inventory, turn it on, then go down. This worked here, and the descent recorded +25.
2. Open the window and enter the Kitchen early; +10 was recorded on that move. Bank surface items before the trap door shuts behind you.
3. Carry a weapon before entering hostile rooms. The sword killed the troll here, and the +5 came on the later east move.
4. After taking treasure, route straight back to the trophy case. Verify the score delta and inventory after taking or depositing.
5. When a room's exits are exhausted, take an unused exit, such as the Cellar south crawlway, and explore past the first room beyond it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black or descend without a lit lantern confirmed in inventory. Turning it on in the Living Room does not mean it is carried.
2. Don't fight, give items to, or linger with the thief. Giving away the sword and material ended in death and -10.
3. Don't loop Cellar↔East Chasm, Deep Canyon↔North-South Passage, or Loud Room up/down. After two repeats with no score, pick an untested action.
4. Don't drop or abandon treasure like the platinum bar underground. Carry it home, because the Actor never verified a return.
5. Don't put and take items repeatedly, or pick up garlic and food with no purpose. Avoid pressing machine buttons while carrying treasure.
