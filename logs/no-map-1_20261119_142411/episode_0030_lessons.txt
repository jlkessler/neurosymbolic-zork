LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the opening: north, east, open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded). Then go straight to the Living Room.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern and turn it on before descending. It was lit this run and the Cellar was navigable.
3. Sequence push rug, open trap, down quickly; the descent recorded +25. Expect the trap door to shut behind you.
4. Also take the sword in the Living Room, and run 'inventory' once before descending to confirm sword and lit lantern.
5. After arriving in the Cellar, test untried exits (the south crawlway reached East Chasm) and follow them to new rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed, and don't stay there. Another action beside the troll cost -10 and ended the episode.
2. Don't repeat a move that yielded nothing: Cellar-Troll Room was shuttled five times with no gain. Change approach after one failed probe.
3. Don't issue nonsense commands like 'take walls' during a threat; use a purposeful action or retreat.
4. Don't spend moves on the sack, Attic, leaflet, mailbox or food; none recorded any score.
5. Don't go underground without confirming the sword is carried; the sword was left in the Living Room this run.
