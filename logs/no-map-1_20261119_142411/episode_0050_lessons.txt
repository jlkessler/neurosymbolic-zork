LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House, west, east, up reached Up a Tree; 'take egg' recorded +5. Repeat it, then head for Behind House.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on entering the Kitchen. Continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately and check the score.
4. 'take lantern' and 'take sword' both succeeded in the Living Room before descending. Keep taking both, and confirm with inventory.
5. 'push rug', 'open trap', 'down' recorded +25 on 'down', reaching the Cellar. Repeat it, trimming detours, for 45 points by about step 13.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never skip 'inventory' before 'down'. It was never run this episode, so gear and light status were unverified.
2. Don't open combat with a single 'hit troll with sword'. The troll dodged, killed you, and -10 was recorded. Reassess before attacking.
3. Avoid scoreless detours: Forest west/east and the Forest Path south to North House east loop cost four steps this run.
4. Don't light the lantern after descending. You arrived in pitch black with a grue warning, so light it in the Living Room first.
5. Don't move north from the Cellar without first looking and reviewing inventory. The Troll Room encounter ended the episode two actions later.
