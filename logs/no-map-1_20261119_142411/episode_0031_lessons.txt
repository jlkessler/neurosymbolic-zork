LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen early; the west move recorded +10. Go straight to the Living Room afterward.
2. Take the sword and lantern, light the lantern, then descend. This run's trap-door descent recorded +25 with both items carried.
3. Fight the troll only while carrying the sword; hitting it with the sword killed it and the next move east recorded +5.
4. Examine and take loose items in newly reached rooms; the Gallery art recorded +4 and the Loud Room bar +10.
5. Try the room's unusual command ('echo') when a room seems special; it revealed the bar. Take what it reveals.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't attack or break unexplained objects such as the pile at Entrance Hades; 'break pile with sword' recorded -10 and death.
2. Don't alternate Cellar and East Chasm repeatedly; the trap door shut behind you. Pick a different unexplored exit after one failed probe.
3. Don't repeat 'touch mirror' after no score or progress; four touches in Mirror Room changed nothing recorded.
4. Don't spend early moves on leaflet, sack, bottle or Attic; none recorded score and they delay exploration.
5. Don't wander with treasure while hunting a return route; test unexplored exits purposefully and stop if the same rooms keep recurring.
