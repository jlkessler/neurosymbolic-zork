LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Forest Path, try 'up' then 'take egg' first; the earlier +5 came from this sequence. Confirm the score change after taking it.
2. With the egg, go to Behind House, 'open window', then west into the Kitchen: +10 was recorded earlier. Repeat it directly.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Keep this two-step deposit and verify the score.
4. Take the lantern and turn it on before 'down'; the trap door earlier recorded +25 and the Cellar was navigable.
5. Before the Troll Room, run inventory and confirm sword and lantern are carried. Use combat commands against the troll, then go east once it is dead.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't spend early steps on exploratory forest moves like 'west' from West House. Go to the known egg and window route instead.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed. An earlier episode died there (-10) after taking only the lantern and no sword.
3. Don't use non-combat actions like 'take walls' near the troll, and don't go 'north' while it blocks the way.
4. Don't oscillate north/south between the Cellar and Troll Room without new information. Prepare first, then commit once.
5. Don't loop West/South/North House and Forest while carrying the egg. Take the shortest path to Behind House and the window.
