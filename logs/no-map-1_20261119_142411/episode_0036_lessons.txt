LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From Forest Path 'up' then 'take egg' recorded +5 again. Do it early and keep the egg safe.
2. With the egg, go to Behind House, 'open window', then west into the Kitchen: +10 recorded. Repeat it directly, without detours.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' followed immediately by 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Keep that two-step deposit and verify the score.
4. 'take lantern' and 'turn on lantern' before 'down' preceded +25 at the trap door, and the Cellar was navigable. Keep lantern carried and lit.
5. Before leaving the Living Room, run inventory and confirm lantern and sword are carried. Do this before any Troll Room entry.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed. This episode went north from the Cellar without a sword and died there (-10).
2. Don't spend turns on junk actions like 'take walls' next to a hostile troll; the axe hit immediately. Retreat or act purposefully.
3. Don't loop North/West/South House and Forest while carrying the egg (about nine wasted steps). From North House go east to Behind House.
4. Don't oscillate Cellar/Troll Room (north, south, north, south) without new information. Prepare first, then commit once.
5. Don't make exploratory forest moves like 'go around trees' or west into Forest from West House. Reach the known route and score opportunities directly.
