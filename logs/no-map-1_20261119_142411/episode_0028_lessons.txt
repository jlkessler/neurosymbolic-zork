LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the verified house route: North House, Behind House, open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded). Skip the mailbox.
2. Take the sword in the Living Room and keep it; check inventory shows it before going underground.
3. After 'push rug', open the trap door and descend (+25 recorded). It shuts behind you, so prepare first.
4. Use 'take lantern' explicitly, turn it on, and confirm with 'inventory' that you carry it before descending.
5. Take visible treasure early (Up a Tree egg +5), then open the case and 'put egg in case'.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move while in darkness: 'south' from the pitch-black Cellar got you eaten by a grue (-10 recorded). Fix the light first.
2. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed: an earlier run recorded -10, a knockout and being thrown back out.
3. Don't waste moves on the leaflet, sack or Kitchen↔Attic bounce, or on Cellar↔East Chasm oscillation; try an untried exit after one zero-score revisit.
4. Don't drop treasure in the Living Room and leave it; the egg sat on the floor, never deposited.
5. Don't give items to the troll or thief ('give troll painting' recorded -10), or loop Forest/Clearing for 30+ zero-score moves.
