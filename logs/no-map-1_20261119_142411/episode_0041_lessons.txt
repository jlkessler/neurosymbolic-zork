LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From Forest Path go 'up' and 'take egg'; +5 recorded on the take action again. Carry it straight home.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west'; +10 was recorded on the move into the Kitchen. Repeat it directly.
3. In the Living Room, right after 'open case', immediately run 'put egg in case', then check score and inventory. Deposit still unverified.
4. Return route that worked: Forest Path south, east to Behind House, open window, west twice to the Living Room, in 8 moves.
5. Before descending: take sword and lantern, push rug, open trap door, go down (+25 earlier). Attack troll with combat commands, then east (+5).

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't waste early steps on the mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle or Attic; none produced score or progress.
2. Don't enter the Troll Room without the sword, and don't take garlic or give the troll garlic there (knocked out, -10).
3. Don't say 'north' while the troll blocks the way, and don't shuffle between Cellar and East Chasm. Prepare once, then commit.
4. Don't let 'turn on lantern' or other filler follow 'open case'; the egg stayed undeposited and the run ended at 12 steps.
5. Don't ignore a mismatch between your stated plan and the move issued; the first step said Kitchen but went west to the Forest.
