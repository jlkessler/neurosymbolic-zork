LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in the tree once; it was the only confirmed score event (+5). Repeat visits scored 0, so do not return.
2. Use plain verbs (take, open, examine) on listed objects; they gave clear feedback, while odd compound commands only gave jokes or no effect.
3. After about 10 zero-score steps, switch to an unexplored exit; North House and Behind House were visited only briefly.
4. Track tried exits per room and try each untried one before revisiting; CanyView and North House got far less attention than the loops.
5. Before reusing a past scoring action, check that its score delta recurs; take egg gave +5 once and nothing on later attempts.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat take leaves / put down leaves / throw leaves cycles; about 100 such steps gave 0 score and no new information.
2. Do not shuttle Forest Path to Up a Tree and back without a new reason; it was repeated dozens of times for nothing.
3. Do not loop 'go around forest' through Clearing, Forest and CanyView; those cycles found no new route over hundreds of steps.
4. Do not throw or roughly handle fragile treasures; the egg ended up broken, and rough handling gave no score.
5. Do not keep a 'return to Living Room' goal with no route; reread room descriptions for exits and test unexplored ones instead.
