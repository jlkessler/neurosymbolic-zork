LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House go west, east to Forest Path, then 'up' and 'take egg'; this confirmed +5 in 4 moves. Skip the shuffling.
2. After taking the egg, verify score and inventory immediately, then head straight for Behind House to open the window and enter.
3. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits and pick one that is new, so each move gives fresh information.
4. Opening containers with plain verbs ('open mailbox') gave instant feedback; read any item revealed, such as the leaflet, for clues.
5. Carry the egg home to the Living Room and put it in the trophy case; check for a score change to confirm.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not alternate west/east between Forest and Forest Path; it burned four moves for 0 score and no new exits.
2. Do not end the episode with treasure in hand and no deposit; avoid dead-end states with no safe actions when holding the egg.
3. Do not trust inherited '+20' score claims; only a score change observed this episode counts as confirmed.
4. Do not step 'down' from the tree without a route plan; choose the next exit toward Behind House explicitly.
5. Do not ignore revealed items such as the leaflet; read or examine new objects once before moving on.
