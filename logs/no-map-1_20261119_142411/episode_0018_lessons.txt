LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House: west, east, up, take egg (+5 recorded). Then take the known route to Behind House without detours.
2. At Behind House, open window, then west; +10 was recorded on that move into Kitchen. Continue west to Living Room.
3. In Living Room, open case, put egg in case immediately (+5 recorded), before any underground trip.
4. Take lantern, turn it on, and run inventory to confirm it before pushing rug, opening trap, and going down (+25 recorded).
5. After each move underground, read the full text for threats or darkness. Choose the next action from that text, not from curiosity.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't re-enter a room that just showed a hostile creature. The Cellar-north-Troll Room-south-north repeat ended in -10 and episode end at 35/80.
2. Don't descend without an inventory check. This run relied on 'Taken.' text alone; the lantern was never verified.
3. Don't explore underground at the cost of the run. Score 35 was already banked; dying forfeited all remaining scoring.
4. Don't start with Forest west/east detours. The egg route from West House needs only west, east, up.
5. Don't use 'turn on lantern' unless the lantern was taken first, and don't move in darkness. Check inventory or look first.
