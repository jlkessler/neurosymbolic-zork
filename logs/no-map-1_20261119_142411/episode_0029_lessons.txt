LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the verified route: North House, Behind House, open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded). Skip the sack and food.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern and sword and turn the lantern on before going underground; this worked cleanly in 5 moves.
3. After 'push rug' reveals the trap door, open it and descend right away; prior runs recorded +25 for this.
4. Run 'inventory' once before descending to confirm the sword and the lit lantern are carried, rather than assuming it.
5. Spend the move budget on reaching new rooms: an episode that stops at 4 rooms leaves most of the score untouched.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move in darkness: 'south' from a pitch-black Cellar led to a grue death (-10 recorded). Confirm the lantern is lit first.
2. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed; an earlier run recorded -10, a knockout and being thrown back out.
3. Don't spend moves on the sack, food, leaflet, mailbox or Kitchen-Attic bounce; none recorded any score.
4. Don't end an episode at the open trap door: opening and descending were the next required steps and were never taken.
5. Don't give items to the troll or thief ('give troll painting' recorded -10), or loop Forest/Clearing for 30+ zero-score moves.
