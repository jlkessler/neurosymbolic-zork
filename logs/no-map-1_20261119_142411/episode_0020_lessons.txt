LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Before descending, take lantern, check inventory, turn it on, then go down. This episode followed it without a death, though inventory was never checked.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen gave +10 recorded. Repeat early; it is cheap and verified.
3. Push rug, open trap door, down gave +25 on that move. Repeat only with the lantern confirmed carried and lit.
4. Prior-run sequence: take egg at Up a Tree (+5), return via window, put egg in open case (+5). Do it before descending.
5. From the Cellar, the south crawlway reached East Chasm, then Gallery. Keep pushing past newly reached rooms toward unexplored exits.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black or descend without a lit, carried lantern; the prior run died to a grue this way. Never assert items are held without inventory.
2. Don't oscillate Cellar↔East Chasm. Three north/south cycles gave zero score; after a move, take an unexplored exit or a new interaction.
3. Don't spend turns on aimless put/take (leaflet in mailbox, repeated egg put/take) or collecting items with no stated purpose.
4. Don't enter the Troll Room via Cellar north unarmed; a prior note marks it hostile.
5. Don't descend with surface scoring unfinished. The trap door shuts and is barred behind you, so bank known points first.
