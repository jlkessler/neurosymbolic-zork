LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen; that entry coincided with a confirmed +10. Do it directly, without detours.
2. Forest Path, up, take egg gave +5 again this episode. Fetch it early, then confirm the score change before moving on.
3. On reaching the Living Room carrying the egg, open the case, then immediately 'put egg in case' and check score and inventory.
4. Plain verbs on listed objects (open sack, open case, take egg) give clear feedback; keep using them for quick local checks.
5. Keep a per-room list of untried exits and test them after depositing; Kitchen up/down and Living Room's other exits are still unexplored.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not put items back where they came from (put leaflet in mailbox); it scored 0 and spent steps without new information.
2. Do not wander Forest with 'go around trees' or repeated west moves; they gave no new exits or score.
3. Do not detour on a known route home (Forest Path south to North House); head straight to Behind House, west to the Kitchen, then west.
4. Do not stop after 'open case' while carrying treasure; let the Actor, not a recovery default like 'turn on lantern', finish the deposit.
5. Do not open the window and then walk away east to the Clearing; use the new entry right away.
