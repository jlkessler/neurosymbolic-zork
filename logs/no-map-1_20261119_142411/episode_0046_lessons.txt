LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Reach Up a Tree and 'take egg' (+5 recorded). Do it early, then head home by the shortest known route.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 entering the Kitchen. Repeat it, then continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately and check the score.
4. 'take lantern' before 'turn on lantern' worked, and the sword was also taken. Keep that gear order before any descent.
5. After 'push rug', open the trap door next. A prior run recorded +25 for the rug, trap door, then 'down'; verify it again.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't skip 'inventory' before 'down'. This run took the lantern and sword but never listed them, so carried gear went unconfirmed.
2. Don't open with Forest west/east wandering; those two steps scored nothing. Go directly toward the tree.
3. Don't detour Forest Path south to North House while carrying treasure. Unscored side trips delay the deposit and waste steps.
4. Don't end the run right after 'push rug'. The revealed trap door was left unopened, and the underground was never reached.
5. Never move in darkness or enter the Troll Room unarmed. An earlier run died moving south from a dark Cellar. Fix the light and arm first.
