LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Up a Tree, 'take egg' recorded +5. Go there directly, then return home without side detours.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 entering the Kitchen. Repeat it, then continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately and verify the score.
4. Take the sword and lantern, then turn the lantern on. Last run's order worked: 'take lantern' came before 'turn on lantern'.
5. After pushing the rug, open the trap door, run 'inventory' to confirm sword and lit lantern, then descend and 'look'.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't skip 'inventory' before 'down'. Last run took the gear but never listed it, so it never confirmed what was carried.
2. Don't spend early steps on Forest west/east or the North House detour; neither scored. Go to the tree, then home.
3. Don't end the run after 'push rug' without opening the trap door; the revealed door was left unopened and unscored.
4. Never move in darkness: an earlier run died after 'south' from a pitch-black Cellar (-10). If it's dark, fix the light first.
5. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed or shuffle between Cellar and Troll Room. Arm up, verify light, then commit to attacking.
