LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree first; the +5 was confirmed again. Then walk straight back to the house without side actions.
2. From Behind House, open the window and go west into the Kitchen (+10 on an earlier route). Do this before any other house probing.
3. In the Living Room, put the egg in the trophy case first; an earlier episode recorded +2 for it. Check the score afterward.
4. Before going down, take the lantern and light it. An earlier route through rug and trap door scored +25 on 'down'.
5. Underground, use look after each move and pick one unexplored lit exit. Carry the Gallery art (+4 earlier) up to the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle West House and South House. This episode repeated that loop about six times with zero score; try Behind House instead.
2. Don't use the mailbox as storage for the egg, canary or leaflet. Every mailbox shuffle this episode scored 0 and wasted steps.
3. Avoid odd commands on the egg ('take on egg', 'close', 'shake', 'put canary in egg'). It ended up broken after 'take on egg'; cause unconfirmed.
4. Never jump at East Chasm (death, -10) or move in the dark. Never shuttle north/south between Cellar and East Chasm.
5. Do not end with the egg undeposited. The trap door shuts behind you, so deposit first and then pick one concrete target.
