LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen early; +10 was recorded on that move. Bank easy surface items before descending.
2. Take the lantern, confirm it in inventory, turn it on, then go down. The descent recorded +25 here.
3. Carry the sword underground. This run had no weapon at the troll room and only took an axe nick, with no progress.
4. Use unexplored exits when a room is exhausted: Cellar south led to East Chasm and the Gallery, where 'take art' recorded +4.
5. After taking treasure, check inventory and score, then seek a verified route to the trophy case. Home return was never confirmed this run.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never give treasure or gear to the troll or thief. 'Give troll painting' recorded -10 and a knockout that ended the episode.
2. Don't drop carried treasure to 'explore'. The painting was put down in the Troll room and Gallery with no score benefit.
3. Don't oscillate Cellar↔East Chasm, Gallery↔Studio or Cellar↔Troll room. After two repeats with no score, try a different action or untested exit.
4. Don't repeatedly re-enter the troll room unarmed or hope it stops blocking. Each entry risks axe damage and gains nothing.
5. Don't waste early steps on mailbox put/take, or on grabbing food and a manual without purpose. Confirm each item's use before spending a move.
