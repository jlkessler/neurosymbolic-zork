LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House go west, east, then 'up' and 'take egg'; this reached the confirmed +5 in 6 moves. Skip the mailbox shuffling.
2. After taking the egg, check score and inventory, then plan a route toward Behind House or the Living Room before moving.
3. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits and choose a new one; at Forest Path try north, and at North House try its other exits.
4. Open containers and read what they reveal, such as the leaflet, before moving on. Do not take and re-drop items unread.
5. Take the egg, then bring it to the Living Room and put it in the trophy case; verify the score change observed.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not take an item and immediately put it back (the leaflet cost 2 moves for 0 score); read it or leave it.
2. Do not alternate west/east between Forest and Forest Path; it produced no score and no new exits.
3. Do not wander south into unexplored rooms while holding treasure without a return route; the episode ended in no_safe_actions at North House.
4. Do not trust inherited score claims like '+20' or '+25'; only a score change observed this episode counts.
5. Do not leave a room's exits unlisted; at Forest Path and North House unexplored exits remained when the episode ended.
