LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go straight to Up a Tree and 'take egg' (+5 recorded). Then return by the shortest known route, with no side trips.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on entering the Kitchen. Repeat it, then continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately and compare the score.
4. Keep the gear order: 'take lantern' before 'turn on lantern', and also take the sword. Both worked this run.
5. After 'push rug', next 'open trap door'. A prior run recorded +25 for rug, trap door, then 'down'; verify it again.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't skip 'inventory' before 'down'. This run took the lantern and sword but never listed them, so carried gear went unconfirmed.
2. Don't open with Forest west/east wandering. Those two steps scored nothing and delayed the egg.
3. Don't detour Forest Path south into North House while carrying treasure. It scored nothing and added steps before the deposit.
4. Don't end right after 'push rug'. The revealed trap door was left closed, so the underground was never reached and no +25 was recorded.
5. Never move in darkness or enter the Troll Room unarmed. An earlier run died moving south from a dark Cellar. Confirm light and weapon first.
