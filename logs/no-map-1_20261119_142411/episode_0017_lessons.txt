LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House: west, east, up, take egg (+5 recorded). Then take the shortest known path to Behind House without detours.
2. At Behind House, open window, then west; +10 was recorded on that move into Kitchen. Continue west to Living Room.
3. In Living Room, open case and put egg in case immediately; +5 was recorded. Do this before going underground.
4. In Living Room, take lantern, turn it on, and run inventory to confirm it is carried. Only then push rug, open trap, and go down (+25 recorded).
5. After any descent, read the text for darkness warnings. If it says pitch black, fix the light with inventory or look before any movement.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the text says pitch black. East from the Cellar caused grue death (-10) after 'put down leaflet' wasted the only dark turn.
2. Don't use 'turn on lantern' without first having taken the lantern. Verify it is in inventory before any descent.
3. Don't wander the house perimeter (North House, South House, Forest, Clearing). Steps 5-21 cost about 17 moves when the window route was known.
4. Don't oscillate between two rooms (West House/South House repeated three times) or put the egg in the mailbox and take it back out.
5. Don't take blind moves from a dark room. Dying at 35/80 forfeited all further scoring.
