Historian episode review — episode 32
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest play was the opening route. It opened the window and went west, which recorded +10. Later it pushed the rug, opened the trap door and descended, which recorded +25. Both score events are confirmed. The route took only 18 moves, but about nine of them recorded no score: mailbox, leaflet, sack, Attic and bottle. It also never recorded taking the sword, so it went underground less prepared than the lessons advised. The worst decision was its first move in the dark Cellar. After the trap door shut, it moved south, and a grue killed it for -10. The log does not show whether the lantern was carried or lit at that point, so the cause is partly unverified. Next episode: skip the mailbox, sack and Attic, go straight to the Kitchen and Living Room, and equip the sword and lantern. Check that the light is on before any move underground.
