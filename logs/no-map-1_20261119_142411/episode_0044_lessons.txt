LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Up a Tree, 'take egg' recorded +5. Take it first, then head home without side detours.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 entering the Kitchen. Repeat directly, then go west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately.
4. Before 'down', take the sword, turn on the lantern, push the rug and open the trap door (+25 recorded).
5. On arriving underground, check inventory and 'look' before any exit; confirm the lantern is lit and the sword is held.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in darkness: 'south' from the pitch-black Cellar ended in grue death and -10. Stop and diagnose the light first.
2. Don't descend without an inventory check; this run never verified what was carried or that the lantern was lit.
3. Don't treat 'pitch black' text after a lantern turn-on as safe; fix the light problem before choosing any exit.
4. Don't waste early steps on Forest west/east, the North House detour, the mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle or Attic.
5. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed or shuffle between Cellar and Troll Room; arm up and verify light first.
