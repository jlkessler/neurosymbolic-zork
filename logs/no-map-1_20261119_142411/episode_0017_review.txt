Historian episode review — episode 17
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest decisions were the recorded score routes: take egg in the tree (+5), open window then west into the Kitchen (+10), put egg in case (+5), and down the trap door (+25). Its Living Room sequence of open case, deposit, push rug, open trap was efficient. Steps 5-21 were wasted. It wandered between North House, South House, Forest and Clearing, looped West House/South House three times, and shuffled the egg in and out of the mailbox for no score. The fatal error was descending without ever taking the lantern. 'turn on lantern' appears, but no 'take lantern' or inventory check does. The Cellar was pitch black, and east from the Cellar was a blind move that got it eaten (-10). It ended at 35/80. The goal framing pushed it to hunt for a route home, but every move was chosen by the Actor. Next time: take lantern, check inventory, then descend, and never move in the dark.
