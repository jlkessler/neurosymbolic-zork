LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Bank treasure first: after taking the egg, return home and 'put egg in case' (+5 confirmed). Verify the score afterward.
2. The route Behind House window → west into Kitchen gave a confirmed +10. Reuse it as the known way home.
3. Take the egg from Up a Tree (+5 confirmed). It is reachable via Forest Path, then 'up'.
4. Moving down from the Living Room into the Cellar gave a confirmed +25. Repeat it only after verifying a carried, lit light.
5. Before every descent, run 'inventory' and 'look' to confirm the lantern is carried and lit. Check again after arrival.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. Moving east in the dark Cellar caused a grue death and -10 and ended the episode.
2. Turning the lantern on is not proof of light. Do not assume it is carried; take it explicitly and recheck inventory.
3. Avoid zero-score fiddling such as taking and re-putting the leaflet. It wasted three moves with no score or information.
4. Do not wander Forest rooms without a target ('go around trees', 'west'). These moves produced no score and no new exits.
5. After an underground score, return only by verified exits. Record exits per room, including the untried Kitchen up and down.
