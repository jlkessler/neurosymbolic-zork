LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House, west, east, up reached Up a Tree; 'take egg' recorded +5. Repeat it directly, then head for Behind House.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on entering the Kitchen. Repeat it, then continue west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Deposit treasure immediately and check the score.
4. Take the lantern, turn it on and take the sword in the Living Room before descending. This ordering worked.
5. After 'push rug', do 'open trap' then 'down'; +25 was recorded on 'down'. Verify the score again.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never skip 'inventory' before 'down'. Gear was again never listed, so lantern and sword possession stayed unconfirmed.
2. Don't attack the troll blindly. One 'hit troll with sword' got the Actor knocked out and killed (-10). Check inventory and your options first.
3. Avoid score-less shuffling: Forest west/east, the North House detour, and Cellar south-then-north cost steps and recorded no points.
4. Don't ignore warning cues. The sword glowed blue in the Cellar, then north led to the Troll Room. Possible danger signal; pause and check.
5. Never move in darkness. An earlier run died moving south from a dark Cellar. Confirm the lantern is carried and lit first.
