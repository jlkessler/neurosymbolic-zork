Historian episode review — episode 6
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest decision was the direct run from Forest Path to 'up' to 'take egg' at Up a Tree. That action carries the only confirmed score event (+5, 0 to 5). It was also right to open the mailbox and then check that the egg was secured.

The opening was wasteful. Taking the leaflet and putting it back in the mailbox cost two moves for 0 score, and the leaflet was never read. The west/east shuffle between Forest and Forest Path gave no new exits. After the egg, 'down' then 'south' reached North House, and the episode ended with no_safe_actions while the egg was still undeposited. The Actor never tried the untried exits at Forest Path (north, and west if it connects) or at North House. Next time, read the leaflet instead of dropping it, then go straight to the tree. Once holding the egg, plan the route home before moving. The score claim in the goal text (+25) was inherited and unverified; only +5 is confirmed. If progress stalls, target the window at Behind House.
