LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House: west, east, up, take egg (+5 recorded). Then go to Behind House by the shortest known path.
2. At Behind House, open window, then west; +10 was recorded on that move into Kitchen. Continue west to Living Room.
3. In Living Room, open case and put egg in case immediately; +5 was recorded. Do this before going underground.
4. Push rug, open trap, down; +25 was recorded on that move. Confirm lantern is carried and lit first.
5. After arriving underground, read the text for darkness warnings, then use look or inventory to verify light before choosing any exit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move from the Cellar while the text says pitch black. East from the Cellar caused grue death (-10) this episode.
2. Don't trust an internal note like 'ensuring lantern is on' as proof of safety; verify with look or inventory before moving.
3. Avoid house-perimeter detours (North House, South House) when the window route is known. This episode's detour cost extra steps.
4. Don't waste turns on the mailbox or other idle actions. Keep every action tied to a known score route or a clear exploration goal.
5. Don't die with the target unmet: death cost -10 and ended the episode at 35 of 80. Avoid risky blind moves such as jumping at East Chasm.
