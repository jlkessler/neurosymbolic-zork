LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Reach the house early and circle its exterior for entrances; a prior run opened the window into the Kitchen for +10 (not reproduced this run).
2. Take the lantern, confirm it in inventory, light it, then go down; a prior run recorded +25 on that descent.
3. Carry the sword underground. A prior unarmed troll-room visit gave only damage and no progress.
4. When a area is exhausted, test an unvisited exit. South House was the only new room this run, reached by Forest northwest.
5. After taking treasure, check inventory and score, then find and verify a route back to the trophy case; none was confirmed so far.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never give treasure or gear to the troll or thief; 'give troll painting' recorded -10 and a knockout in a prior run.
2. Don't drop and re-take items like leaves or the painting as 'investigation'; this repeated many times with zero score effect.
3. Don't oscillate Clearing↔Forest Path↔Forest, Cellar↔East Chasm, Gallery↔Studio or Cellar↔Troll Room. After two repeats without score, pick an untried exit.
4. Don't wander the forest for hundreds of steps. After about 30 zero-score moves, go to the house and examine its exterior.
5. Don't use vague pseudo-directions like 'go around forest/trees' or re-enter the troll room unarmed. Use real compass exits and verify the new room name.
