LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen; +10 was recorded on that move again. Repeat it directly.
2. Push the rug, take and light the lantern, open the trap door, then go 'down'; +25 was recorded on the descent.
3. Try 'up' at Forest Path and 'take egg' for the egg route; verify any score change before moving on.
4. After the egg, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' in the Living Room; confirm any score change.
5. Before the Troll Room, check inventory for a weapon and lit lantern; use combat verbs, then move east only after the troll is dead.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't open the mailbox, take the leaflet and put it back; it cost three steps with no score or progress.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed. This episode ended in a -10 knockout after taking garlic there with no weapon.
3. Don't use non-combat actions near the troll: no taking items, 'give troll garlic', or 'north' while it blocks the way.
4. Don't oscillate between Cellar, East Chasm and Troll Room; this episode repeated that with no new information. Prepare, then commit once.
5. Don't spend steps on sack, garlic and lunch handling or Attic detours unless a clue needs them; none scored this episode.
