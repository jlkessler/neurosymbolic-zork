LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg early: 'west, east, up, take egg' gave a confirmed +5. Then head directly to the Behind House window.
2. Open the Behind House window and enter the Kitchen; this transition gave a confirmed +10. Reuse it for a fast route home.
3. Open the trophy case, then 'put egg in case'; this gave a confirmed +5 in two moves. Verify the score afterward.
4. Push the rug and open the trap door; descending to the Cellar gave a confirmed +25 on that transition.
5. Before descending, confirm with 'inventory' that the lantern is carried and lit. The Cellar was pitch black despite the lantern-on message.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room without a confirmed light source; moving east in the dark Cellar caused a grue death and -10.
2. Do not shuttle between North House and Behind House; moves 5-9 looped through them for 0 score once the route was known.
3. Avoid zero-score side actions like taking the sword before checking whether they matter; it cost a move with no score.
4. Do not take unexplored exits while carrying treasure; deposit the egg first. Banking score before risk protects gains.
5. Do not leave exits unlisted: Kitchen up and down stayed untried, so record untried exits per room and pick one.
