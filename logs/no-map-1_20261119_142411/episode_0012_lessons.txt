LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree early; +5 confirmed again this episode. Then head directly back toward the house.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen gave confirmed +10. Use this as the direct house-entry route.
3. Once in the Living Room with the case open, immediately 'put egg in case'. Deposit before any other action.
4. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending. A prior Cellar route via rug and trap door scored +25.
5. Underground, look after each move and pick one unexplored lit exit. Gallery 'take art' scored +4 earlier; carry it up to the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not wander North, West and South House while carrying the egg. Steps 6-12 scored nothing; use the known window route.
2. Skip low-value detours like the mailbox leaflet when the goal is to deposit treasure. Don't shuffle items at the mailbox.
3. Never 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge (death, -10). Never move in the dark; keep the lantern on.
4. Do not shuttle north/south between Cellar and East Chasm. Choose a new unexplored exit instead.
5. Do not end the episode with the egg undeposited. The trap door shuts behind you, so deposit first and plan the route.
