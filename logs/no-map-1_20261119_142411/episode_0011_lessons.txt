LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree first; +5 confirmed. Carry it by the window route to the Living Room and deposit it.
2. Behind House: open window, then west into the Kitchen gave a confirmed +10. Reuse it as the house entry route.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' gave a confirmed +5. Bank carried valuables there promptly.
4. After banking, take the lantern, turn it on, push rug, open trap door, then go down lit. Earlier episodes scored +25 for this route.
5. Underground, after each score run look and choose one unexplored lit exit. A Gallery 'take art' scored +4 earlier; carry it up to the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not loop Forest, Clearing and Forest Path. About 23 steps there scored nothing; head straight for known score routes.
2. Avoid take/drop cycles with leaves and invalid commands like 'go around forest' or 'go around trees'. Use real exits only.
3. Never 'jump' at a chasm or edge; it caused death and -10. Never move in the dark; keep the lantern on.
4. Do not shuttle north/south between Cellar and East Chasm, or shuffle items at the mailbox. Pick a new exit instead.
5. Do not stop after revealing the trap door. Open it and descend; the door shuts behind you, so plan the exploration route first.
