LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take visible treasure early (Up a Tree egg +5 here; Gallery art +4 in an earlier run), then head straight for the house instead of lingering in the forest.
2. Verified house route: Forest Path south to North House, east to Behind House, open window, west into Kitchen (+10 recorded). Earlier route: Forest northwest to South House.
3. Take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, go down (+25 recorded). Try lighting the lantern before descending, since this run arrived in the dark (untested).
4. Deposit treasure with an explicit 'put egg in case' after opening the case, then check inventory and score. 'Put down' only drops the item.
5. Find and carry a weapon before the Troll Room; unarmed visits have only produced a knockout and -10. Verify inventory before heading north.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed: the last move this run recorded -10 and a knockout, and the Actor was thrown back to the Forest.
2. Don't oscillate Cellar↔East Chasm or Cellar↔Troll Room. After two zero-score repeats, try an untried exit or a different goal, such as depositing the egg.
3. Don't give items to the troll or thief ('give troll painting' recorded -10), and don't drop and re-take items as 'investigation'.
4. Don't drop treasure in the Living Room and then pursue unrelated exploration. The egg was left on the floor with no deposit or verified return.
5. Don't loop through Forest, Forest Path and Clearing (an earlier run burned 500 steps at 0 score). After about 30 zero-score moves, return to the house.
