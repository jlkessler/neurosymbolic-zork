LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House, go Forest Path then up and take the egg (+5 confirmed again). Skip the Forest west/east detour that wasted two steps.
2. At Behind House, open window then go west into the Kitchen; the +10 was recorded on that west move. Then go west to the Living Room.
3. In the Living Room, open the case and put the egg in at once. This episode recorded +5 for it, so check the score afterward.
4. After the rug move reveals the trap door, open it and go down with the lantern lit. An earlier route scored +25 on 'down'.
5. Underground, use look after each move and pick one unexplored lit exit. If you find the Gallery art (+4 earlier), carry it up to the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't stop after 'push rug'. The trap door stays unopened and the episode ends at 20 points; the next action must be open trap door, then down.
2. Never jump at East Chasm (death, -10) or move in the dark. Never shuttle north/south between Cellar and East Chasm.
3. Don't use the mailbox as storage for the egg, canary or leaflet. Every earlier shuffle scored 0 and wasted steps.
4. Avoid odd commands on the egg ('take on egg', 'close', 'shake', 'put canary in egg'). It ended up broken after 'take on egg'; cause unconfirmed.
5. Don't pick up unscored items like the sword without a reason. Skip aimless wandering and loops such as West House/South House shuttling; check score and inventory instead.
