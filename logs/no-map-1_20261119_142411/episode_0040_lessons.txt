LESSONS LEARNED
================================

2. From Forest Path go 'up' and 'take egg'; +5 recorded on the take action again. Carry it straight home.
3. In the Living Room, right after 'open case', run 'put egg in case', then check score and inventory. The deposit is still unverified.
4. Return route that worked: Forest Path south, then east to Behind House, open window, west twice to the Living Room.
5. Before descending: take sword and lantern, push rug, open trap door, go down (+25 earlier). Attack troll with combat commands, then east (+5).

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't waste early steps on the mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle or Attic; none produced score or progress.
2. Don't enter the Troll Room without the sword, and don't take garlic or give the troll garlic there (knocked out, -10).
3. Don't say 'north' while the troll blocks the way, and don't shuffle between Cellar and East Chasm. Prepare once, then commit.
4. Don't let unrelated actions like 'turn on lantern' interrupt a deposit; the egg was left out of the case after 'open case'.
5. Don't end the run at about 12 steps with a treasure undeposited and no safe actions; confirm inventory and score before the options run out.
