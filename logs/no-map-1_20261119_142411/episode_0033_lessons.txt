LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and go west into the Kitchen; this recorded +10 again. Then go straight to the Living Room.
2. Take the sword, move the rug, open the trap door, and descend; the +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Run inventory before 'down'. Confirm the lantern is in hand, not just switched on in the room, and the sword is carried.
4. Deposit treasure in the trophy case once back in the Living Room; 'put egg in case' recorded +5.
5. Examine and take items in new rooms, and try special-room commands: Gallery art +4, Loud Room bar +10, troll kill with sword then east +5.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never descend without taking the lantern. Turning it on in the room is not enough; the dark Cellar led to grue death (-10).
2. Never move in a dark room. The north move in the unlit Cellar ended in death; check light and inventory first.
3. Avoid early mailbox, leaflet, sack, bottle and Attic detours, and don't circle outside after finding treasure; go directly to the known window route.
4. Don't shuttle between Cellar and East Chasm or repeat 'touch mirror'; neither produced score or progress.
5. Don't attack at Entrance Hades; attacking the pile recorded death. Inspect unexplained objects before hitting them.
