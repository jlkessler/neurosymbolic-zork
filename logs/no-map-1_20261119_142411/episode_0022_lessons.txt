LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and go west into the Kitchen early; +10 is recorded. Then bank any surface items before descending, since the trap door shuts behind you.
2. Before the trap door: take lantern, confirm it in inventory, turn it on, then go down. Down recorded +25 on that move.
3. After each take, compare the score. 'take art' gave +4 once; later 'Taken' repeats gave 0, so verify the delta before repeating.
4. When a room's exits are exhausted, try an unused exit listed in its text or examine an object. Prefer new rooms over reversing.
5. Before carrying treasure deeper, plan a return route to the trophy case. Reaching the Living Room unverified left the +4 item undeposited.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black or descend without a lit lantern that inventory confirms. 'Turn on lantern' in the Living Room does not mean it is carried; grue death is fatal.
2. Don't cycle Cellar↔East Chasm or Gallery↔Studio. Dozens of repeats gave 0 score; after two repeats pick an untested action.
3. Don't repeatedly put and take the art or painting in the sack. Roughly 20 cycles produced no score change.
4. Don't descend with surface scoring unfinished. Here 39 points stayed in transit with no verified return home.
5. Don't re-enter hostile rooms unarmed, and don't pick up items like the manual or garlic without a stated purpose.
