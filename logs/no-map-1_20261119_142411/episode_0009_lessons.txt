LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen gave a confirmed +10. Reuse it as the entry route to the house.
2. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then down gave a confirmed +25. Repeat only after inventory proves a carried, lit lantern.
3. Before any descent, run 'inventory' and 'look', explicitly 'take lantern', turn it on, and recheck light on arrival.
4. Earlier episodes confirmed the egg from Up a Tree (Forest Path, then up) and 'put egg in case' for +5 each. Do these before descending.
5. After a score, run 'look' and list the exits. Choose one concrete next target and make only lit, verified moves toward it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while an observation says pitch black. North from the dark Cellar caused a grue death and -10. The dark Attic descent was also lethal-risk.
2. 'Lantern is now on' is not proof of light. The lantern was never taken, and the Cellar was still pitch black.
3. Do not loop open/close/put/take on the sack, garlic or lunch. About ten dark Cellar moves produced zero score.
4. Avoid zero-score detours such as the mailbox, the sack, garlic and bottle pickups, and the Attic trip, before banking known score routes.
5. Do not drop or spill items in darkness or assume an exit back up. The trap door barred shut behind the descent.
