LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: +10 recorded again. Do it directly rather than looping the forest first.
2. Open the case and use 'put egg in case' once: +5 recorded. Bank treasure immediately without dropping or re-taking it.
3. Rug, trap door, down gave +25 recorded. Repeat early, but only after the sword and lantern are in inventory.
4. Take the sword before any troll risk (kept in inventory this run). Also take the lantern, and light it before moving in dark rooms.
5. If a goal hint names an unreachable place (stale Loud Room 'take bar'), drop it and pursue a concrete local prerequisite.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in the dark Cellar without a lit lantern: a grue ate the Actor on the first move (−10) and ended the episode.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; the sword must be in inventory before the troll. A previous run died there.
3. Do not oscillate: forest loops took ~9 steps, Kitchen↔Attic ran twice and Kitchen↔Living Room three times, all for zero points.
4. Do not churn items: drop egg, take sword, open case, then re-take egg wasted turns. Hold the egg and deposit it directly.
5. Do not let the stale Loud Room 'take bar' goal override prerequisites like light and weapon. Never jump or take treasures out of the case.
