LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take lantern and sword in the Living Room before descending. Carrying both let the Actor hit and kill the troll while armed.
2. Open with window entry (+10) and trap door down (+25). The mailbox and North House detour from past runs is gone; keep it that way.
3. Bank a treasure in the trophy case soon after taking it. The painting deposit recorded +6; the bar and coins never got banked.
4. After every take, compare the score delta with the claimed hint value. Bar and coins each recorded +10, not the hinted +50.
5. When a return route is blocked, list the unexplored exits and test them one by one. Do not repeat known loops.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not take skeletons or cursed remains in the maze. A ghost cursed the Actor, and a knife taken soon after preceded the −10 death.
2. Do not shuttle between two rooms three or more times without new items or information. Studio/Gallery and Cellar/East Chasm burned many zero-score steps.
3. Do not carry unbanked treasure into the unmapped maze. The bar and coins were still unbanked when the episode ended.
4. Do not give the troll your weapon (sword or axe). It wastes turns and risks being disarmed; hit it only while armed.
5. Do not attack objects or use unknown weapons on yourself or odd targets. Treat 'hit X with knife' near cursed items as a death risk.
