3. Gallery 'take art' scored +4 (reached via Cellar south, East Chasm east). Studio is north of the Gallery, and the manual there is unread.

4. Don't drop and retake the same item without a reason. 'put down art' then 'take art' cost two steps and scored nothing.
5. Don't mark the painting 'DONE' or banked: it was never deposited anywhere. Check inventory before declaring any goal finished.
