LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Opening sequence works: Behind House window west gave +10, and Living Room trap door down gave +25. Repeat it quickly.
2. Gallery "take art" recorded +4. Check unexplored rooms for takeable items and verify the score delta after each take.
3. Prioritize unvisited exits from Gallery, Studio and East Chasm over re-walking known room pairs.
4. Treat "lamp is nearly out" as a deadline: choose one concrete objective immediately, instead of wandering in the dark.
5. After one unscored put/take pair, check inventory, then change location or plan; do not retry identical item handling.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't loop put art in sack/take art; about ten repeats produced no score and no progress.
2. Don't open and close the sack as exploration; every cycle changed nothing and spent turns.
3. Don't oscillate Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio; about 280 steps after +4 yielded zero further score.
4. Don't keep reviving 'take bar' or 'return home' goals after they fail repeatedly; drop them and replan from the current room.
5. Don't keep moving when the lamp is nearly out; the grue death cost -10 and ended the episode.
