LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and go down (+25). Both gains were confirmed again.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on on arrival in the dark Cellar. No grue incident occurred.
3. Taking the Gallery art gave +4. Once back in the Living Room, immediately open the trophy case and put the art inside, then check the score.
4. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits and take one unvisited exit instead of re-entering a known room.
5. Drop a goal whose target room was never reached after two failed approaches. Judge progress only by recorded score deltas, not '+1100' or '+60' goal text.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never type 'jump' at the East Chasm or any ledge. It caused death and -10 at step 147, and nothing scored.
2. Do not alternate Cellar/East Chasm or Gallery/Studio more than twice without a new item, clue or exit. About 90 steps scored zero.
3. Stop chasing 'take bar' at the Loud Room. It was pursued for over 100 steps and the room was never reached.
4. Do not wander with an unbanked treasure. The art was carried through the Attic, Kitchen and Cellar without being put in the case.
5. Skip low-value pickups (leaflet, sack contents, manual, coil) and repeated pick-up/drop of the manual. They cost steps and scored nothing.
