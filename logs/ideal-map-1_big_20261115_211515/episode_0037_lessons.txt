LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the kitchen (+10), then go down the trap door (+25); this opening was confirmed again and costs few steps.
2. Pass the Troll Room carrying the lantern and a weapon; leaving east recorded +5. Retrace known routes, then stop.
3. Take treasure on sight (bar +10 at Loud Room), then head straight to the Living Room and 'put <item> in case'. Verify the score afterward.
4. When the room goes pitch black, 'turn on' the lantern and read the reply; light restored sight in the maze. Keep it carried and lit.
5. Probe unfamiliar objects with benign verbs (examine, take, look) first. Check the reply and score delta before escalating.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw, smash or drop the lantern; 'throw light at skeleton' broke it, and the next move in the dark meant a grue death (-10).
2. Do not act on an aggregate goal like 'up' (+15) without a fresh score gain; about 10 retries scored 0.
3. After two repeats of a movement pair (167↔64, 167↔65, 167↔68), stop. Take an unvisited exit or leave the maze entirely.
4. Do not use force verbs ('break', attack) on unfamiliar scenery such as the Hades pile; 'break pile with axe' was fatal in an earlier run (-10).
5. Do not wander carrying an unbanked bar, or use 'put down' to shed gear; it only dropped items, with no score change. Bank first.
