LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take and light the lantern, and take the sword, before descending. This was done at 0 cost and preceded the +25 from the trap-door 'down'.
2. Open window then west into the Kitchen scored +10, and 'push rug', 'open trap', 'down' scored +25. These are done; do not repeat them.
3. Attack the troll only while carrying the sword; the observation confirmed the kill. Check what you carry before any fight.
4. Open the trophy case, then 'put painting in case': this scored +6 in the Living Room. Deposit treasure promptly after returning home.
5. Credit score only to the action immediately before a recorded change, and note unexplained changes as unconfirmed. Check the observation text first.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio. About 17 steps passed with no score or new item after the Gallery +4. Choose an untried exit instead.
2. Do not flip northwest/southeast between the same maze rooms. Three repeats of one move pair gained nothing; map each room's exits and change direction.
3. Do not take the skeleton or other remains. A ghost appeared and cursed the Actor, and the effect on inventory is unclear.
4. Do not re-enter the Treasure Room carelessly: the move scored +25 but the next move scored -10 and ended the episode. Check state and exits before moving.
5. Never 'give' treasure to the troll, and avoid repeating zero-score actions like a second 'open trap' then 'down'. Verify new score before retrying.
