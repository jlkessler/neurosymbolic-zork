LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10), take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, then go 'down' (+25). Repeat this proven opening.
2. Take the Living Room sword and confirm it in inventory before any Troll Room entry; the sword fight did knock the troll out.
3. When symbolic navigation heads home with art, follow it to the Living Room and put the art in the trophy case. Verify the score change.
4. After the troll is knocked out, use the opened east exit and explore beyond it; the move east recorded +5.
5. Read combat messages ('recovering', 'dreamland', 'unarmed'). Retreat south when the fight state is unclear instead of trading blows.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not return to the Troll Room and attack the troll after knocking it out; that final attack killed the Actor (−10).
2. Do not drop treasure to 'secure' it. The painting left in the Studio was never banked; carry it home.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen/Attic/Living Room or Cellar/Troll/Chasm. About 20 steps scored nothing; stale 'up' score hints from other rooms do not apply.
4. Do not write that you are armed without checking inventory. The first two Troll Room entries were unarmed because the sword was never taken.
5. Never go 'up' from the Cyclops Room; an earlier run flagged it deadly (not observed this episode). Don't chase generic 'up' bonuses.
