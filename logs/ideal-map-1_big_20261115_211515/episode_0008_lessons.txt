LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Credit only the action just before a score event: west into Kitchen +10 (window opened first), down +25. Gallery 'take art' +4 comes from an earlier episode.
2. On arriving anywhere dark, check inventory and light state first and stay put until a lit lantern is confirmed.
3. Keep carrying the sword; it glowed faintly blue on arrival underground, so treat the glow as a possible danger cue and plan a retreat.
4. After the one-way trap door, plan only from the underground map. Cellar north (Troll Room) is untried: go armed, lit, with a retreat south.
5. When exits stall, interact with room features (rug, trap door, window) and check the score after each action, as this episode did.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black: 'north' from the dark Cellar ended in a grue death (−10), even though the risk had been noted.
2. Stop 2-room shuttles after two repeats; Living Room↔Kitchen and Kitchen↔Attic burned about twenty moves for no score.
3. Do not re-chase replayed 'previously productive' goals or stale ones (Tree/egg, trap door up); verify prerequisites, then discard unreachable goals.
4. Do not repeat take/open on the sack or bottle without a new clue; those actions scored nothing.
5. Don't assume the lantern is lit because it was turned on earlier; confirm it is carried and on. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed or give items to the troll.
