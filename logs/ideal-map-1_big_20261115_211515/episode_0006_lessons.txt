LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Light the lantern before typing 'down' through the trap door; this episode it was lit only after arrival, so verify inventory and light first.
2. Credit only the action immediately before a score gain: 'west' into the Kitchen +10, 'down' +25, 'take art' +4.
3. After a one-way descent, plan from the current underground map and stop pursuing goals that need the Living Room.
4. Take portable valuables in new rooms and check the score afterward; keep them rather than dropping them in danger.
5. Probe unexplored exits from Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio one move at a time, using the room text for options.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter the Troll Room north of the Cellar unarmed; it is lethal. Veto symbolic-navigation edges into it.
2. Never 'jump' at the East Chasm; it is fatal. Also never move in pitch black, since a grue killed an earlier episode.
3. Do not treat the trap door as a return route: it barred behind the Actor, so 'navigate home' goals are stale.
4. Do not drop treasure or give items to the troll while unarmed; 'put down painting' and 'give troll leaflet' preceded death.
5. Break loops: after three visits to a room with no score change, or any 6-move cycle, change area or verb.
