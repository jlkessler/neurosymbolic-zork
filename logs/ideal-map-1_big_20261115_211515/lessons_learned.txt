LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, then go west into the Kitchen: +10 confirmed. Do this first instead of wandering North House/West House.
2. Move the rug, open the trap door, then go down for the confirmed +25. Do it in the Living Room.
3. Before going down into the dark Cellar, carry a light source and have it ready to turn on immediately.
4. If an observation says pitch black, make light your next action before any movement.
5. Check the live observation against each goal. Override a symbolic goal when the room is dark or the target is far away.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black: 'south' from the dark Cellar met a grue, causing death and -10 with no light.
2. Do not pursue a stale Loud Room 'take bar' goal from the Cellar. It was unreachable then and led straight to death.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen/Living Room or North/West House without a purpose. About six opening moves scored nothing.
4. Do not open containers like the sack unless you will take or use the contents; it cost a step and scored nothing.
5. Never repeat the Troll Room entry or drop treasure away from the trophy case; earlier episodes showed both cost points and steps.
