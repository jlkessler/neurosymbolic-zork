LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Credit only the action just before a score gain: west into Kitchen +10, down +25, take art +4 in Gallery.
2. When text says the lamp is dimmer, stop exploring and fix light first; stay in known lit rooms until then.
3. After the one-way trap door descent, plan only from the underground map; drop Living Room, trophy case, Tree and egg goals.
4. Take portable items in newly reached rooms and check the score afterward; the Gallery take gave +4.
5. Spend moves on untried exits one at a time instead of revisiting rooms already searched without score change.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black: 'west' from a dark East Chasm ended in a grue death (-10). Light first or stay put.
2. Never enter the Troll Room north of the Cellar without a weapon confirmed in hand; avoid repeated Cellar<->Troll moves and giving items to the troll.
3. Stop 2-room oscillations after two repeats; about 300 moves among Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio scored nothing.
4. Do not follow reissued 'take egg' or Tree goals while unreachable; discard stale goals instead of probing around them.
5. Do not repeat open/close/put-in-sack actions on the bottle or sack without a new clue; they produced no score and wasted turns.
