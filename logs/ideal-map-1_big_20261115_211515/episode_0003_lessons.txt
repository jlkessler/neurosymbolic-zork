LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Try verbs a room's text suggests (open window, push rug, open trap door); the moves that followed gave +10 and +25.
2. Light the lantern immediately on arriving in darkness; the Cellar became usable and was explored safely.
3. Take portable items in new rooms and check whether the score moves; 'take art' at the Gallery recorded +4.
4. Open containers and note contents before leaving; the sack held lunch and garlic. Decide deliberately whether either helps before dangerous rooms.
5. Before a descent that may close behind you, check inventory and note the one-way risk. The trap door barred behind the Actor.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter, wait in, or return to the Troll Room unarmed; it hit twice, and the second hit knocked the Actor out for -10.
2. Don't let home-navigation route through a known lethal room; veto that edge and try other exits (East Chasm, Gallery) first.
3. Don't wander forest and Clearing rooms with no objective; the first seven steps scored nothing until the window led to the Kitchen.
4. Don't carry treasure deeper without a verified route home; the art stayed unbanked and the return path was blocked.
5. Drop stale goals like the tree egg once the Actor is underground or the route is blocked; the plan kept chasing it.
