LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the opening: open window, Kitchen (+10), take sword, push rug, open trap door, 'down' (+25). Do it without detours.
2. Before 'down', take a light source and confirm it in inventory; the trap door barred behind the Actor, leaving the Cellar pitch black.
3. Take the sword and check inventory before any Troll Room entry; earlier the sword fight knocked the troll out. Read combat messages before acting.
4. After the troll is knocked out, use the opened east exit; the move east recorded +5. Explore beyond rather than re-engaging.
5. Carry art to the Living Room and put it in the trophy case; verify the score change.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without light. 'Pitch black… eaten by a grue' preceded the south move that killed the Actor (−10).
2. Do not return to the Troll Room and attack the troll after knocking it out; that attack was fatal last run.
3. Do not drop treasure to 'secure' it; the painting left in the Studio was never banked.
4. Skip mailbox, leaflet, sack, lunch, garlic, and Kitchen/Attic shuttling. About half of this episode's steps scored nothing.
5. Never go 'up' from the Cyclops Room (flagged deadly earlier). Ignore stale 'up' score-goal hints from other rooms.
