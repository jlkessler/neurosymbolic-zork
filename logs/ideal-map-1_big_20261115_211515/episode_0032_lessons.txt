LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, go west (+10), push the rug, open the trap door, go down (+25). Confirmed again, reliable and fast.
2. Take the lantern and sword, and turn the lantern on before going down. No dark deaths occurred this run.
3. Keep attacking the troll with the sword until it dies; then go east, where +5 was recorded.
4. Read room text after each action for newly shown items. Try 'take' once on anything portable and valuable-looking, such as the Loud Room platinum bar.
5. After taking a treasure (trident +4), walk the known route to the trophy case and bank it before further exploring.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not attack the bag-carrying thief with the sword. 'hit thief' ended the run (-10); leave the room instead.
2. Do not wander Forest and Clearing at the start. Roughly 12 steps passed before the window with no score.
3. Do not repeat up/down or north/south pairs three or more times (Loud Room/Deep Canyon, Atlant/Cave). Pick an untried exit.
4. Do not keep a 'take bar' goal alive without attempting it. Try it once, read the response, then drop or act on the goal.
5. Do not drop and re-take treasure (trident), or grab random items like the pump or 'hand'. They scored nothing and delayed banking.
