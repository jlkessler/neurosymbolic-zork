LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go Behind House, open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10 confirmed), then take the lantern and light it before descending.
2. Take the sword together with the lantern in the Living Room, before the first descent; it was only taken at step 31 this run.
3. When carrying the painting, go straight home and use 'put painting in case' (+6 confirmed after the +4 pickup); check score afterward.
4. The Studio→Kitchen→Living Room route worked after the trapdoor closed; use it rather than assuming a closed trapdoor means no way home.
5. Explore each new room once and act on what you read; the Gallery detour yielded the only underground treasure score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not start with 'west' into the forest; it cost 4 moves before the window was reached.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar↔Troll Room or Cellar↔East Chasm for stale 'up'/'down' hints; 11 moves gave zero score.
3. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed and walk out again; decide to prepare or to act, not to oscillate.
4. Do not 'put down' treasure anywhere except the trophy case; earlier runs lost progress dropping the painting.
5. Do not let action notes drift from the actual move; check the room and inventory after each step.
