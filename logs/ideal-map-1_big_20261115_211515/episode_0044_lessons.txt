LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and go down (+25). Both gains were confirmed again this run.
2. Take the Gallery art (+4), then return to the Living Room via Studio up and Kitchen west, and put it in the open trophy case (+6).
3. Light the lantern immediately on reaching the dark Cellar. No grue incident occurred in the runs where this was done.
4. Deposit a treasure in the case on arrival: open case, put item in case, confirm the score delta. Do not drop it and wander off.
5. Judge progress only by recorded score deltas. Ignore stale goal text such as '+1000' and re-verify any previously productive action before pursuing it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not interact with the troll while armed without a verified plan. The Troll Room ended this run with -10, and earlier runs ended there too.
2. Do not shuttle between Cellar and East Chasm, or between Gallery and East Chasm, more than twice without a new item or clue. Those loops scored zero.
3. Abandon a stale goal like 'take bar' after two failed attempts. About 30 steps were spent without ever reaching it or scoring.
4. Do not drop a treasure and leave, then return for it. Drop-and-retrieve cycles and Kitchen/Living Room loops wasted steps.
5. Skip the early North House detour and the mailbox. Both cost steps and recorded no score; go straight to the window.
