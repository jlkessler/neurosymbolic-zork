LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; +10 was recorded on that move again. Do it first, skipping detours.
2. Take lantern and sword, push the rug, open the trap door, go down; +25 was recorded on reaching the Cellar.
3. With the sword held, deal with the troll, then go east from the Troll Room; +5 was recorded on that east move.
4. After each score event, check inventory for treasures and carry them to the Living Room trophy case before exploring further.
5. When a room lists an object (like the Loud Room bar), try a plain 'take <object>' once and read the reply.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'jump' at the Dome Room or any high place: it caused death, -10, and ended the episode.
2. Do not loop Loud Room, Deep Canyon, Dam, Dam Lobby and Round Room: about 100 steps earned zero. Drop stale goals after two scoreless visits.
3. Do not repeatedly push Maintenance Room buttons; water rose to neck level with no score. Leave once nothing scores.
4. Do not shuttle between two rooms, like Dome Room and Engravings Cave, without trying an unexplored exit or new object.
5. Never move in darkness or give the lantern or sack to the thief; an earlier episode died that way.
