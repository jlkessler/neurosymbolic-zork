LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Try verbs the room text suggests: 'open window' then 'west' coincided with +10; 'push rug', 'open trap', 'down' with +25.
2. Before a one-way descent (the trap door barred behind the Actor), check inventory and confirm a light source is carried.
3. Light the lantern the moment you enter a dark room; the earlier Cellar and chasm exploration was safe only with light.
4. Take portable items in new rooms and check the score afterward; 'take art' at the Gallery recorded +4.
5. Open containers and note contents (sack: lunch, garlic), then decide deliberately whether anything is worth taking before danger.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never make any move in a pitch-black room without light; a grue killed the Actor one move after the Cellar's +25 score.
2. Never enter the Troll Room north of the Cellar while unarmed; it is lethal. Veto navigation edges into it.
3. Never type 'jump' at the East Chasm; it is fatal (-10) and ended an earlier episode with 29 points unbanked.
4. Drop stale or unreachable goals (tree egg, Gallery 'take art' while in the Cellar) and re-plan from the current room's observation.
5. After three revisits to a room with no score change, or any 6-move cycle, change area or verb; avoid aimless detours like the forest loop.
