LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Route to the Kitchen is North House→Behind House→open window→west; +10 was recorded on entry. Skip the mailbox detour.
2. Take the lantern and the sword together in the Living Room before descending; the sword was skipped again this run.
3. In a pitch-black arrival, turn on the lantern on the very next step; this run it avoided any grue death.
4. Compare score before and after each action and record which action produced each delta, instead of assuming a cause.
5. Treat a closed trapdoor as an unverified way home; look for an alternate route back before carrying treasure, and deposit treasure in the trophy case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle Cellar↔Troll Room: 8 consecutive moves gave zero score and ended with a -10 hit.
2. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed after already noting it blocks all exits; prepare first or choose another direction.
3. Do not follow stale 'up'/'down' score hints blindly; check the current room, inventory, and threats before repeating one.
4. Do not open with 'west' into the forest, and do not detour to the mailbox; both waste early moves before the window.
5. Do not let actor notes drift from reality (it claimed to carry the leaflet after only opening the mailbox) or drop treasure outside the case.
