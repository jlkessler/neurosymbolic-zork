LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the kitchen (+10), then go down the trap door (+25). Take treasures on sight (art +4 Gallery, bar +10 Loud Room) and check the score delta.
2. After taking any treasure, return to the Living Room at once and use 'put <item> in case'; check the score and inventory afterward.
3. Keep the lantern and a weapon when passing the Troll Room; leaving it east recorded +5. Use the persistent map to retrace known routes, then stop.
4. Try unfamiliar objects once with benign verbs (examine, take, look) and read the reply before escalating.
5. When a goal stalls, pick an unvisited exit or a new object after two repeats, but probe with low-risk commands first.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not use 'break', attack or similar force verbs on unfamiliar scenery like the pile at Entrance Hades; 'break pile with axe' was fatal (-10, death).
2. Do not use 'put down' to bank treasure; it only dropped the bar, twice, in the Studio with no score change.
3. Do not shuttle Studio, Gallery, East Chasm, Cellar and Troll Room, or Round, Narrow and Mirror Room; those loops scored nothing for over 200 steps.
4. Do not replay stale 'up' goals whose reward is already collected; revisit only if the observation shows a new item or exit.
5. Do not keep exploring while carrying an uncased treasure; bank it first, then explore, so a death does not forfeit its value.
