LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Before moving on, try the verbs a room's text suggests (open window, push rug, open trap door); these preceded the +10 and +25 gains.
2. Take portable items in new rooms and log them as candidate tools; the sack, lantern and art were all picked up without incident.
3. After a score gain, re-read the room text and try its listed exits and objects before leaving.
4. Before descending through any one-way-looking opening, note that it may close behind you, and look for another route back first.
5. Examine unexplored objects such as the attic rope and sack contents before leaving a room; these were seen but never used.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't shuttle among Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Troll room; about 50 scoreless steps there. After three scoreless revisits, leave and try a new verb or room.
2. Don't give items to or linger beside the troll; 'give troll garlic' ended the episode with a recorded -10 and a knockout.
3. Don't carry treasures far underground without a verified route home; the art and painting were never banked in the trophy case.
4. Don't keep re-adopting the tree egg goal if the Actor never acts on it; test it once or drop it.
5. Don't repeat a failed move to the same blocked place; the return-route goal was stuck and the Actor kept retrying south, north and east.
