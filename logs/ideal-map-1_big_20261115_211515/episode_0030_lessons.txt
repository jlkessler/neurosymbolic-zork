LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Opening confirmed again: open window at Behind House, west (+10); push rug, open trap door, down (+25). Repeat it quickly in about 12 steps.
2. Before going down the trap door, check inventory for a light source. The Cellar was pitch black on arrival, with a grue warning.
3. On a 'pitch black' warning, use only light-seeking or other non-moving actions. Do not step into unseen exits.
4. After each take or move, check the score delta. Earlier run: Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Check new rooms for takeable items.
5. Prefer unvisited exits over re-walking known room pairs. Probe one new exit at a time and note the result.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move in darkness. Going north from the pitch-black Cellar produced a grue death (-10) and ended the episode.
2. Don't spend turns on inventory housekeeping, such as dropping a leaflet, while stuck in the dark. It scores nothing and wastes the turns.
3. Don't follow a stale historical goal such as 'take bar' at Loud Room. Replan from the current room and visible exits.
4. Don't loop sack open/close or put/take art, or cycle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio. About 300 steps in the earlier run yielded no score.
5. Don't leave Kitchen items (sack, bottle) uninspected before descending. Check which carried items could help in the dark or underground.
