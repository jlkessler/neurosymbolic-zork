LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: +10 recorded again. Reliable early score, so do it directly instead of wandering the forest first.
2. Take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door and descend: +25 recorded. Repeat this sequence early and efficiently.
3. Turn on the lantern immediately on arriving in the dark Cellar; the lit state was confirmed this run. Check it before every dark room.
4. When a goal hint names an unreachable place, reassess immediately and pick a concrete reachable frontier instead of drifting through already-visited rooms.
5. Bank each treasure in the trophy case right after pickup, and credit only recorded score deltas. Earlier runs scored painting, bar and egg this way.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed: this run died there at step 23 (−10). Take a weapon first, and retreat if the troll is present.
2. Do not shuttle between two rooms: Cellar↔Troll Room repeated three times for zero points. Two zero-score visits means pick a new exit.
3. Do not issue odd verbs like 'take walls' when a hostile is present; the action also contradicted the Actor's own stated plan to go south.
4. Do not spend the opening steps looping Forest/Clearing/Behind House; the score came only after the window and kitchen.
5. Never take treasures out of the trophy case, jump at chasms, or churn window/put/take items; earlier runs showed these only cost points or lives.
