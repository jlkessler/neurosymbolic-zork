LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen for +10. This was confirmed again and costs few steps.
2. Push the rug, open the trap door and go down for +25. Confirmed again, but only after securing light first.
3. Pass the Troll Room with a light and weapon; leaving east recorded +5. Retrace that known route.
4. After taking any treasure, walk the reverse route to the Living Room, put it in the trophy case, and verify the score.
5. Check the score delta after every action. Repeat an interaction only after a fresh recorded gain, never on a stale goal.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black. Entering the dark Cellar and going south without a lit light caused a grue death (-10).
2. Do not descend through the trap door without a light source carried; the door shuts behind you and blocks retreat.
3. Do not enter or linger in the Maze. Over 250 steps looping rooms 64-70 and 167 gave no score; retrace the entry path at the first loop.
4. Ignore the stale 'up' +15 aggregate and re-proposed Loud Room goals when there is no fresh gain. Avoid early detours like mailbox and North House that score 0.
5. Do not wander with an unbanked treasure for over 20 steps, and avoid 'put down', 'look around' or a third repeat of any movement cycle.
