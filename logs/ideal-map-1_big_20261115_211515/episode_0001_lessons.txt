LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take portable items on first sight in new rooms; the egg pickup in the tree was confirmed +5.
2. After a score gain, re-read that room's text and try its listed exits and objects; entering the Kitchen scored +10.
3. Carry found items to the Living Room and try putting them in the case; 'put egg in case' gave +2.
4. Check observations for named objects and exits (table, sack, bottle, staircase, chimney, window) before leaving a room.
5. Keep a list of exits already tried per room and pick the untried ones first.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't repeat the up/down tree cycle after the tree is exhausted; it was run about 10 times with zero reward.
2. Don't loop between Forest Path, Clearing and Forest rooms; dozens of steps there produced no score or new rooms.
3. Avoid drop/take test actions with no clue behind them, such as the sword; they cost steps and gave nothing.
4. Don't keep exploring only the forest after the 17 points; the unexplored house exits (staircase, attic, passages) were left alone.
5. Don't spend steps re-entering Behind House and Clearing back and forth; revisits without a new plan waste the step budget.
