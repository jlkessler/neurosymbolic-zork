LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open window then go west; +10 was recorded on that move again. Do this first, skipping mailbox and North House.
2. In the Living Room take lantern and sword, push rug, open trap door, go down; +25 was recorded on reaching the Cellar.
3. Light the lantern immediately in the dark Cellar before moving; done again this episode with no darkness trouble.
4. Enter the Troll Room carrying the sword and attack; the troll died to it unharmed, and +5 was recorded on the next east move.
5. After any score event, check inventory and bank carried treasures in the Living Room trophy case before exploring deeper.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't spend early steps on mailbox, leaflet or North House detours when a scoring route is known; four scoreless steps were wasted.
2. At a target location, don't act elsewhere ('echo') while the named object is visible. Try 'take bar' once, read the reply, then change tactics.
3. Don't shuttle Cellar and Troll Room after the troll is dead. Move on through the east exit instead of re-entering.
4. Never 'jump' at the Dome Room or any high place; it caused a death and -10 in an earlier episode.
5. Don't loop Loud Room, Dam, Round Room and Engravings Cave. Drop goals after two failed tries, and don't write unsupported claims like 'cannot be taken'.
