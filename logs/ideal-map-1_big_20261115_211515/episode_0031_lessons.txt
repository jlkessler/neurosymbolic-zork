LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the confirmed opening: open window, west (+10), then open trap door and go down (+25). It was reliable and fast.
2. Pick up the lantern and sword before descending. Both were carried underground, and the sword was in hand when the troll fight ended.
3. Push through new exits beyond known loops. Going east from the Troll room recorded +5 and opened the passage, Loud Room and cave areas.
4. Try 'take' on valuable-looking items visible in the room text. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4; the Loud Room bar was never tried.
5. After carrying a treasure home, put it inside the open trophy case, then check the score delta. Dropping an item on the floor scored nothing.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle between the same room pair three or more times. Cellar/East Chasm and Gallery/Studio burned over 100 steps with no score.
2. Do not chase a remote 'take bar' goal while ignoring the room in front of you. It was re-issued about 20 times without a single attempt.
3. Do not put sack, bottle, lunch, manual or garlic through the grating. Those items were lost with no score and no observed purpose.
4. Do not drop and re-take items (art, leaves, egg) as 'inventory management'. It produced no score and wasted turns.
5. Do not repeat Kitchen/Attic/Behind House walks for new ideas. Choose an unvisited exit, or check the exits of the room you just entered.
