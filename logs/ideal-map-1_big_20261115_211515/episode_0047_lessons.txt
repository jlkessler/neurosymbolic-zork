LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and go down (+25). Both gains are confirmed in repeated runs.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending to the dark Cellar. No darkness incident occurred.
3. Take the Gallery art (+4), return to the Living Room, open the case and put the painting in (+6) immediately. Confirmed this episode.
4. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits and pick an unvisited one rather than re-entering a known room.
5. Abandon a goal whose target room was never reached after two failed approaches. Judge progress only by recorded score deltas, not by goal text.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not attack a hostile creature repeatedly or give it items. At the Troll Room, 'give troll axe' ended in death and -10.
2. Never type 'jump' at the East Chasm or any ledge. A previous episode died that way (-10) with nothing scored.
3. Do not chase the 'take bar' Loud Room goal again. It stayed active for 40 or more steps, the room was never reached, and nothing scored.
4. Do not shuttle between two rooms (Kitchen/Attic, Cellar/Troll Room, Cellar/East Chasm) more than twice without a new item, clue or exit.
5. Skip low-value pickups (coil, knife, sack contents, leaflet, manual) unless they have a stated purpose. Such pickups scored nothing and cost steps.
