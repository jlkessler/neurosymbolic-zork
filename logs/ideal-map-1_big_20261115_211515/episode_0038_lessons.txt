LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the kitchen (+10), then go down the trap door (+25). Confirmed again; it is cheap and early.
2. Pass the Troll Room with lantern and weapon; leaving east recorded +5. Retrace that known route, then stop.
3. After taking a treasure (bar +10 at Loud Room), walk the reverse route straight to the Living Room, put it in the case, and verify the score.
4. When the room goes pitch black, 'turn on' the lantern and read the reply. Keep it carried and lit.
5. Probe unfamiliar objects with benign verbs first, and check the score delta after every action. Repeat an interaction only after a fresh gain.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw, drop or 'put down' the lantern or other light. Losing light led to a grue death (-10) in the dark.
2. Ignore the stale 'up' +15 aggregate. About 15 goal re-proposals produced 0 gain; require a fresh score gain before repeating any action.
3. Do not enter or linger in the Maze. About 200 steps looping rooms 167/68/64/65/67 gave no score; leave when the first loop appears.
4. After 2 repeats of a movement pair, stop and take an unvisited exit or leave the area. Do not run a third cycle.
5. Do not wander with an unbanked treasure (the bar was never banked), and do not shed gear with 'put down'. Bank first. Avoid destructive verbs at Hades.
