Historian episode review — episode 5
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest play was reading the room text. 'open window' then 'west' coincided with +10 on entering the Kitchen. 'push rug', 'open trap' and 'down' coincided with +25 on reaching the Cellar. The score credit belongs to the final move in each sequence; the preparatory verbs only enabled it. It opened the sack, but took nothing from it. It also reached the trap door quickly.

The main failure was dying at the Cellar. The Actor arrived in pitch darkness, carrying only a leaflet and no light source, and moved south at once; a grue killed it (-10 shown on that final step). The trap door barred behind it, so retreat was impossible. Steps 3-6 were a four-move forest detour with no score. The Gallery 'take art' goal was unreachable from the Cellar, and the Actor still followed it. No treasure was carried, so nothing was lost beyond the unbanked 25 points.

Next time, obtain a light source before descending and do not move in darkness.
