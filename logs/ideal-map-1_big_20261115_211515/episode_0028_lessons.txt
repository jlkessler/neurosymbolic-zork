LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From the start, go to Behind House, open the window, go west; +10 was recorded on that move. Skip mailbox and forest detours.
2. In the Living Room, push rug, open trap door, go down; +25 was recorded on reaching the Cellar.
3. Turn on the lantern at once in the dark Cellar; it worked with no grue trouble.
4. Check inventory for a weapon before going north from the Cellar; this run entered the Troll Room unarmed and ended there.
5. When damage text appears ('axe gets you'), react immediately: leave or fight with a weapon, rather than continuing pickups.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't keep the stale 'take bar' Loud Room goal; it persisted all 25 steps without being attempted. Re-plan from the current observation.
2. Don't spend early steps on mailbox leaflet, forest loops, sack or garlic; the first score came only at step 8.
3. Don't oscillate Cellar/Troll Room; five norths and four souths produced zero score and ended in a knockout.
4. Don't take or fiddle with items in the Troll Room while the troll is hostile; 'take lunch' drew an axe hit.
5. Don't re-enter a room that just damaged you without changing something (weapon, plan); the next entry coincided with -10 and the episode ending.
