LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Try verbs a room's text suggests (open window, push rug, open trap door); these preceded the +10 and +25 score events.
2. Light the lantern immediately on arriving in darkness; the Cellar and chasm rooms were then explored safely.
3. Take portable items in new rooms and check the score; 'take art' at the Gallery recorded +4.
4. Open containers and note contents before leaving; the sack held lunch and garlic. Decide deliberately whether either helps before dangerous rooms.
5. Before a descent that may close behind you, check inventory and note the one-way risk; the trap door barred behind the Actor.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter or wait in the Troll Room north of the Cellar while unarmed; veto symbolic-navigation edges into it and use other exits.
2. Never type 'jump' at chasms or ledges; it killed the Actor (-10) and ended the episode while 29 points were unbanked.
3. After three revisits to a room pair with no score change (Gallery↔Studio, Cellar↔East Chasm), change verb or area instead of pacing.
4. Don't drop carried treasure (art, painting) mid-route; carry it while searching for a verified route back to bank it.
5. Drop stale goals like the tree egg once underground or the route is blocked; the plan kept re-proposing it for 50 steps.
