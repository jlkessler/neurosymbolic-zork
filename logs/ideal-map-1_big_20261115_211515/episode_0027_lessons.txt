LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window then go west; +10 was recorded on that move again. Do it first, before any forest wandering.
2. In the Living Room take lantern and sword, push rug, open trap door, go down; +25 was recorded on reaching the Cellar.
3. Light the lantern immediately in the dark Cellar; it worked again with no darkness trouble.
4. Before the Troll Room, confirm the sword is in inventory; an earlier episode killed the troll with it, and +5 followed on the next east move.
5. After any score event, check inventory and bank any confirmed treasure in the Living Room trophy case before exploring deeper.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't keep a stale goal like 'take bar' (Loud Room) while elsewhere; it was never attempted in 25 steps. Re-plan from the current observation.
2. Don't spend early steps on forest wandering, leaves, sack, bottle or Attic; seven steps passed before the first score, plus several more on pickups.
3. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed or oscillate Cellar/Troll Room; five back-and-forth moves gained nothing and ended the episode.
4. Don't 'give' junk items to the troll; 'give troll leaves' coincided with -10 and a knockout. Use the sword instead.
5. Never 'jump' at the Dome Room or any high place; an earlier episode recorded a death and -10 from it.
