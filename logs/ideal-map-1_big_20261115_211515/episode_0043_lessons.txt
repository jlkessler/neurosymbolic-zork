LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen (+10), then open the trap door and go down (+25). Both are confirmed score events, so do them first.
2. Bank a treasure in the trophy case right after returning. The painting deposit recorded +6; the other treasures in earlier runs were never banked.
3. Light the lantern as soon as you arrive in a dark Cellar. No grue incident occurred.
4. Judge actions by recorded score deltas only. Ignore stale goal text such as '+1025' or '+60' and verify each gain.
5. Reuse confirmed return routes (Studio up, Kitchen west) to reach the Living Room. At a blocked or looping spot, test unexplored exits.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not attack the troll with the sword on a hunch. 'Hit troll with sword' ended in death (−10); look for other exits first.
2. Do not shuttle between two rooms three or more times without a new item or information. Cellar/East Chasm and Gallery/Studio were repeated at zero score.
3. Skip the mailbox and North House detour. It cost steps and recorded no score.
4. Do not follow a stale 'take bar' goal without a verified route. The Actor wandered for about 30 steps without reaching it.
5. Do not take skeletons or knives, or carry unbanked treasure into the Maze. The task summary reports a cursed-knife death there.
