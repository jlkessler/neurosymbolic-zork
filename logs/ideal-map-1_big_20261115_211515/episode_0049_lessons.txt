LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and descend (+25). Both are confirmed score events.
2. Turn the lantern on right after arriving in the dark Cellar; no grue incident occurred this run.
3. Take the Gallery art (+4) but keep it carried, and keep the route toward banking it active.
4. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits (such as Studio 'up') and test one before revisiting a known room.
5. When a symbolic route enters a room that just hurt you, retreat and pick a different exit yourself.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never drop a carried treasure away from the trophy case. The art was dropped in the Gallery, then retaken, wasting steps.
2. Do not enter the Troll Room again. Three entries ended in a -10 knockout; do not follow a navigate-home route through it.
3. Drop the stale Loud Room 'take bar' goal when the room is unreachable; it stayed active and nothing scored.
4. Do not shuttle Studio/Gallery/East Chasm/Cellar more than twice without a new item, clue or exit; about 30 steps scored nothing.
5. Skip low-value pickups like the leaflet unless they have a stated purpose; they scored nothing and cost steps.
