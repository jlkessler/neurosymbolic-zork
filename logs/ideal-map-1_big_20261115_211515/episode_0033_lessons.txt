LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, go west (+10), push the rug, open the trap door, go down (+25). Confirmed twice; do it first, quickly.
2. Turn the lantern on immediately after the trap door drops you into darkness. No dark deaths occurred.
3. Take both lantern and sword from the Living Room before descending, so you are armed before reaching any hostile creature.
4. Read each observation for new items or exits, and try 'take' once on anything portable and valuable-looking, then check the score.
5. After scoring a treasure, return along the known route to the trophy case and bank it before exploring further.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not 'eat garlic' or try other improvised item gambits while the troll is attacking. It cost -10 and ended the run.
2. Do not enter the troll room unarmed. Do not stand there trying inventory tricks; retreat or fight with the sword.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm and Troll repeatedly. After two visits with no change, pick an untried exit or a new action.
4. Do not keep chasing a stale 'take bar' goal that is unreachable. Try it once, read the response, then drop the goal.
5. Do not attack the thief, and do not wander the Forest early or take items like the leaflet, sack or lunch with no purpose.
