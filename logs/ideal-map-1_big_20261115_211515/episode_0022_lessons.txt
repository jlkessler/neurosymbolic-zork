LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg from the nest in the tree and head straight back; +5 was recorded on pickup.
2. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; the +10 was recorded on that move. Skip forest loops first.
3. Open the case and 'put egg in case' once, directly; +5 was recorded with no item churn.
4. Take the sword before pushing the rug, opening the trap door and going down; +25 was recorded and the sword stayed in inventory.
5. Explicitly 'take lantern', confirm it appears in inventory, and check that the Cellar is lit before any move there.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room, especially the Cellar, after arriving. 'Pitch black' means stop and fix light; moving north got the Actor eaten (-10).
2. Do not assume 'turn on lantern' means you hold it; the Cellar was dark despite 'now on'. Check inventory before descending.
3. Do not shuttle Clearing/Forest Path/Forest: about 13 steps produced no points, and picking up and dropping leaves achieved nothing.
4. Do not follow the stale Loud Room 'take bar' goal; it is unreachable. Pursue concrete local prerequisites like light first.
5. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed, never 'jump', and never take treasures back out of the case.
