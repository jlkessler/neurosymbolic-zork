LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen early; the move west scored +10. Verify the score response, then do not repeat it.
2. Take and light the lantern, then move the rug and open the trap door before descending. 'down' recorded +25.
3. Take portable valuables in newly reached rooms, and read the score response. Taking the art in the Gallery recorded +4.
4. Check inventory and what you carry before entering any room with a known hostile occupant. Carry a weapon first if one is available.
5. When a room's description says it blocks all exits, stop and act on that: change plan, and do not re-enter.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed. Four entries gained nothing, and the fifth knocked the Actor out for -10 and ended the episode.
2. Do not shuttle between Cellar and Troll Room or between Cellar and East Chasm. About 12 moves scored nothing; pick an untried exit.
3. Do not accept a symbolic home route through a known hostile room. Check the destination's danger first, and stop if the map edge leads into it.
4. Do not spend opening moves on forest loops, the leaflet or the attic with no new objective. Use a goal-directed route to the house.
5. Do not carry the art into danger and then wander without a plan. Find a verified safe route home before banking, and avoid fights.
