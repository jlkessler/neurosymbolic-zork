LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House, go to Behind House, open the window and enter west. The Kitchen entry recorded +10, so do it first and skip the mailbox and North House.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door and go down. This recorded +25, but take the lantern first.
3. Before descending, confirm the inventory holds a light source. The trap door crashes shut behind you, so prepare in the Living Room.
4. On arriving in darkness, make lighting the lantern the very next action. Take no exits until the room description is visible.
5. After each action, compare the score delta and the observation. Drop any goal hint whose prerequisites, such as light or a weapon, are not met.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move from the dark Cellar without light. The 'south' move in pitch black was followed by grue death and −10.
2. Do not descend with an empty inventory. This run took only a leaflet, and the lantern was never picked up.
3. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet or North House. Four zero-score actions delayed the only confirmed scoring route.
4. Do not follow stale 'take bar' or Loud Room goals while unlit and unarmed. Navigation hints are candidates, not permission to ignore danger.
5. Do not treat a warning like 'likely to be eaten by a grue' as flavor text. It is a signal to fix the problem immediately.
