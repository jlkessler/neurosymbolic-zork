LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, go west (+10), then do the trap door descent (+25). Confirmed twice: go directly, skip the Forest.
2. Carry the lantern and a weapon from the Living Room before going down; this run had both and passed the Troll Room safely.
3. After a score event, note the exact room and action; take, then immediately check inventory and score delta (+4 art, +10 bar confirmed).
4. Use the persistent map to return to the Living Room after taking a treasure; the home return was verified by step 21.
5. Try unfamiliar objects once and read the response. Reacting to feedback worked when a spoken name made the cyclops flee.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat a room action while the observation reports a worsening hazard (rising water level). Leave the room at the second warning; the run ended by drowning, -10.
2. Do not use 'put down' for treasure. It only dropped the bar. Use 'put <item> in case' in the Living Room and check for a score change.
3. Do not cycle Round Room, Loud Room, Deep Canyon, Narrow Passage and Mirror Room. About 100 steps scored nothing; after two repeats, pick an unvisited exit or a new goal.
4. Do not give items to the troll or enter the Troll Room unarmed. The earlier 'give troll leaves' run cost -10 and a knockout.
5. Do not replay a score-positive action whose reward is already collected. Revisit the stale 'take bar' or 'up' goals only if the observation shows a new item.
