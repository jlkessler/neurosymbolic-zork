LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, then west into the Kitchen scored +10. The Living Room lantern, rug and trap door sequence then scored +25 down; that goal is DONE, so do not repeat it.
2. Take and light the lantern before descending, and confirm it with inventory. The Cellar stayed safe this episode while the lantern was lit.
3. Underground route Cellar south, East Chasm, east to Gallery works. 'Take art' there scored +4 this episode; Studio is north of the Gallery.
4. Check the score after each action and credit only the action just before a score event, as the window/Kitchen and trap-door events were verified.
5. Use the underground map for planning once the trap door has crashed shut. Pick unexplored exits deliberately instead of reaching them by random wandering.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed or without a retreat plan. The final entry was followed by a knockout, a −10 and the end of the episode.
2. Do not follow symbolic 'return home' routes north from the Cellar blindly. The trap door is barred, so the home return was never verified.
3. Stop Cellar↔Troll Room shuttling. Three round trips with no inventory check, action or new plan produced no score.
4. Do not take, drop and retake the same treasure without a reason. 'Put down art' then 'take art' wasted two steps and scored nothing.
5. Do not open with stale goals or aimless loops. The Forest/Clearing detour cost about four moves before the productive Kitchen route.
