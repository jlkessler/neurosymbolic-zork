LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and go down (+25). Both gains reconfirmed this run.
2. Take the Gallery art (+4), return via Studio up and Kitchen west, open the case, then put the art in it (+6).
3. Take the lantern from the Living Room and turn it on right after reaching the dark Cellar. No grue incident occurred.
4. After banking treasure, list the exits you have not tried in each visited room and test those before revisiting any known room.
5. Judge progress only by recorded score deltas. Treat '+1000' and '+60' goal text as stale and drop a goal when prerequisites are unmet.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll Room again without a verified plan. This room caused the death and -10 here, and earlier episodes ended there too.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm or Gallery/Studio more than twice without a new item or clue. These loops scored zero for ~70 steps.
3. Abandon 'take bar' as a goal now. About 35 steps went to it without reaching the Loud Room or scoring.
4. Do not claim 'only one action available' when exits exist. Check the room's exit list and try an untested direction before a known-dangerous one.
5. Skip the opening North House detour and ordinary-item pickups (sack, lunch, garlic, manual) unless a clear use appears. They cost steps and scored nothing.
