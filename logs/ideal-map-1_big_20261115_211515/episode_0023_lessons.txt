LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; the +10 was recorded on that move. Do it first, skip forest and mailbox detours.
2. Take the lantern and sword from the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, go down; +25 recorded with both items held.
3. Kill the troll with the sword you carry (it died in one hit here). Fight only while armed.
4. 'take art' in the Gallery gave +4 once. Carry it directly back to the Living Room trophy case instead of juggling it.
5. After any 'turn on lantern', confirm the room text is lit and the lantern is in inventory before moving.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black. Death came from moving northwest with the lantern given away; relight first or stay put.
2. Never give the lantern, sack or other tools to the thief ('give thief ...'). It removed your light and sealed the death.
3. Do not toggle the lantern off/on beside the cyclops or take/drop the same item repeatedly; roughly 100 steps produced zero points.
4. Ignore the stale Loud Room 'take bar' goal when no route is confirmed; it drove the maze shuttling. Pick a concrete local objective.
5. Do not leave the painting loose or in transit for long; no banking happened after the +4. Return to the case before exploring further.
