LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; +10 was recorded on that move in two episodes. Do it first, skipping detours.
2. In the Living Room take lantern and sword, push the rug, open the trap door, go down; +25 was recorded on reaching the Cellar.
3. Turn on the lantern as soon as you arrive in the dark Cellar, before any movement; this episode did so and stayed safe.
4. Check inventory for the sword before entering the Troll Room; fetch it from the Living Room first. An earlier +5 followed an east move with the sword held.
5. After any score event, check inventory for treasures and bank them in the Living Room trophy case before exploring deeper.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed or retry it repeatedly: the troll knocked me out, -10, and the episode ended on the fifth visit.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar and Troll Room (eight scoreless moves). After two scoreless visits, try a different exit, object, or the Living Room.
3. Never 'jump' at the Dome Room or any high place: it caused death, -10, and ended the episode.
4. Do not loop Loud Room, Dam, Dam Lobby, Round Room and Engravings Cave: about 120 steps earned zero. Drop 'take bar' and 'up' goals after two failures.
5. Never move in darkness or give the lantern or sack to the thief; do not keep pushing Maintenance Room buttons once nothing scores.
