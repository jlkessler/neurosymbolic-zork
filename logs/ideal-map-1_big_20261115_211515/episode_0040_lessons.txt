LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen from Behind House for +10; go there directly without the North House detour.
2. Take the lantern, push rug, open trap, go down (+25), then immediately turn on the lantern in the Cellar. This was confirmed safe.
3. With the lantern lit, Cellar south reaches East Chasm, then Gallery east. Taking the painting there recorded +4; repeat this scoring side route.
4. When carrying a treasure, use the symbolic route home (Studio up, Kitchen west) and verify the score at the destination.
5. After each action, compare the score delta and drop goal hints that show no fresh gain.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room without a weapon in inventory. The axe hit, then north produced -10 and the terminal state.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar and Troll Room. About ten zero-score moves followed an 'axe gets you' warning; change plan after one hostile blow.
3. 'Put down' or 'drop' does not bank a treasure. Use an explicit put-in-trophy-case command and confirm the score before leaving the Living Room.
4. Ignore stale 'take bar' goals while the troll is alive and the prerequisites are unmet. They drove the fatal Troll Room retries.
5. Avoid zero-score opening detours such as North House and returning east. Do not descend again with only a lantern and no banked or defended plan.
