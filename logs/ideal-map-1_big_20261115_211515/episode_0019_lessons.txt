LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window from Behind House and go west for +10, then take the trapdoor down for +25; both scored again and are reliable early.
2. Bank each treasure in the trophy case right after pickup: painting (+4/+6), bar (+10/+5) and egg (+5/+5) each scored once.
3. Carry the sword and lantern before going underground; armed Troll Room passage worked, and the east exit gave +5 with no death.
4. When score stalls, probe unvisited exits and rooms, such as maze branches and forest areas; the bar and egg both came from exploration.
5. Check inventory for a lit lantern before dark rooms, and credit only recorded score deltas; the lit state was never confirmed this run.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never take a treasure out of the trophy case; each take cost -5 or -6 and re-depositing only restored it, about a dozen times for net zero.
2. After two zero-score passes, stop cycling Living Room↔Cellar or Kitchen↔Attic; roughly 100 moves after step 404 produced nothing.
3. Ignore stale "'up' +15" score hints when repeated 'up' probes at Cellar, Kitchen and Attic return 0; re-read the room and exits instead.
4. Don't churn open/close window or put/take axe, leaves and sack items; none of these ever changed the score.
5. Don't 'jump' at East Chasm or use other risky verbs; that killed an earlier run (-10).
