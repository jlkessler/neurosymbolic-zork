LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, go west (+10), take lantern, push rug, open trap door, go down (+25). Confirmed again; do it directly, skipping the Forest.
2. Light the lantern the moment you land in darkness below the trap door. It worked again with no dark-related harm.
3. Take both lantern and sword from the Living Room before descending. This run took only the lantern, so check inventory for a weapon first.
4. Read each observation for new exits and portable items, try one 'take', then check the score delta to see whether it mattered.
5. After scoring a treasure, return by the known route to the trophy case and bank it before exploring further.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not give items to the troll or try other gambits there. 'give troll leaves' gave -10 and a knockout that ended the run.
2. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed. Without the sword, retreat and fetch it rather than standing beside the troll.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar and Troll Room. Five moves yielded nothing; after one blocked visit, change plan or try another exit.
4. Do not spend early steps wandering the Forest and Clearing before the window; about 7 steps scored nothing.
5. Do not carry a stale 'take bar' goal whose location is unreachable. Try it once or drop it, and plan from current observations.
