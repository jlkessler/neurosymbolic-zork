LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window and enter the Kitchen (+10), then move the rug, open the trap door and go down (+25). Both are confirmed score events.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before or immediately after entering the dark Cellar; no grue incident occurred.
3. Take the Gallery art (+4) and keep it in inventory. Head straight for the trophy case rather than exploring first.
4. Before leaving a room, list its untried exits and choose an unvisited one over a known room.
5. Drop a stale score goal after two failed approaches and re-plan from the current room. Judge progress by recorded score deltas, not goal text.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never drop or put down a carried treasure away from the trophy case. The art was dropped in the Gallery and abandoned, scoring nothing.
2. Treat the Troll Room as lethal: one look, then leave. Never fight it or give it items; a -10 death ended this run.
3. Do not pursue the 'take bar' Loud Room goal. It stayed active for most of the run, the room was never reached, and nothing scored.
4. Do not shuttle between two rooms more than twice without a new item, clue or exit (Cellar/Troll Room/East Chasm, Gallery/Studio).
5. Skip low-value pickups (leaflet, manual, sack, coil, knife) unless they have a stated purpose. They scored nothing and cost steps.
