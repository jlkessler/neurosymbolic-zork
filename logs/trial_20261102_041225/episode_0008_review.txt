Historian episode review — episode 8
================================================

Review source: llm

The Actor demonstrated strong adherence to safety protocols, specifically turning on the lantern before descending into the cellar, and executed a complex navigation route through the house window to deposit the egg. Combat with the troll was handled decisively, and the Actor successfully collected several items across multiple locations.

However, the Actor's efficiency suffered due to repetitive movement loops between the Reservoir, Stream View, and the Dam. There was a notable failure to collect the platinum bar in the Loud Room despite the Actor's internal notes acknowledging its availability. Most critically, the Actor made a high-risk decision to jump across the chasm without prior confirmation of safety, which resulted in character death and a score penalty.
