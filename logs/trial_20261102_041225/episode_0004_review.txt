Historian episode review — episode 4
================================================

Review source: llm

The Actor showed strong initiative in environmental interaction, successfully retrieving the jewel-encrusted egg and utilizing the trophy case. A particularly strong decision was discovering and using the open window to enter the kitchen, which provided a score increase and a route into the house. The Actor also correctly identified the need to collect the sword and lantern before descending into the cellar.

However, the Actor's performance was marred by inefficient navigation, specifically a repetitive oscillation between the West House and North House. Most critically, while the Actor acquired the lantern, it failed to light it before entering the pitch-black cellar. This ineffective behavior led directly to the Actor's death by a grue when attempting to move north in the dark. Future episodes must prioritize the active use of light sources immediately upon entering dark environments.
