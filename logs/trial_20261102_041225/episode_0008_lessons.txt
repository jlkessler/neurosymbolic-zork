LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Use environmental interactions, such as opening windows, to uncover new paths and increase score.
2. Prioritize the collection of essential tools like the lantern and sword during initial exploration.
3. Secure treasures in designated containers (e.g., the trophy case) to realize score gains.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid high-risk physical actions, such as jumping, without evidence that the action is safe.
2. Avoid repetitive movement loops between known locations when no new items or score increases are observed.
3. Ensure items explicitly mentioned in room descriptions are targeted for collection before leaving the area.
