LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Investigate environmental objects (e.g., mailbox, rugs) to uncover hidden items or passages.
2. Ensure light sources are active before entering dark areas like the cellar.
3. Utilize available equipment, such as the sword, to remove hostile obstacles.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid aimless oscillation between rooms (e.g., the Dam and Deep Canyon) without a clear exit strategy.
2. Avoid high-risk actions like jumping in hazardous areas (e.g., chasms) without evidence of safety.
3. Do not repeat 'put item in case' actions if they do not yield an immediate recorded score increase.
