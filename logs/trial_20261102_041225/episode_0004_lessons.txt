LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Investigate environmental objects (e.g., mailbox, rugs, windows) to discover hidden items or alternative paths.
2. Collect all available items in a new room to ensure necessary resources are gathered.
3. Deposit discovered treasures into the trophy case to secure score increases.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repetitive oscillation between a small set of rooms (e.g., West House and North House).
2. Do not enter pitch-black environments without first activating a light source, even if the item is in the inventory.
3. Avoid making movement attempts in total darkness, as this leads to death by a grue.
