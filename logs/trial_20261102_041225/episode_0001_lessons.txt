LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Retrieve and place treasures in the trophy case promptly after discovery.
2. Ensure light sources are active before entering dark areas like the cellar.
3. Investigate environmental objects (e.g., mailbox, rugs) to uncover hidden items or passages.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat 'put item in case' actions if they do not yield an immediate score increase.
2. Avoid giving items to hostile creatures unless there is a confirmed benefit.
3. Avoid aimless oscillation between rooms (e.g., Cellar and Troll Room) without a clear objective.
