LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Use environmental interactions, such as opening windows, to uncover new paths and increase score.
2. Prioritize the collection of essential tools like the lantern and sword during initial exploration.
3. Validate successful transitions into new rooms by monitoring for score increases.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repetitive cycles of placing and removing items from containers when no score increase is observed.
2. Do not enter dark areas without first confirming that a light source is active.
3. Avoid irrelevant action loops, such as toggling equipment on and off, when a clear objective is available.
