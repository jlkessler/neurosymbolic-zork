LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Investigate environmental objects (e.g., mailbox, rugs) to uncover hidden items or passages.
2. Use active light sources (e.g., lantern, matches) when entering dark environments.
3. Apply appropriate tools (e.g., sword) to remove hostile obstacles.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repetitive oscillation between a small set of rooms (e.g., Reservoir, Stream View, Chasm).
2. Avoid destroying permanent equipment (e.g., the lantern) for experimental interactions.
3. Do not repeat actions that do not yield a recorded score increase, such as depositing items in the trophy case.
