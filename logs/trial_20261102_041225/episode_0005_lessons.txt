LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Prioritize interactions that produced an observed score increase; revisit them only when the context supports it.
2. Continue exploring newly discovered locations and recording their transitions in the persistent map.
3. Use Jericho score deltas as feedback rather than relying only on whether an action sounds promising.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repeating an unsuccessful command without a new clue, item, or change in circumstances.
2. Do not treat remembered routes, inventory guesses, or puzzle hypotheses as confirmed facts.
3. Avoid exploring without a concrete short-term objective when a score opportunity or unresolved obstacle is already evident.
