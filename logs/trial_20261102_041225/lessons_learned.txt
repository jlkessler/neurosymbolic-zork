LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Use environmental interactions, such as opening windows, to discover new paths and access locked areas.
2. Prioritize the collection of essential tools like the lantern and sword from the trophy case during initial exploration.
3. Secure treasures in designated containers (e.g., the trophy case) to realize score gains.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repetitive navigation loops between a small set of rooms, such as the loop between Reservoir South, Stream View, and the Chasm.
2. Do not use arbitrary 'hit' or 'cut' commands with weapons unless a specific, non-self target is identified.
3. Avoid random walking in maze environments; establish a systematic method for tracking visited rooms to find an exit.
