Historian episode review — episode 6
================================================

Review source: llm

The Actor demonstrated strong sequencing and environmental interaction once inside the house. It successfully executed a series of logical steps: opening the window to enter the Kitchen, collecting items, and preparing for the descent into the Cellar by acquiring the lantern and sword, lighting the lantern, and clearing the trap door. These actions were efficient and directly correlated with the observed score increases in the Kitchen and Cellar.

However, the Actor's early performance was plagued by ineffective repetitive movement, cycling between the North, West, and Behind House locations multiple times before attempting the window. Additionally, the Actor failed to pivot strategy after a failed attack on the troll; the second attempt resulted in a score penalty and the termination of the episode. The Actor should prioritize breaking movement loops and re-evaluating combat tactics after an initial failure.
