LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Use observed score increases to validate that environmental interactions (like entering a building) are successful.
2. Execute logical item-use sequences, such as lighting a lantern before descending into darkness, to ensure safe transitions.
3. Prioritize the collection and placement of items in discovered containers to test for score opportunities.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Avoid repetitive movement cycles between known rooms when no new objective or clue has been found.
2. Do not repeat a failed combat action without first searching the environment for alternative tools or clues.
3. Avoid entering high-threat encounters without a confirmed strategy or necessary equipment.
