5. Do not trust planner gain figures (+425, +15, +20); observed events were +10, +25, +4, then -10.
6. Do not drop and retake a treasure (art or painting) without a purpose; the drop, retake and put-down loop scored 0.
7. Do not collect or hand over items (leaflet, manual, food, garlic) without a stated purpose, and never give them to hostile creatures.
8. Do not treat the thief as solved: giving it lunch is unverified, and an earlier sword attack killed the Actor.
