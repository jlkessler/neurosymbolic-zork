LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. 'Take art' at the Gallery gave +4. In new rooms, examine objects and take plausible valuables, then check the score line to verify.
4. Run 'inventory' before descending or entering any hostile room, and confirm both lantern and sword are carried.
5. In pitch black, light the lantern first and do not move until the room text shows light.
6. Trust only recorded score events. Ignore replayed 'take bar' and 'take egg' goals that point at unreachable places.
7. After two visits to the same room pair, pick an untried exit or use look/examine instead of reversing.
8. Before a room with a hostile occupant, read the text and use look/examine once. Retreat if blocked; never test risky interactions.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'give' items to the troll. 'give troll manual' led to decapitation and -10, ending the episode.
2. Do not keep re-entering the Troll room unprepared. Repeated visits gave no score and no new information.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. Over 100 moves after +4 produced no new score.
4. Do not follow replayed 'take bar' or 'take egg' goals; they never produced points here and drove the loops.
5. Do not move north in the dark Cellar without a lit lantern; a grue risk killed a previous episode.
6. Do not descend without confirming lantern and sword via 'inventory'. Notes claimed a lantern, but it was never verified.
7. Do not use drop/take of the leaflet or manual as a probe. It did nothing, and read the leaflet instead.
8. Do not wander underground with treasure while the route home is unverified. The painting was never banked.
