LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat this opening before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down: that 'down' move recorded Cellar +25.
3. Before descending, run 'inventory' and confirm a light source is held and lit. The Cellar arrival text was pitch black.
4. If text says dark or pitch black, stop and fix light first: check inventory, take or light the lantern, and do not move.
5. Prefer visible, state-matching actions over stale historical goals; the window, rug and trap-door steps all matched the room text.
6. On a second entry to any room, pick an unvisited exit or an unused item action instead of the move just used.
7. Trust only recorded score events. Treat historical gain estimates for take egg, take bar or down as unverified candidates.
8. Take the lantern from the Living Room before descending, then check inventory to verify it is carried and lit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the room is pitch black. Leaving the dark Cellar (logged 'south') ended the game with a -10 grue death.
2. Do not descend the trap door without confirming a lit light source. It crashes shut behind you, so there is no retreat.
3. Do not treat a dark exit as the only direction to explore. Dark means light first, or stay put.
4. Do not shuttle Kitchen<->Living Room. Both rooms were entered twice this episode with no score. Use a new exit after one revisit.
5. Do not trust a 'lantern is on' message alone. The Cellar was still pitch black, so verify with inventory and look before moving.
6. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals. They pointed at unreachable places and never produced recorded points.
7. Do not leave the leaflet unread. Read it and check inventory before a risky descent.
8. Do not make exploratory detours such as North House when the scoring route is known, unless the exits are untried and safe.
