LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: Kitchen +10 was recorded. Repeat this opening before any side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, take sword and lantern, check 'inventory', then go down. That 'down' recorded Cellar +25.
3. 'take art' in the Gallery recorded +4, and 'put painting in case' at the Living Room recorded +6. Carry scored items straight home.
4. Check that the lantern is lit on arriving underground. If the text says dim, return to a lit room before any further move.
5. Retreating south from the Troll room each time avoided harm. Re-enter only with a sword confirmed by 'inventory', and attack once.
6. Set one unexplored exit per goal. After a room's second entry, take a different exit than the one just used.
7. Trust only recorded score events. Ignore historical 'gain' estimates that lack a verified route from your current room.
8. When the text warns about light, fix that first by checking the lantern or going to a lit room. Never push on.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move while the lantern is dim or the room is dark. The run ended with a -10 move out of the Cellar.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar<->East Chasm, Gallery<->Studio or Cellar<->Troll. Dozens of moves there scored 0. Pick an unvisited exit after the second visit.
3. Do not follow replayed 'take bar' or 'take egg' goals. No route was ever verified, and they drove most of the cycling.
4. Do not enter the Troll room without 'inventory' showing a sword. A knife attack only pinked the troll, so do not repeat it.
5. Do not 'open bottle', drop the manual, or give items to the thief. Those actions scored nothing and cost steps.
6. Do not hand items to the thief or interact with him near the Cellar. 'Give thief water' changed nothing, and the last room showed a hostile bag-carrier.
7. Do not keep issuing moves after 20 steps with no new score or new room. Switch to an untried exit immediately.
8. Do not leave the Living Room area with a scored treasure still in hand. Deposit it first, then explore.
