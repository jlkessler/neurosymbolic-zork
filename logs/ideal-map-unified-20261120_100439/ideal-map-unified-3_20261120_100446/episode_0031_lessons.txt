LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this early instead of wandering the mailbox, North House or forest.
2. Living Room 'push rug', 'open trap', 'down' recorded Cellar +25. The trap door then shut and was barred behind us.
3. 'turn on lantern' succeeded in the Living Room before the descent. Light the lantern, and confirm the light source with 'inventory', before any trap door descent.
4. After any one-way descent, run 'look' and 'inventory' before moving. If the room says pitch black, do not use any exit.
5. Credit only recorded score changes. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and points-per-attempt figures.
6. Treat planner goals as candidates. Check the current room, inventory and prerequisites first, and drop goals that are stale or unreachable.
7. If a room pair repeats three times with no new observation, switch to an untried verb, object or exit.
8. Take portable valuables when seen and bank them in the Living Room trophy case before risky descents. An earlier run recorded 'take art' +4.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch darkness. 'south' from the dark Cellar recorded a grue death (-10) and ended the run at 25.
2. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge. An earlier run recorded -10 and death.
3. Do not assume a lit lantern means light. The Cellar still read 'pitch black' after 'turn on lantern', so verify with 'look' before acting.
4. Do not pursue 'take bar' or any Loud Room goal. It is unreachable until a lit route there is verified.
5. Do not follow 'take egg' or 'down' goals blindly. Check the prerequisites and the current room first, because they were repeated without checks.
6. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet, or North House/West House shuttle. Steps 1-8 gained nothing.
7. Do not waste turns on item management in the dark, such as dropping the leaflet. Use that turn for 'look' or 'inventory'.
8. Do not shuttle among East Chasm, Cellar, Gallery and Studio. An earlier run spent about 35-45 steps there with no score after +4.
