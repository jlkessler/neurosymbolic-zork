LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window and go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Do this first and skip forest detours.
2. In the Living Room take lantern and sword, push rug, open trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on descent.
3. On pitch-black Cellar arrival, 'turn on lantern' first and move only after light is confirmed; this run it avoided the grue.
4. Wield the sword and attack the troll; it was knocked out and the next east move recorded +5. Never hand items over.
5. Examine and take portable items in newly entered rooms; Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Check each new room's objects once on first entry.
6. Read the manual and guidebook right after taking them; both were taken this run but never read, so their hints went unused.
7. After two failed or repeated moves between the same rooms, try a never-used exit instead of reversing; list untried exits before choosing.
8. Override stale goals such as 'take egg', 'take bar' and repeated 'down' when unreachable; trust only recorded score events and flag rooms visited 3+ times.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without light; dark movement in the Cellar caused grue deaths and -10 in earlier runs.
2. Never 'give' items to the troll; earlier runs ended in death and -10. Wield the sword and attack instead.
3. Do not shuttle between adjacent rooms such as Gallery/Studio or Dam Lobby/Maintenance Room; this run spent ~100 moves cycling for zero score.
4. Do not push the brown button repeatedly in the Maintenance Room; water rose from knees to neck with no score. Stop once an action only worsens the room.
5. Do not run the 'return to Living Room' goal for dozens of steps once the route is unknown; the trap door is barred, so explore fresh exits.
6. Do not chase the stale 'take bar' goal into the Loud Room again; repeated visits produced no pickup or score this run.
7. Do not descend without both lantern and sword; check inventory before 'down' because the trap door bars behind you.
8. Do not collect tools like wrench, matchbook and tube without a plan or a recorded score effect; none produced points this run.
