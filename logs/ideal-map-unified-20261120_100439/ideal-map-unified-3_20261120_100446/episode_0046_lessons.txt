LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it early.
2. Take the lantern in the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. In pitch black, make 'turn on lantern' the only first action. This run did so and survived the Cellar arrival.
4. Run 'inventory' before descending and confirm the lantern is carried. This run held only lantern and leaflet, so check for a weapon too.
5. After taking the leaflet from the mailbox, read it; it is free information and was carried unread this run.
6. Trust only recorded score events. Override stale 'take egg' and 'take bar' goals when their locations are unreachable or irrelevant.
7. When blocked or attacked, stop and use look, examine or inventory, then pick a different action instead of repeating the same move.
8. After two visits to a room pair, take an untried exit, such as the Kitchen's upward staircase, instead of reversing.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'give' items to the troll. 'give troll manual' and 'give troll leaflet' both ended in death and -10.
2. Never move in a dark room without a lit light source. Moving south in the dark Cellar caused grue death and -10.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar and Troll. About five round trips this run gave no score and brought axe hits.
4. Do not drop and retake items in front of a hostile troll. It wasted turns and cost health without helping.
5. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals; they produced loops and no observed score this run.
6. Do not re-enter the Troll room unarmed. Verify a weapon is wielded, and attack instead of offering items.
7. Do not descend without checking the house for useful gear. Only a lantern and leaflet were carried underground this run.
8. Do not keep a failing pattern past three visits to the same room. Flag Cellar and Troll as overvisited and change plan.
