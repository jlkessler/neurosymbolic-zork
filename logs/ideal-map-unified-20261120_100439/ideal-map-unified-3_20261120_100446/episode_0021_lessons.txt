LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move in this episode and in earlier ones.
2. In the Living Room: take lantern, push rug, open trap door, go down. Cellar +25 was recorded on the descent.
3. Light the lantern right after reaching the Cellar; arrival was pitch black and the trap door shuts behind you.
4. Verified edges: Cellar south to East Chasm, East Chasm east to Gallery, Gallery north to Studio, Kitchen west to Living Room.
5. Gallery 'take art' gave +4. After taking a treasure, head straight to untested exits that could lead home.
6. From the Studio, try 'up' first; it is the untested candidate route back to the Kitchen and was never tried.
7. Check the score after every action and credit only recorded score events, never planner gain estimates.
8. When a goal stalls, test one unused exit or examine one object (e.g. the Studio paper) rather than re-walking known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; it killed the Actor (-10). Avoid untested risky verbs near drops.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio without a new observation; roughly 35 moves scored nothing this episode.
3. Avoid the Troll room, which has an armed troll. A brief entry and exit survived here, but it offers no route home.
4. Do not chase stale goal hints ('down', 'take egg', 'take bar'); none produced reachable score after the Cellar.
5. Do not trust planner gain figures (+450, +15, +20); observed events were +10, +25, +4, then -10.
6. Do not drop and retake a treasure (art or painting) without a purpose; each loop scored 0 and wasted moves.
7. Do not open with the forest loop or wander North House and forest before the window; about 7 early moves scored 0.
8. Do not pick up or carry items (leaflet, manual) without a stated purpose, and never give items to hostile creatures.
