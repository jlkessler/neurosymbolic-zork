LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take portable objects in newly entered rooms and compare score immediately; taking the egg in Up a Tree gave a recorded +5.
2. Use room ids to tell apart same-named rooms, such as Clearing #74 versus #143, before declaring a location new.
3. When a fixed feature like a grating is noticed, examine it and try several plausible verbs on it before leaving.
4. Run 'inventory' after any score event to confirm what is carried, and avoid dropping items that may have scored.
5. Before leaving a new room, run look and examine each listed object once, then record what changed.
6. Choose exits whose destinations are absent from the visited list; verify against the list before labeling a move as frontier exploration.
7. Rely on confirmed transitions to build a map of exits, then use it to select unexplored branches methodically.
8. After about 20 steps with no score or new room, switch to a different region or a different verb type.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat take/drop leaves; many repeats across several rooms gave zero score and no new information.
2. Do not oscillate between Forest Path, Clearing and Forest; this loop consumed hundreds of steps without discovering anything.
3. Do not repeat up/down tree pairs once the room is examined; they produced no score after the first visit.
4. Do not call a move 'frontier exploration' when the destination was already visited; this mislabeling hid the lack of progress.
5. Do not ignore noted fixed features such as the grating; leaving them untested wasted a concrete lead.
6. Do not stay in the same ten-room area for 500 steps; unvisited exits near the house were left untried.
7. Do not assume inventory state from memory; the notes repeatedly said 'empty-handed' without verification after taking the egg.
8. Do not spend actions on 'go around forest' loops; the same movement cycle recurred with identical results.
