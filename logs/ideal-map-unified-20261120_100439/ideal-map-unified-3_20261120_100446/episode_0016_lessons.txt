LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move.
2. Take the lantern, turn it on, and carry the sword before descending; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Verified route: Behind House west, Kitchen west, push rug, open trap door, then down to the Cellar; the trap door shuts behind you.
4. Verified underground edges: Cellar north to Troll, east to East-West Passage, north to Chasm, south to North-South Passage, then northeast to Deep Canyon.
5. From Deep Canyon, 'down' reaches the Loud Room, where a platinum bar was observed on the ground; take visible items before experimenting.
6. 'hit troll with sword' killed the troll in one action; +5 appeared on the next move east, so credit only recorded events.
7. Check the score and observation after every action; credit only recorded score events, never planner estimates.
8. Before leaving a room carrying a valuable, note and test a route back to the Living Room, and deposit treasures in the case.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not open with mailbox, North House, west/east forest or 'go around forest' wandering; about nine early steps scored 0.
2. Do not shuttle Kitchen/Living Room or Cellar/Troll without a new reason; repeated back-and-forth moves scored nothing.
3. Do not chase stale planner hints ('take egg', 'take bar', Living Room 'down') unless the observation confirms the object and a route exists.
4. Do not trust planner gain figures (+350, +15, +20); the observed events were +10, +25 and +5.
5. Do not spend a turn on 'echo' or other aimless commands when a visible object, such as the bar, can be examined or taken.
6. Do not push deeper underground carrying nothing of value and with no verified path home; the home return was never confirmed.
7. Do not start melee with a thief (an earlier report, not re-observed here, says it ended in death at East Chasm); retreat instead.
8. Do not pick up a dropped weapon while an enemy is still attacking; reassess first, and do not assume the thief is avoidable.
