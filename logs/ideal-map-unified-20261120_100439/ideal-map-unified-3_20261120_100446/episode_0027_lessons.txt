LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10; do this directly instead of wandering the forest.
2. 'take egg' at Up a Tree recorded +5; keep the treasure in inventory and carry it straight toward the Living Room case.
3. In the Living Room, push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded Cellar +25; light source first.
4. Take the lantern and light it before any descent; the trap door shuts behind you and the Cellar is pitch black.
5. Credit only recorded score events; compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates.
6. Prefer 'carry treasure home' over repeated exploit goals; bank treasure in the trophy case before exploring further.
7. If a room is revisited three times with no new observation, switch to an untried action or a known route instead of cycling.
8. From the Studio, test UP as the way home (unverified) before trying the Cellar, since the trap door does not reopen.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while in pitch darkness; 'north' from the dark Cellar caused the grue death (-10) and ended the run.
2. Do not descend the trap door without a lit lantern; the door crashes shut behind you and there is no light.
3. Treat 'take bar' and Loud Room goals as stale and unreachable; they pulled the Actor north into the dark.
4. Do not put treasure into the grating or drop it; the egg fell into the darkness below and left inventory.
5. Do not loop Forest Path, Clearing, Forest and Up a Tree; about 25 steps produced no score while the window route stayed unused.
6. Do not retreat through the Cellar while carrying treasure, or shuttle Cellar/East Chasm; those loops never scored.
7. Do not end an episode with treasure unbanked; the art (+4) and the egg count for nothing outside the case.
8. Avoid the Cellar's north exit to the Troll room; it recorded no gain.
