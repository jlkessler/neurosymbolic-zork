LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Do it first, skipping forest detours.
2. In the Living Room, take the sword (confirmed 'Taken.'), push the rug, open the trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded.
3. Run 'inventory' before descending and confirm a light source is carried; this run descended without any recorded lantern.
4. Take the lantern from the Living Room before the trap door; the Cellar is pitch black and the door shut behind us.
5. On pitch-black arrival, make 'turn on lantern' the first action if carried; avoid any movement command until light is confirmed.
6. Take and read the mailbox leaflet; opening the mailbox revealed it this run, but the Actor left it behind.
7. Override stale 'take egg', 'take bar' and repeated 'down' goals when they are unreachable or already used; trust only recorded score events.
8. After two visits to a room, take an untried exit, such as the Kitchen's upward staircase, instead of reversing.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without a lit light source; moving south in the dark Cellar caused grue death and -10.
2. Never 'give' items to the troll; earlier runs ended in death and -10. Wield the sword and attack instead.
3. Do not spend the opening moves looping Forest, Forest Path and Clearings; six such moves gave no score.
4. Do not descend carrying only a sword; check for lantern and other useful gear first.
5. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals; they produced no observed score and pointed to unreachable places.
6. Do not shuttle between Cellar and Troll rooms; flag any room visited 3+ times as overvisited and change plan.
7. Do not enter the Troll room unarmed or without a wielded weapon; do not drop and retake items beside a hostile troll.
8. Do not open the mailbox and walk away without taking the leaflet; unread items waste free information.
