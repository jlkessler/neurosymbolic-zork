LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window Behind House and enter the Kitchen; the recorded +10 came on that entry. Reach it quickly.
2. Open the trophy case before depositing treasure; put egg in case gave a recorded +2, so repeat that sequence.
3. Push the rug and open the trap door to reach the Cellar; that descent recorded +25.
4. Take portable objects in new rooms and compare score at once; take egg in Up a Tree recorded +5.
5. Light the lantern before the dark Attic or underground rooms; the Attic was dark and risky.
6. Check room ids to tell same-named rooms apart (Clearing #74 vs #143) before calling a destination new.
7. After 20 steps with no score or new room, switch to a different region or verb type.
8. Before leaving Gallery, East Chasm, Attic or Kitchen, examine each listed object and untested exit once, and record the result.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not cycle Clearing, Forest, Forest Path and Up a Tree; about 290 steps there gave no score.
2. Do not repeat open/close on the egg, nest or window; these toggles produced no score and no new route.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm and Troll without a new action; roughly 20 steps passed with no score.
4. Do not take items or fight in the Troll room; taking the sword there cost −10 and killed the Actor.
5. Do not return to the troll after a missed hit; the first swing was dodged and the counterattack was lethal.
6. Do not call 'go around forest' or up/down tree moves frontier exploration; they only revisited known rooms.
7. Do not leave Gallery, the Kitchen chimney, the Attic and the Cellar's west ramp unexamined while looping elsewhere.
8. Do not pick up the sword or other items next to a hostile creature without checking its state.
