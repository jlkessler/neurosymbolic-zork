LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this early instead of wandering through North House or the forest.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending. This run did so, and the Living Room 'down' step recorded Cellar +25.
3. In the Living Room, push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded Cellar +25. The door shut behind the Actor, so prepare first.
4. In the Gallery, 'take art' recorded +4. Pick up portable valuables in new rooms, then keep them in inventory.
5. Credit only recorded score events. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and historical points-per-attempt figures.
6. Prefer 'carry treasure home' goals. Keep treasure in inventory and bank it in the Living Room trophy case before exploring further.
7. After a one-way descent, use 'look' and examine features, then test each untried exit once. Record dead ends so you do not revisit them.
8. If a room pair repeats three times with no new observation, switch to an untried verb, object or exit instead of cycling.

2. Treat 'take bar'/Loud Room as stale. The planner pushed it about 15 times and the Actor never reached it.
3. Do not shuttle East Chasm, Cellar, Gallery and Studio. About 35 steps there produced no score after the Gallery +4.
4. Do not put down or drop a carried treasure, as 'put down art' did in the Gallery, while the way home is unknown.
5. Do not waste opening steps shuttling West House and North House. Steps 3-7 gained nothing before the window route was used.
6. Do not retry the Cellar's north exit to the Troll room. It recorded no gain and the Actor noted the troll blocks exits.
7. Do not end with treasure unbanked or without a verified route home. Home return was never verified this run.
8. When every exit is exhausted, do not choose a random risky action. Try examine, look, inventory or an untried object first.
