LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this first instead of wandering the North House or forest.
2. Living Room sequence 'push rug', 'open trap', 'down' recorded Cellar +25. The trap door then shut and was barred behind us.
3. Before 'down', run 'inventory' and confirm a light source is carried and lit. If none, search the house with 'look' and 'examine' first.
4. Credit only recorded score changes. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and points-per-attempt figures.
5. Bank treasures in the Living Room trophy case before descending, and take portable valuables when seen. An earlier run recorded 'take art' +4.
6. After a one-way descent, run 'look' and 'inventory' first. If the room is lit, test each untried exit once and record dead ends.
7. If a room pair repeats three times with no new observation, switch to an untried verb, object or exit.
8. Treat planner goals as candidates. Check the current observation and prerequisites first, and drop goals that are stale or unreachable.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch darkness. 'north' from the unlit Cellar recorded a grue death (-10) and ended the run at 25.
2. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge. An earlier run recorded -10 and death.
3. Do not descend with no lit light source or verified route home. This run descended unlit and the trap door barred behind us.
4. Do not pursue 'take bar' or any Loud Room goal. It is unreachable until a lit route there is verified.
5. Do not follow 'take egg' or 'down' goals blindly. Check the prerequisites and the current room first, because they were repeated without checks.
6. Do not waste opening steps on the mailbox, North House, Forest Path and Clearing loops. Steps 1-10 gained nothing.
7. Do not drop or put down a carried treasure while the way home is unknown.
8. Do not shuttle among East Chasm, Cellar, Gallery and Studio. An earlier run spent about 35-45 steps there with no score after +4.
