LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window before entering; the recorded Kitchen +10 followed 'west' once the window was open.
2. Take the lantern, push the rug and open the trap door before descending; Cellar +25 followed that descent.
3. Light the lantern immediately on reaching a dark room, then re-check fuel before each further move.
4. Once the painting is held, go straight home (Gallery, Studio, up to Kitchen, west) and deposit it, verifying the score.
5. Drop a stale goal after one failed attempt, such as 'take egg at Up a Tree' when the tree was never reached.
6. Read items you open, such as the mailbox leaflet, before leaving; text clues are cheap and unused.
7. Use room ids to tell same-named rooms apart, and treat the verified home route as the safe fallback.
8. Credit score only to the exact action and observation that recorded it, and log the before and after score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move into a dark room with a nearly empty lantern; a reported grue death (-10) came from such a move.
2. Do not shuttle Gallery and East Chasm, or Cellar and East Chasm, without a new objective; these repeats gave no score.
3. Do not keep a goal whose target room is unreached; the egg goal steered the actions for many steps without progress.
4. Do not wander the forest while the goal is the Living Room; four such moves gave no score.
5. Do not open the mailbox and leave without reading the leaflet; the clue went unused.
6. Do not assume a hostile occupant makes an exit lethal; the troll only blocked Cellar north, per the corrected note.
7. Do not take and drop lunch, pepper, garlic or sack without a stated reason; none produced score.
8. Do not trust the planner's 'previously productive' gain figures over the latest observation; verify score, location and light first.
