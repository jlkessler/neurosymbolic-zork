LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it early.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on that move.
3. Run 'inventory' in the Kitchen or Living Room before descending, and confirm a light source is carried; only then use the trap door.
4. On arriving in pitch black, the first action must be to light a carried lamp. Make no movement commands until the text shows light.
5. Take plausible portable objects in the house before going underground; this run went down empty-handed. Verify any score change on the score line.
6. Trust only recorded score events. Ignore replayed 'take egg' and 'take bar' goals when their locations are unreachable.
7. Before each move, check that the command matches the stated plan and exits. Avoid contradictions like planning north and sending south.
8. After two visits to the same room pair, pick an untried exit or use look/examine instead of reversing.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without a lit light source. Moving south in the dark Cellar led to grue death and -10.
2. Do not descend the trap door empty-handed or without checking inventory. This run reached the Cellar with nothing.
3. Never 'give' items to the troll. A previous 'give troll manual' ended in decapitation and -10.
4. Do not follow stale 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals; they previously produced loops and were irrelevant to the actual route here.
5. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. Over 100 such moves after +4 earlier gave no new score.
6. Do not re-enter the Troll room unprepared. Repeat visits gave no score or information; attack only with a wielded weapon.
7. Do not skip cheap information: open the mailbox but also read the leaflet instead of dropping or taking it as a probe.
8. Do not wander underground carrying treasure with the route home unverified. The earlier painting was never banked.
