LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10 again. Do this first, before any side exploration.
2. Take lantern, push rug, open trap, 'down' recorded Cellar +25. Light the lantern on arrival and confirm it is lit.
3. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Keep it, carry it to the Living Room trophy case, and verify the deposit score.
4. Judge actions only by the observed score afterwards. Ignore planner estimates such as +800, +45 or the 'take bar' +20.
5. Retreating south from the Troll room after one visit avoided harm. Leave at once when a hostile appears, then change plan.
6. Check inventory for sword and lantern before entering any danger room, and confirm the lantern is lit before dark areas.
7. When a goal stalls, test an untried exit or object: Kitchen sack and bottle, Attic stairs, the grating found by taking leaves.
8. After a room is visited three times, or a room pair repeats without score, leave and choose an unvisited frontier.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'jump' at East Chasm or similar edges. It recorded a -10 death. Do not use risky verbs as a last-resort exploration move.
2. Do not 'put down' a scored treasure away from the trophy case. The painting was dropped twice in the Gallery and left behind.
3. Do not shuttle Cellar<->East Chasm or East Chasm<->Gallery. About 40 moves produced zero score and no new information.
4. Do not pursue the stale 'take bar' Loud Room goal. No route was verified and repeated attempts only produced cycling.
5. Do not enter the Troll room without a sword confirmed in inventory, and never wait there. An earlier run died doing so.
6. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet or North House. They scored nothing in this or earlier runs.
7. Do not stay underground looping while the Kitchen sack, bottle and Attic go unexamined. Unexplored objects beat repeated rooms.
8. Do not burn lantern turns on aimless loops. Walk purposefully to an unexplored frontier or the trophy case.
