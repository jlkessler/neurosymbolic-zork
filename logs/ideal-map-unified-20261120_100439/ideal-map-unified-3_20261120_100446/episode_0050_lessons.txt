LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window and go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Do this first and skip house-perimeter loops.
2. In the Living Room take lantern and sword before push rug, open trap, down; Cellar +25 was recorded with both in hand.
3. On pitch-black arrival, 'turn on lantern' before any move; this run it avoided the grue. Confirm light first.
4. Wield the sword and attack the troll; it died and the next east move recorded +5. Never hand items over.
5. Check each new room's objects on first entry; Loud Room 'take bar' recorded +10. Take what is visible, then move on.
6. Before reversing between two rooms, list untried exits and pick one; the Dam Lobby/Maintenance Room shuttle scored nothing.
7. Treat warnings like rising water as stop signals: leave the room immediately and do not re-enter.
8. Override stale goals ('take egg', 'take bar', repeated 'down'); trust only recorded score events and flag rooms visited 3+ times.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not push the Maintenance Room buttons; the blue button started a leak that flooded the room, and drowning cost -10 and ended the game.
2. Do not re-enter a room where water is rising; about ten returns to the Maintenance Room ended in death at neck-deep water.
3. Do not run 'return to Living Room' as a long shuttle between two rooms once the route is unknown; try other exits.
4. Do not wander West/North/South House at the start; about 12 opening moves produced zero score before the window was opened.
5. Never move in a dark room without light; dark movement in the Cellar caused grue deaths (-10) in earlier runs.
6. Never 'give' items to the troll; earlier runs ended in death. Wield the sword and attack instead.
7. Do not descend without both lantern and sword; the trap door bars behind you, so check inventory before 'down'.
8. Do not revisit the Loud Room or take items like matchbook without a plan; they produced no score after the first +10.
