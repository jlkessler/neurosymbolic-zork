LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: Kitchen +10 was recorded. Repeat this opening before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down: the 'down' move recorded Cellar +25.
3. Before descending, take the lantern, run 'inventory', and confirm it is both held and lit. The Cellar arrival text was pitch black.
4. Take the sword before going underground. The sword glowed faintly blue on Cellar arrival, which is useful hazard feedback worth reading.
5. If the text says dark or pitch black, handle light first: check inventory, light the lantern, or stop. Never take a blind exit.
6. After a room's second entry, choose a different, unvisited exit or an unused item action instead of the move just used.
7. Trust only recorded score events. Treat historical 'gain' estimates for take egg, take bar or down as unverified candidates.
8. Carry any scored item back to the Living Room and deposit it in the open case, then explore. Check the case state first.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the room is pitch black. A 'south' move from the dark Cellar was followed by a grue death (-10).
2. Do not shuttle Kitchen<->Living Room. About 25 such moves scored 0. After one revisit, use a new exit.
3. Do not go Kitchen<->Attic repeatedly. The Attic was dark on all 3 entries and each visit scored nothing.
4. Do not turn on the lantern and then leave it behind. 'turn on lantern' is not 'take lantern'; confirm with 'inventory'.
5. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals. They never produced points and caused most of the wandering.
6. Do not descend the trap door without a lit light source in hand. It crashed shut behind the Actor, so there is no retreat.
7. Do not open the trophy case with nothing to deposit. It cost a step and scored nothing.
8. Do not keep issuing moves for 20 steps without a new room or new score. Switch to an untried exit or item action.
