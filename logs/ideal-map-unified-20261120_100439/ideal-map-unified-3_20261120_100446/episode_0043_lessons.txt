LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Take the sword before descending; it was taken this episode, and arriving armed matches the rule never to enter hostile rooms unarmed.
4. Run 'inventory' before any descent and confirm both lantern and sword are carried. Do not assume an item was picked up.
5. Trust only recorded score events: Kitchen +10 and Cellar +25. Treat egg, bar and large 'down' gains as unverified.
6. Choose actions that match the room text, such as window, rug and trap door, over stale replayed goals.
7. On arriving in a room you have already entered, pick an untried exit instead of reversing the move you just made.
8. If text says dark or pitch black, stop and use 'inventory' or 'look'; act only to establish light.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move while the room is pitch black. Moving north in the dark Cellar led to grue death and a -10 penalty.
2. Do not descend without the lantern. It was never taken this episode, so the Cellar arrival left the Actor with no light.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen↔Living Room. Four entries into each room, with no new score or information, wasted about eight moves.
4. Do not chase replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals when the location is unreachable or unverified; they gave no points.
5. Do not skip 'inventory' before risky steps. The check was skipped again despite earlier lessons.
6. Do not leave the leaflet unread after taking it; its text may carry useful information.
7. Do not treat the trap door crashing shut as a cue to explore; stop and assess light and exits first.
8. Do not enter the Troll Room unarmed; no visit is recorded here, but carry the sword and check room text before any move.
