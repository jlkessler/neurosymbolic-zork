LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this early instead of wandering the mailbox, North House or forest.
2. In the Living Room, 'take lantern', 'push rug', 'turn on lantern', 'open trap', 'down' recorded Cellar +25 and a visible room description.
3. Before any descent, take the lantern, run 'inventory' to confirm it is carried and lit, and only then go down.
4. After any one-way descent, run 'look' and 'inventory' before moving. If the room is pitch black, use no exit; dark rooms are lethal.
5. Credit only recorded score changes. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and per-attempt point figures.
6. Treat planner goals as candidates. Check room, inventory and prerequisites first, and drop stale ones such as 'take egg', 'take bar' or repeated 'down'.
7. After seeing a hostile blocker in a room, leave once and try untried exits such as the Cellar's south crawlway, rather than re-entering.
8. If a room pair repeats twice with no new observation or score, switch to an untried verb, object or exit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch darkness. An earlier run's 'south' from the dark Cellar recorded a grue death (-10).
2. Do not re-enter the Troll Room once the troll is seen blocking it. Four entries ended in a knockout death (-10) with no score.
3. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge. An earlier run recorded -10 and death.
4. Do not pursue 'take bar', 'take egg' or Loud Room goals without a verified route and prerequisites. They drove the Cellar-Troll shuttle with no gain.
5. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet or North House/West House. Steps 1-4 gained nothing.
6. Do not drop or take items with a hostile in the room. The leaflet drop and take drew axe hits and gained nothing.
7. Do not assume the lantern is carried or lit. An earlier run turned it on without taking it, so check 'inventory'.
8. Do not shuttle between adjacent rooms such as Cellar-Troll or East Chasm-Gallery-Studio. Past loops burned many steps for no score.
