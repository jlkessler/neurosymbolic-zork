LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: Kitchen +10 was recorded. Repeat this opening before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down: that 'down' move recorded Cellar +25.
3. Before descending, run 'inventory' and confirm a light source is held and lit. The Cellar arrival text was pitch black.
4. If text says dark or pitch black, handle light first: check inventory, light the lantern if held, otherwise do not move.
5. Read room text and prefer visible, state-matching actions over stale historical goals; the Actor's window, rug and trap-door steps scored.
6. After a second entry to any room, pick an unvisited exit or an unused item action instead of the move just used.
7. Trust only recorded score events. Treat historical gain estimates for take egg, take bar or down as unverified candidates.
8. Look for a portable light source and any weapon in the house before going underground, and check inventory after taking them.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the room is pitch black. A move from the dark Cellar (logged as 'north') led to a grue death (-10).
2. Do not descend the trap door without a lit light source. It crashed shut behind the Actor, so there is no retreat.
3. Do not reason that a dark exit is the only direction to explore. Dark means light first, or stay put.
4. Do not shuttle Kitchen<->Living Room. A prior episode lost about 25 moves to it with 0 score. Use a new exit after one revisit.
5. Do not go Kitchen<->Attic repeatedly. The Attic was dark on all 3 prior entries and every visit scored nothing.
6. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals. They never produced points in recorded events and caused wandering.
7. Do not leave the leaflet or other starting items unread and unused without a reason; check for hints before risky descents.
8. Do not make exploratory detours like North House when the score route is known, unless the exits are untried and safe.
