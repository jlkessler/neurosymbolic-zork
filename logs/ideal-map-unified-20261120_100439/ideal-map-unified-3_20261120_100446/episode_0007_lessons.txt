LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; the recorded Kitchen +10 followed that move.
2. Push the rug and open the trap door before descending; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Secure a light source and check lantern fuel before descending or entering any dark room.
4. Use the reopenable trap door and the verified Studio-up-Kitchen route as the safe return path.
5. Retire a goal after one failed attempt, such as 'take egg' when the tree was never reached.
6. Read items you take, such as the leaflet, before dropping them or moving on.
7. Use room ids to tell same-named rooms apart, and note the exits seen in each.
8. Log score before and after each action, and credit only the exact recorded event.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move in a dark room without light or enough fuel; both recorded grue deaths (-10) came from such moves.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm or Gallery/Studio without a new objective; about 300 such steps gave no score.
3. Do not keep a goal whose target room is unreached; the egg goal steered actions without progress.
4. Do not wander the forest while the stated goal is the Living Room; those moves scored nothing.
5. Do not drop inventory items in the dark without a reason; dropping the leaflet helped nothing.
6. Do not treat the troll room as lethal; the troll only blocks Cellar north.
7. Do not trust the planner's 'previously productive' gain figures over the latest observation; verify score, location and light first.
8. Do not descend with unfinished prerequisites; the trap door shuts behind you and leaves you in the dark.
