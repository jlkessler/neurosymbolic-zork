LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Do it first, skipping forest detours.
2. In the Living Room, take the sword, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down; Cellar +25 was recorded on descent.
3. Take the lantern in the Living Room before opening the trap door; the Cellar is pitch black and the door shuts behind you.
4. Run 'inventory' before descending and confirm both lantern and sword are carried; the last run descended with the sword only.
5. On pitch-black arrival, make 'turn on lantern' the very first action; use no movement command until light is confirmed.
6. Read the leaflet after taking it; it was taken last run but never read, so its information went unused.
7. Override stale 'take egg', 'take bar' and repeated 'down' goals when unreachable or already used; trust only recorded score events.
8. After two visits to a room, try an untried exit, such as the Kitchen's staircase, instead of reversing; flag 3+ visits.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without light; moving in the dark Cellar (north last run, south earlier) led to grue death and -10.
2. Never 'give' items to the troll; earlier runs ended in death and -10. Wield the sword and attack instead.
3. Do not spend opening moves looping Forest, Forest Path and Clearings; six such moves earlier gave no score.
4. Do not descend carrying only a sword; check for the lantern first, since descending without light proved fatal.
5. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals; they produced no observed score this run and pointed to unreachable places.
6. Do not shuttle between Cellar and Troll rooms; any room visited 3+ times is overvisited, so change plan.
7. Do not enter the Troll room unarmed or without a wielded weapon; do not drop and retake items beside a hostile troll.
8. Do not take items like the leaflet and never read them; carrying unread information wastes free hints.
