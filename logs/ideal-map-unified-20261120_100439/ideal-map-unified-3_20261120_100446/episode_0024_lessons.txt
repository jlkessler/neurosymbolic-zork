LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move.
2. Take the lantern, open the trap door and go down for the recorded Cellar +25; light the lantern before exploring.
3. Cellar south to East Chasm, east to Gallery; 'take art' recorded +4 there.
4. With art in hand: Gallery, north to Studio, UP to Kitchen, west to Living Room, 'put painting in case' (+6 recorded).
5. At Up a Tree, 'take egg' recorded +5; at the Living Room, 'put egg in case' recorded +5.
6. Keep the lantern lit and carried underground; check light before every move and stop if it dims or goes out.
7. Credit only recorded score events; compare the score after every action and ignore planner gain estimates.
8. When two objectives stall in a row, take one untried exit or head for the Living Room instead of re-walking known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw or pour water at the lantern; it smashed, the light went out, and the grue killed the Actor (-10).
2. Never move while in darkness; a grue devoured the Actor on a north move from East Chasm after the lamp broke.
3. Ignore the stale 'take bar' Loud Room goal; it was never reached and fed about 200 steps of looping.
4. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio without a new observation; none of those loops scored.
5. Do not 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge; an earlier lesson recorded it as lethal, so don't test it.
6. Avoid the Troll room north of the Cellar; the Actor entered and left alive three times with no gain, and the troll blocks all exits.
7. Do not open/close the bottle or drop/take lunch, pepper or manual repeatedly; every cycle scored 0 and wasted moves.
8. Do not set return-route goals through the trap door; it crashes shut behind you. Use Studio UP to Kitchen instead.
