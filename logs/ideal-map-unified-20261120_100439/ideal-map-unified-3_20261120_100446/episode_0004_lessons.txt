LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window early, then go west into the Kitchen (+10); this was only found after roughly 200 low-value steps.
2. Light the lantern and carry it before descending; this episode's trap-door descent with light scored +25 in the Cellar.
3. From the Kitchen, go west to the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down.
4. Check the score after each action and record which exact action produced a gain, such as taking the egg for +5.
5. Break a no-score streak by switching to untried verbs on visible objects, such as open, examine or take, not more movement.
6. After 10 steps with no score change, list untested features of the current room before leaving it.
7. Use room ids to tell same-named rooms apart, such as Clearing #74 and #143, before treating a destination as new.
8. Before entering a room newly reached from the Cellar, read its exits and objects, and weigh the risk before committing.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move north from the Cellar again without a plan; the −10 terminal event followed that move into a Troll Room with a troll.
2. Do not shuttle Behind House and Clearing; east-west repeated many times and produced no score or new room.
3. Do not shuttle West House and North House or South House; these loops repeated and consumed many steps.
4. Do not cycle Clearing, Forest, Forest Path and 'go around forest' or 'go around trees'; these only revisit known rooms.
5. Do not toggle the mailbox open and closed repeatedly; every repeat produced no score or new route.
6. Do not repeatedly take, drop or put the egg in the mailbox or nest; these gave no score and the egg ended up damaged.
7. Do not keep chasing a 'previously productive' action by pure navigation when the prerequisite action, such as opening the window, has not been done.
8. Do not assume unsupported causes or points, such as grue deaths, sword blame or trophy-case scores, without text evidence from this episode.
