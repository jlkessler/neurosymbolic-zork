LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat this opening before side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, then go down: 'down' recorded Cellar +25.
3. Before descending, run 'inventory' and confirm the lantern is carried; after arriving in darkness, turn it on immediately, as done this episode.
4. If text says dark or pitch black, do not move until the light source is confirmed on, via inventory or look.
5. Prefer actions that match the room text, such as window, rug and trap door, over stale replayed goals.
6. On re-entering a room, choose an unvisited exit instead of the move just used. Cellar, Troll and East Chasm were all revisited.
7. Trust only recorded score events: Kitchen +10 and Cellar +25. Treat egg, bar and large 'down' gain estimates as unverified.
8. When a room's text names a hazard, such as a troll blocking all passages, leave and record it as dangerous.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not re-enter the Troll room. Four visits brought axe hits, and the last one knocked the Actor out with a -10 terminal penalty.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar<->Troll<->East Chasm. Repeating a move with no score and no new information wasted about ten steps.
3. Do not follow replayed 'take egg' or 'take bar' goals when their locations are unreachable or unverified. They produced no points.
4. Do not spend turns on actions like dropping the leaflet while taking damage. Retreat or change approach, since it did not stop the axe hit.
5. Do not leave the leaflet unread or skip 'inventory' before a risky descent. Neither check was done despite the earlier lessons.
6. Do not wander the forest for three moves when the Behind House route is already known.
7. Do not treat 'lantern is on' as proof that exploration is safe. Check room text for hostile creatures before each move.
8. Do not keep moving after repeated damage or warning text. Stop and reassess exits before trying the same direction again.
