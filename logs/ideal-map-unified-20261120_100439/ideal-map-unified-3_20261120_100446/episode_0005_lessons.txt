LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Check the lantern's remaining fuel before each move once it is lit, and return to lit, known rooms before it runs out.
2. When a recorded goal's location is unreachable or unmapped, drop it after one failed attempt instead of re-selecting it.
3. Open the Behind House window before trying to enter it; the recorded Kitchen +10 followed 'west' once the window was open.
4. Open the trap door and carry a lit lantern before descending; the recorded Cellar +25 followed a descent with light.
5. Retreat to the previous room after seeing a hostile occupant; the Actor survived two Troll visits that way.
6. Break a no-score streak of 10 steps by examining objects or testing untried verbs, not by repeating known moves.
7. Use room ids to tell same-named rooms apart before treating a destination as new.
8. Credit a score gain only to the exact action and observation that recorded it, and log it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not make a dark-room move such as west from East Chasm with a nearly empty lantern; this ended in the grue death (-10).
2. Do not loop Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio; dozens of repeats produced no score and no new room.
3. Do not re-select the 'take egg at Up a Tree' goal repeatedly when the route has stalled and the prerequisites are unmet.
4. Do not keep taking, dropping and rearranging lunch, pepper, garlic and sack without a stated reason; these gave no score.
5. Do not toggle between Kitchen and Attic or Kitchen and Living Room as pure movement when nothing changed.
6. Do not blame an untested cause, such as a grue or troll, unless this episode's text shows it.
7. Do not give items to the thief or take scenery such as the chimney without a purpose; neither produced score.
8. Do not trust summaries over the room text; verify score, location and light from the latest observation before choosing a goal.
