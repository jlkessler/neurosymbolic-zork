LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move.
2. Take the lantern and light it before opening the trap door and going down; Cellar +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Cellar south to East Chasm, east to Gallery; 'take art' recorded +4 there.
4. With art in hand: Gallery, north to Studio, UP to Kitchen, west to Living Room, then 'put painting in case' (+6 recorded).
5. At Up a Tree, 'take egg' recorded +5; at the Living Room, 'put egg in case' directly recorded +5.
6. Check light before every move underground; if the lantern is missing, dim or out, stay put and do not move.
7. Credit only recorded score events; compare the score after every action and ignore planner gain estimates.
8. After banking the egg, try one untried exit at a time near Living Room, Kitchen or Attic, only with a lit lantern.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw things at the lantern; water thrown at it smashed it, the light went out, and a grue killed the Actor.
2. Never move in darkness: a grue killed the Actor on the first move south from the pitch-black Cellar (-10).
3. Treat 'take bar' (Loud Room) as stale and unreachable; it fed 150+ steps of looping and a fatal move.
4. Do not descend the trap door without a lit lantern; it crashes shut behind you and the Cellar is pitch black.
5. Do not set return-route goals pointing at the trap door; use Studio UP to Kitchen instead.
6. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio without a new observation; none of those loops scored.
7. Avoid the north exit from the Cellar to the Troll room; it is dangerous and gave no gain.
8. Do not put down, open case and take the egg before depositing; use 'put egg in case' directly.
