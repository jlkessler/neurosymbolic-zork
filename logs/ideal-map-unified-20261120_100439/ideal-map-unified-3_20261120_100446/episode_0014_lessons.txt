================================

2. Take and light the lantern before going down; the Cellar +25 was recorded and this kept the Cellar lit.
3. Verified edges: Behind House west to Kitchen, Kitchen west to Living Room, Living Room down to Cellar (trap door then shuts).
4. Check inventory just before descending: the trap door shuts behind you, so confirm light and a weapon are held first.
5. Read score, location and observation after every action, and credit only recorded score events; this death recorded -10.
6. State a concrete purpose before each untested action, and verify the score change afterward.
7. When a room's observation shows a threat, treat it as new evidence and choose a safer action than the planner's goal.
8. Bank treasures in the Living Room trophy case before exploring further, and verify the score change on deposit.

4. Do not enter the Troll room without checking that you hold a weapon; this run went in unarmed and died.
5. Do not open with the west/east/go-around-forest loop; it cost 4 steps and scored nothing.
6. Do not chase stale planner goals ('take egg', 'take bar', Living Room 'down') without a verified route and matching observation.
7. Do not trust planner gain figures (+300, +15, +20); the observed Cellar event was only +25.
8. Do not rely on old notes about Maintenance buttons or the dam area; they were not re-observed this run.
