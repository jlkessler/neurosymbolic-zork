LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move.
2. Take the lantern, open the trap door and go down for the recorded Cellar +25; light the lantern in the dark.
3. Verified route to Gallery: Cellar south to East Chasm, east to Gallery. 'take art' there recorded +4.
4. With a treasure in hand, try Studio 'up' toward Kitchen, then west to Living Room, and use the trophy case.
5. When the planner says a route is blocked, test each untried exit in the current room once, including up and down.
6. Keep a per-room list of tried exits; when every exit is exhausted, change area instead of re-walking known rooms.
7. Credit only recorded score events; after every action compare the score and ignore planner gain estimates.
8. After depositing a treasure, explore only genuinely unvisited exits and examine one specific object per new room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never use 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge; it killed the Actor (-10) and ended the run.
2. Avoid the Troll room north of the Cellar; this run entered and left it alive, but the risk is unproven and earlier lessons record a death there.
3. Ignore stale hints 'take bar', 'take egg' and 'down'; they scored nothing and drove about 150 steps of looping.
4. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio without a new observation; none of those loops scored.
5. Do not drop and re-take the art, painting, manual or leaflet; each cycle wasted moves and scored 0.
6. Do not 'wait' in the Cellar; a wandering figure appeared and nothing was gained.
7. Do not trust planner gain figures such as +20; the observed events were +10, +25, +4 and -10.
8. Do not take items like the manual or leaflet without a stated purpose; they cost moves and added no score.
