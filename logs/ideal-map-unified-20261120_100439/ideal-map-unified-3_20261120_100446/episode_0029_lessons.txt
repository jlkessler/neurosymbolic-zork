LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this early rather than wandering the North House or forest.
2. In the Living Room, push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded Cellar +25. The trap door then shut and was barred behind us.
3. Before 'down', check inventory for a working light source. If you have none, find one first, because the Cellar was pitch black.
4. Credit only recorded score changes. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and points-per-attempt figures.
5. Bank treasure in the Living Room trophy case before descending, and take portable valuables when seen. An earlier run recorded 'take art' +4.
6. After a one-way descent, run 'look' and 'inventory' first. Then test each untried exit once and record dead ends.
7. If a room pair repeats three times with no new observation, switch to an untried verb, object or exit.
8. Treat planner goals as candidates. Check the current observation and prerequisites before following one, and drop goals that are stale or unreachable.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any edge. An earlier run recorded -10 and death.
2. Never move blindly in pitch darkness. 'south' from the unlit Cellar recorded a grue death, -10, and ended this run at 25.
3. Do not pursue 'take bar'/Loud Room. It was a stale planner goal and unreachable in practice. Treat it as unavailable.
4. Do not descend without a light source, a banked treasure, or a verified route home. This run's home return was never verified.
5. Do not shuttle East Chasm, Cellar, Gallery and Studio. An earlier run spent about 35-45 steps there with no score after the +4.
6. Do not waste opening steps on the mailbox and the North House/Forest Path/Clearing loop. Steps 1-6 gained nothing.
7. Do not drop or put down a carried treasure while the way home is unknown.
8. When exits look exhausted, do not assume one is 'the only viable option'. Try look, inventory, examine or an untried object first.
