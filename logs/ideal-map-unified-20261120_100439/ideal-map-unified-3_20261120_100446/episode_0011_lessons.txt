LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen +10 was recorded on that move again this episode.
2. Take lantern, push rug, open trap door, go down; Cellar +25 was recorded. Light the lantern immediately on arrival.
3. Before the Troll room, check inventory for a weapon; an earlier run killed the troll with the sword, so fetch it first.
4. Read score and location after every action; credit only score events that are recorded exactly.
5. Keep a per-room list of exits tried, and test one untried exit instead of repeating a blocked move.
6. Treat hostile blockers, darkness and rising water as stop signals: leave, rethink, and prepare before re-engaging.
7. Earlier run: after taking treasure, return along a confirmed route to the Living Room trophy case and verify the score.
8. Override stale planner goals when the current room and inventory do not match; state a purpose for each untested action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll room unarmed again; the troll knocked the Actor out and the run ended with -10 (final 25).
2. Do not shuttle Cellar and Troll; five entries gained nothing, and the last one was fatal.
3. Do not chase planner goals like 'take egg' or 'take bar' without a verified route; Up a Tree was never reached.
4. Do not open with mailbox and forest loops; about 5 early moves scored nothing.
5. Do not shuffle Kitchen and Living Room with nothing to pick up; about 4 moves were wasted.
6. Do not trust planner 'previously productive' gain figures (+225, +15); this episode's Cellar event was only +25.
7. Do not push Maintenance Room buttons: an earlier run's blue button started a flood and the Actor drowned (-10, final 40).
8. Do not loop Chasm, North-South Passage, Round Room, Loud Room and Deep Canyon, or carry treasure without a verified route home.
