LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this first, before any side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, take sword and lantern, check 'inventory', then 'down'. That 'down' recorded Cellar +25.
3. 'take art' in the Gallery recorded +4. Keep any scored treasure in inventory and do not drop it away from a verified deposit spot.
4. Turn the lantern on at Cellar arrival and confirm it is lit. Re-check light before any exit into an unlit room.
5. Retreating 'south' from the Troll room each time avoided harm. Stay out until inventory confirms a sword.
6. Set one new frontier per goal. After a second entry into any room, take a different exit instead of the one just used.
7. Trust only recorded score events. Ignore historical +20 or +45 estimates that lack a verified route from your current room.
8. When text warns about light, fix it first: check the lantern, or return to a lit room. Never push on.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move into a dark room. 'north' from East Chasm with a failing lantern ended in a grue death (-10).
2. Do not shuttle Cellar<->East Chasm or Gallery<->Studio. Dozens of moves there gave zero score. Pick an unvisited exit after the second visit.
3. Do not follow replayed 'take bar' or 'take egg' goals. They have no verified route and produced only cycling.
4. Do not enter the Troll room without 'inventory' showing a sword. It was entered four times with no check.
5. Do not 'wait' or toggle the bottle while lost in the dark. Those actions cost steps and fixed nothing about the light.
6. Do not give items to the thief or shuffle the manual and bottle. Those actions scored nothing.
7. Never use 'jump' or other risky verbs at edges or in dark places. An earlier run recorded a death that way.
8. Do not spend more than a few steps without new information. Over 300 steps after the +4 gained nothing; try a fresh frontier early.
