LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this first, before any side exploration.
2. Take lantern, push rug, light lantern, open trap, then 'down' recorded Cellar +25. Confirm a lit lantern in inventory before descending.
3. Treasures scored on pickup and again on deposit: painting +4/+6, egg +5/+5. Carry finds to the Living Room case and verify the score.
4. Studio 'up' led straight to the Kitchen, a confirmed shortcut home from the Gallery. Use it instead of backtracking through the Cellar.
5. Credit only observed score changes. Ignore planner point estimates such as the 'take bar' +20 and judge each action by the score after it.
6. Exploring objects can reveal exits: 'take leaves' exposed a grating in a clearing. Test that grating and other untried exits first.
7. Check lantern fuel when it warns of dimness, and return to the lit surface before it fails.
8. After two failed attempts at a goal, or one room pair repeating without a score, switch to an untried exit, object or area.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never linger in the Troll room. 'wait' there recorded a -10 death. After the first axe hit, leave south at once and do not return.
2. Do not enter the Troll room without a sword confirmed in inventory. Repeated visits gained nothing and cost health until death.
3. Do not follow the stale 'take bar' Loud Room goal. About 300 steps chasing it scored zero and no route was verified.
4. Do not shuttle Cellar<->East Chasm, Gallery<->Studio or Clearing<->Forest Path. Each pair repeated many times with zero score or new information.
5. Do not take or drop junk (manual, leaves) as exploration, especially in danger. It wasted turns and invited axe hits.
6. Do not wait or idle in a room where the last observation showed combat. Move away or change approach.
7. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet or North House. They gave no score in this or earlier runs.
8. Do not burn lantern turns on aimless loops. Walk with a purpose to an unexplored frontier or the trophy case.
