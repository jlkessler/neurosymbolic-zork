LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded Kitchen +10. Do this early, before any side exploration.
2. In the Living Room, take lantern, push rug, light lantern, open trap, 'down' recorded Cellar +25. Check 'inventory' for a lit lantern first.
3. Treasures scored only when deposited: 'take art' +4 then 'put painting in case' +6. Carry finds to the Living Room case and verify the score.
4. Outdoor exploration scored: 'take egg' at Up a Tree gave +5 and 'put egg in case' +5. Explore surface frontiers when underground stalls.
5. Credit only recorded score changes. Compare the score after each action and ignore planner gain estimates and per-attempt point figures.
6. When the lantern warns it is dim or nearly out, stop underground wandering and head back to a lit area at once.
7. Treat planner goals as candidates. Check room, inventory and prerequisites, and drop a goal after two failed attempts.
8. If a room pair repeats twice with no new observation or score, switch to an untried verb, object, exit or area.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch darkness. A grue death (-10) at East Chasm followed the lantern nearly running out, and an earlier run died the same way.
2. Do not chase the stale 'take bar' Loud Room goal. About 300 steps of Gallery-Studio-East Chasm-Cellar cycling gained nothing.
3. Do not shuttle Gallery<->Studio or East Chasm<->Cellar. Each pair repeated more than 20 times with zero score.
4. Do not enter the Troll Room unless the sword is carried and confirmed by inventory. The troll was seen blocking it and it is lethal.
5. Do not spend opening steps on the mailbox, leaflet or North House. They gained nothing in earlier runs.
6. Do not waste lantern turns on aimless loops. The lantern ran down, which left the Gallery east exit blocked for lack of light.
7. Do not drop or take junk (manual, lunch, pepper) as 'exploration'. These actions gave no score and no new information.
8. Do not accept a stalled goal re-issued by the planner. If a goal stalls twice, pick a new frontier instead.
