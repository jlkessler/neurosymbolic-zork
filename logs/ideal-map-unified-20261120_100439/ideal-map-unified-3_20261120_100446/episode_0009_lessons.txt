LESSONS LEARNED
================================

2. Take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, then 'down'; Cellar +25 was recorded. Light the lantern immediately below.
3. 'take art' at the Gallery recorded +4; carry it north to the Studio, up to the Kitchen, west to the Living Room.
4. In the Living Room, use 'put painting in trophy case' rather than 'put down'; an earlier episode recorded +6, so verify the score.
5. Read score and location after each action and credit only exactly recorded score events.
6. Retire a goal after one failed attempt or when its target room is unreached, such as 'take egg'.
7. When the score stalls, test one untried exit or object at a time instead of re-walking mapped rooms.
8. Examine items found along the way (sack, bottle, manual, attic contents) before leaving a room, but verify they matter before carrying them.

4. Do not keep the 'take egg' goal while Up a Tree is unreached; it recurred in many planner goals without progress.
5. Do not open with forest loops (west, east, around forest); about 5 early moves scored nothing.
6. Do not treat the Troll room as safe or as proven lethal: one visit was survived, so enter only deliberately, armed or with a plan.
7. Do not trust planner 'previously productive' gain figures over the latest observation; verify score and location.
8. Do not re-descend empty-handed or repeat a stalled move; first bank carried valuables and choose an untested action.
