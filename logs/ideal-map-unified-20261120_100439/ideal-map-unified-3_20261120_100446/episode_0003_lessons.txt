LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Use the verified route: Behind House, open window, west to Kitchen (+10), Living Room, push rug, open trap door, down (+25).
2. Reach the Kitchen directly; this episode scored first at step 16 after six wasted moves between West House and North House.
3. Take the egg in Up a Tree (+5) and put it in the open trophy case (+2); check the score after each.
4. Secure a light source before descending; the Cellar was pitch black and the trap door crashed shut behind the Actor.
5. In a dark room, do not guess exits; light it, or take the one known safe action, before any frontier move.
6. After descending, explore the untried exits (Gallery, East Chasm, Cellar crawlway) one at a time once light is available.
7. Check room ids to tell same-named rooms apart (Clearing #74 vs #143) before calling a destination new.
8. After 20 steps with no score or new room, switch region or verb type instead of repeating moves.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move into unexplored exits in a pitch-black room; going south from the dark Cellar caused grue death and a −10 penalty.
2. Do not shuttle West House and North House; three round trips yielded nothing new and cost six steps.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen and Living Room without a new action; four such moves passed with zero score.
4. Do not take the sword or any item in the Troll room with the troll awake; that killed the Actor earlier. Treat it as high risk.
5. Do not waste turns in dark rooms on inventory actions like dropping the leaflet; they gain nothing while a grue threat exists.
6. Do not cycle Clearing, Forest and Forest Path or use 'go around forest'; it only revisits known rooms.
7. Do not repeat open/close toggles on the egg, nest, window or mailbox; they gave no score or new route.
8. Do not descend the trap door unprepared; it shut behind the Actor, and no return route home was verified.
