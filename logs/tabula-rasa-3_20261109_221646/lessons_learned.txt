LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and go west: +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. Take and light the lantern and sword in the Living Room before going down; +25 was recorded on entering the Cellar.
3. Take the art in the Gallery (+4), carry it to the Living Room, and put painting in case (+6 recorded).
4. After each score event, run look and inventory; set score goals only for items you hold or can see.
5. Once equipped and scored, spend steps on unexplored exits (Attic, forest, Studio and East Chasm side exits), not the known route.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go down or move in a dark room without a lit lantern: a grue killed the Actor (-10) after it moved south from the Cellar.
2. Never go north from the Cellar: it leads to the Troll Room, which is lethal.
3. Don't pursue 'put egg in case' without holding an egg, and don't repeat 'take art'; neither can score again.
4. Don't repeat the Living Room, Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio, Kitchen circuit: about 100 steps scored 0. After three repeats, take a new exit.
5. Don't trust planner score claims ('+80/+200', or '+4 per egg'); real gains were +10, +25, +4 and +6. Override stale navigation goals when the item isn't held.
