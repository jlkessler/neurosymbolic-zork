Historian episode review — episode 10
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's best work was early: the recorded score events (+10 entering the Kitchen, +25 entering the Cellar, +4 taking art in the Gallery, +6 putting the painting in the case) total 45. Its two Actor-chosen moves, 'open trap' and 'down', worked every time. After roughly step 100 the run became a zero-score loop (Living Room, Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio, Kitchen, Living Room). The symbolic navigator, chasing a stale 'put egg in case' goal, drove this loop, and the Actor only got two steps per cycle before control returned to it. No egg was ever held, and the +4 and +6 came from art and painting, not eggs. The Actor kept descending without checking inventory or the light. Its notes claimed a lit lantern, which the evidence does not confirm. Next time: verify light and inventory, then leave the loop for unexplored exits.
