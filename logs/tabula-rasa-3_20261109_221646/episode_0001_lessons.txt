LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Check trees and containers early; taking the egg from the tree produced a recorded +5 score event.
2. Open the trophy case and deposit treasure, then confirm the recorded score change rather than assuming it.
3. Opening the window and entering the house by it produced a recorded +10 on the move west into the Kitchen.
4. Move the rug, open the trap door, and descend; this chain produced a recorded +25 on the Cellar descent.
5. Read each observation for hints before acting; room text and item descriptions guided the successful discoveries.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't repeat up/down or east/west loops without new information; the tree and Clearing/Behind House shuffles wasted many steps.
2. Don't use malformed commands like 'take on egg'; the egg broke and the damage was avoidable.
3. Don't remove banked treasure from the trophy case without reason; taking the egg out cost -2 and gained nothing.
4. Don't explore dark underground rooms blindly; moving north then east from the Cellar ended in death and a -10 penalty.
5. Don't continue into hostile rooms without verifying a return route and holding a working light; Living Room return was never verified.
