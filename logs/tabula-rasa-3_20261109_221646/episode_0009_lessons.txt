LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and go west; +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. Push the rug, open the trap door, then 'down' from the Living Room: +25 was recorded on entering the Cellar.
3. The door shuts behind you after descending, so settle your light and safety plan before going down.
4. After each scoring move, run look and inventory; pursue only goals involving items you actually hold or can see.
5. Spend spare steps on unexplored frontiers: Attic upstairs, forest exits, East Chasm's other directions, and Studio's other exits.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room: 'south' from the Cellar in the dark let a grue kill you (-10, episode ended).
2. Never go north from the Cellar: it leads to the Troll Room, which is lethal.
3. Don't pursue 'put egg in case' without holding an egg, and don't repeat 'take art' since the art was already taken; both scored 0.
4. Don't shuttle Studio and Gallery; the circuit yielded 0 points for ~180 steps. If two rooms repeat three times, choose a new exit.
5. Don't trust the stored return route as safe; the 'south' step from the Cellar was fatal. Verify light first and probe one step at a time.
