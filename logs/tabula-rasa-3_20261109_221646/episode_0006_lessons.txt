LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending; this log showed 'Taken.' then 'now on' before 'down'.
3. The +25 came on 'down' after the rug moved and the trap door opened; repeat only if prerequisites match.
4. On arriving underground, run look and inventory first to confirm light, carried items and exits before moving.
5. When a room description names a hostile creature, record it and treat that room as dangerous.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't re-enter the Troll Room; the north exit from the Cellar ended in a knockout and -10.
2. Don't oscillate between two rooms; three north/south cycles gained nothing. Inspect or test an untried exit instead.
3. Don't blame darkness without evidence; here the lantern was lit and the troll caused the death. Read the observation text.
4. Don't assume a return after descending; the trap door shut and the route to the Living Room is unverified.
5. Don't seek a return route by repeating a risky exit; try the unexplored south crawlway or examine surroundings first.
