LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and go west; +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. Repeat 'down' from the Living Room only when the trap door is open; +25 was recorded on that move, with the door crashing shut behind.
3. After arriving somewhere new, run look and inventory to check exits and carried items before choosing a move.
4. Studio 'up' leads to the Kitchen, and Kitchen 'west' to the Living Room, which verified a return route at step 71.
5. Record a score only when the log shows the event: 'take art' at the Gallery gave +4, so list new items there.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't repeat the Living Room, Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio, Kitchen circuit; it was run about 15 times for zero points.
2. Don't follow a 'put egg in case' goal without holding an egg; check inventory first, or the goal sends you on empty trips.
3. Don't reopen the trap door and descend by reflex after navigation returns you to the Living Room; first decide what you will do below.
4. Don't call a return route unverified or an exit untested without checking the map; south from the Cellar was already known to reach East Chasm.
5. Don't claim lantern, darkness or troll outcomes without matching observation text; this log shows none of them.
