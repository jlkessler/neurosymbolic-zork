LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, then enter west: the move into the Kitchen recorded +10. Confirm score on the move, not the opening.
2. Move the rug, open the trap door, descend: the Living Room 'down' recorded +25. Repeat when prerequisites match.
3. Turn on the lantern before going underground, then verify with 'inventory' or 'look' that the room is actually lit.
4. Check trees and containers early; taking the egg from the tree previously produced a recorded +5.
5. Deposit treasure in the trophy case and confirm the recorded score change rather than assuming it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move to a new room while the observation says 'pitch black'; moving north from the Cellar led to a grue death and -10.
2. Don't assume the lantern is working because 'turn on' succeeded; the Cellar still read pitch black. Verify light first.
3. Don't descend without planning a return: the trap door crashed shut behind the player and no route to the Living Room was verified.
4. Don't loop up/down or east/west without new information; earlier tree and Clearing/Behind House shuffles wasted many steps.
5. Don't take banked treasure out of the trophy case, and avoid malformed commands like 'take on egg'; both cost points before.
