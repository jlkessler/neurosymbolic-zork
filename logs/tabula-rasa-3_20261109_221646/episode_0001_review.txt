Historian episode review — episode 1
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest decisions were taking the egg in the tree (+5, recorded), entering the kitchen via the window (+10 recorded on that move), and banking the egg in the trophy case (+2 recorded on deposit). It also found the rug, trap door and cellar (+25 recorded on the descent). Total was 32 after a final -10 penalty at death. The score log appears to double-count the +2 deposit, so treat totals with caution.

Ineffective behavior: repeated up/down tree loops, and the Clearing/Behind House oscillation. Pushing the egg into a nest and re-taking it yielded nothing, and a stray 'take on egg' broke the egg. Taking the egg back out of the case cost -2, then the redeposit regained +2. The worst decision was entering the Troll room, then moving east, which ended in death. The Actor also took the sword and lantern but never confirmed it held a light. Advice: avoid blind exits in the dark underground, stay near the verified Living Room route, and check inventory before descending.
