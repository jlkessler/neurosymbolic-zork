LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. Record +25 came on 'down' after the rug and trap door were opened; repeat only if prerequisites match.
3. Before 'down', look in the Living Room, take useful items, and run inventory to confirm what is actually carried.
4. After lighting a light source, verify it by checking inventory and the next room's description before moving on.
5. On underground arrival, use look and inventory first; these non-moving commands are safe when the room is dark.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move when the observation says 'pitch black'; north from the Cellar led to a grue death and -10.
2. Don't descend without confirming a lit light source is in inventory; 'on' alone didn't prevent darkness.
3. Don't assume a light switched on in a room is carried; no take action appeared in this log.
4. Don't go underground assuming a return; the trap door shut and the route to the Living Room is unverified.
5. Don't treat a dark room's exits as probes; guessing directions blind ended the episode with a 25-point final score.
