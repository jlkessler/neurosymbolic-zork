LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west: the entry move recorded +10 again. Confirm the score on that move, not on the opening.
2. Push the rug, open the trap door, turn on the lantern, then go 'down': +25 was recorded on the descent. Repeat only when the prerequisites match.
3. After every arrival, read the observation and use non-moving commands (look, inventory) to diagnose before picking an exit, especially underground.
4. Check trees and containers early; an earlier episode recorded +5 for taking the egg from the tree. This episode did not retest it.
5. Deposit treasure in the trophy case and confirm the recorded score change rather than assuming it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the observation says 'pitch black'. Going north from the Cellar caused a grue death and -10. Treat every unlit move as high risk.
2. Don't treat 'The lantern is now on' as proof of light; the Cellar still read pitch black. Verify with look or inventory before and after descending.
3. Don't go underground without a verified route back to the Living Room; the trap door crashed shut and home return was never verified.
4. Don't repeat up/down or east/west shuffles without new information; earlier Forest, Clearing, and Behind House loops wasted steps.
5. Don't take banked treasure out of the trophy case, and avoid malformed commands like 'take on egg'; both cost points in earlier episodes.
