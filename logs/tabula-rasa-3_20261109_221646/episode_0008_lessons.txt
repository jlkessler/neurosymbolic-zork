LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and go west; +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen, not on opening the window.
2. With the trap door open, 'down' from the Living Room gave +25 on entering the Cellar; the door then shuts behind you.
3. Verified return loop: Cellar south, East Chasm, east Gallery, north Studio, up Kitchen, west Living Room. Use it to get home, not as a destination.
4. At the Gallery, run 'take art' (+4 recorded in an earlier episode), then carry it to the Living Room and put it in the trophy case.
5. On arriving anywhere new, run look and inventory, and only pursue goals involving items you actually hold.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar: it leads to the Troll Room, which is lethal.
2. Don't pursue 'put egg in case' without holding an egg; it earned no score and fed a 150-step loop.
3. Don't shuttle Studio and Gallery (south/north); if the same two rooms repeat three times, pick a different exit or action.
4. Don't let alternating navigation goals (Gallery vs Living Room) bounce you between routes; commit to one and run its interaction on arrival.
5. Don't rerun the scoreless circuit; try unexplored exits instead: East Chasm's other directions, the Studio, the forest, upstairs in the house.
