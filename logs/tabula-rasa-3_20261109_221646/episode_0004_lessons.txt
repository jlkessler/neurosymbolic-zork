LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west; entering the Kitchen recorded +10. Confirm the score on the move, not on the opening.
2. Push the rug, open the trap door, then go 'down': +25 was recorded on the descent. Repeat only if prerequisites match.
3. On arrival underground, run look and inventory before choosing any exit; these are non-moving and safe.
4. Verify a working light source in inventory before descending; the unlit Cellar was fatal this episode.
5. Check trees and containers early, and confirm any trophy-case deposit by the recorded score change.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move when the observation says 'pitch black'. East from the Cellar caused a grue death and -10; earlier, north did the same.
2. Don't descend without a verified light source; this run had an empty inventory and no lantern action, then a dark Cellar.
3. Don't go underground without a verified route back to the Living Room; the trap door shut and the return was never confirmed.
4. Don't ignore a return-route goal; use safe probes (look, inventory) instead of guessing exits while blind.
5. Don't repeat up/down or east/west shuffles without new information, take banked treasure out of the case, or send malformed commands like 'take on egg'.
