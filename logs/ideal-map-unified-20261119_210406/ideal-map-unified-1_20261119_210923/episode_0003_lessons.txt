LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window and enter the Kitchen; the west move there recorded +10 in this episode.
2. Push the rug to reveal the trap door and open it; both steps were safe, and the door opened as expected.
3. Before using any descent, examine the room and check inventory for a light source; descend only with a lit, working one.
4. Credit score only to actions whose recorded event shows the delta; here +25 came with 'down' into a dark Cellar, so it is unsafe to repeat.
5. Treat a 'pitch black' or grue warning as an immediate stop; reassess inventory and options before choosing any exit.
6. Take portable Kitchen items, including the bottle, and examine their contents before leaving; the sack held a lunch and garlic.
7. Keep an escape route in mind before one-way transitions; the trap door shut behind the Actor on descent.
8. Let current observations override inherited goals; drop a stale score goal when the situation, such as total darkness, makes it unsafe.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never descend through the trap door without a verified light; this run entered the Cellar in darkness and died.
2. Never take an exit in pitch black; moving north from the Cellar led directly to a grue death and a -10 event.
3. Do not chase a remote goal such as 'take egg' while the current room is dark; it caused the fatal blind move.
4. Do not repeat 'down' for its +25 expecting safe points; the gain came with a dangerous, trapping arrival.
5. Do not search a sack or take trivial items in the dark as a substitute for a plan; opening the sack and taking garlic gave no light.
6. Do not leave the Living Room area without checking it for a light source; no such search was made.
7. Do not leave the Kitchen's bottle behind without examining it; this was a missed opportunity from earlier lessons.
8. Do not treat a score-action label or goal-history gain as proof of cause; verify the recorded event and prerequisites.
