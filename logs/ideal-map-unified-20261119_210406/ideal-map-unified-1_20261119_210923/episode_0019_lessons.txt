LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, go west into Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move; do it within the first ten steps.
2. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then 'down'. The +25 was recorded on first Cellar arrival, and the door bars behind you.
3. Before descending, check inventory for sword and lantern. Lantern reported 'on' yet the Cellar was pitch black, so verify the light actually works.
4. In pitch black, fix the light or check inventory first. Never take an exit blindly; the unlit Cellar 'east' move caused the -10 death.
5. Take the egg from the tree (+5) and Gallery art (+4); both scored on pickup. Deposit in the case (+5, +6) and check score after each.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and read the observation before trusting goal text.
7. After two zero-score visits to a loop, leave for an untried exit instead of repeating the cycle.
8. If the platinum bar is visible from a prior echo, try 'take bar' once with free hands before moving on.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move through exits in pitch darkness. East from the dark Cellar triggered a grue death and the -10 penalty.
2. Do not trust the stale 'up +25' goal. The +25 came from Living Room 'down'; Kitchen-to-Attic 'up' scored 0 three times.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen/Attic/Living Room or earlier loops (Chasm/Passage, Loud Room/Canyon). Dozens of zero-score moves burn the step budget.
4. Do not attack or fight the thief. Attacking him in the Dome Room was fatal in an earlier episode.
5. Do not use 'wait' or vague commands like 'push all' near a visible armed character. React to the threat or leave.
6. Do not descend the trap door unprepared; it bars behind you. Confirm light, weapon and a plan before committing.
7. Do not open the trophy case empty-handed or make low-value detours (sack, guidebook, Maintenance buttons) while treasure and exits remain unexplored.
8. Do not enter Troll Room or Dome Room without a survival plan. Prefer unexplored exits and bank treasure rather than wandering.
