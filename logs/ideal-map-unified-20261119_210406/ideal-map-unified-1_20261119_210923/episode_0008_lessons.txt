LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; Kitchen entry recorded +10, and another west reaches the Living Room.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending; if you arrive in darkness, light it as your very next action.
3. Push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded +25. The door shuts behind you but 'open trap' worked again from the Living Room.
4. Take the Gallery art (+4), then bank it: Studio up, Kitchen west, open case, 'put painting in case' recorded +6.
5. Use recorded routes (Studio up to Kitchen, west to Living Room) to bank valuables immediately instead of detouring while carrying them.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain; check the score response after each transition.
7. Treat observations like 'blocks all passages', pitch black or chasm edges as danger cues and change plan on first sight.
8. When stalled, use look or inventory, then try untried exits in Gallery, Studio or Cellar rather than revisiting known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; the fourth unarmed entry ended in a knockout, recorded -10 and episode end.
2. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; the previous run died from it for -10.
3. Do not shuttle among Cellar, Troll Room and East Chasm; about 10 steps this run gave 0 score. Change plan after one revisit.
4. Do not repeatedly drop and retake the same item; after the first +4, further art handling gave 0.
5. Do not let a stale remote goal like 'take egg at Up a Tree' drive moves while underground; it produced no progress.
6. After banking treasure, do not re-descend without a specific untried exit or objective; the aimless descent at step 33 led straight to death.
7. Do not descend without a lit light source or move in pitch black; a grue is a known death risk.
8. Do not spend early turns on mailbox or other low-value detours; the mailbox gave 0 score and nothing was taken.
