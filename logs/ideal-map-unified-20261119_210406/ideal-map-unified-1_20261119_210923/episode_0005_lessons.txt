LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window and enter west; the Kitchen entry recorded +10 and gave access to the Living Room.
2. Take the lantern, then turn it on; this run's order was take → 'Taken' → on → 'now on'.
3. Run 'inventory' immediately before any descent to confirm the lantern is carried and lit, instead of relying on the action log.
4. Push the rug and open the trap door before descending; both gave clear confirmations.
5. After a hostile hit such as 'The axe gets you', retreat to the previous safe room immediately.
6. Read each observation's danger cues (blocking creature, weapon, damage) before choosing the next exit.
7. Treat 'down' +25 as one-time score already earned; afterward prioritize survival and new frontiers over replaying it.
8. Take portable utility items such as the bottle, but use them only for a stated purpose and confirm their contents.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not re-enter the Troll room after seeing a blocking creature; north/south shuttling there gave 0 score and ended in a lethal attack.
2. Do not 'give' items to a hostile creature while it is attacking; here it preceded a knockout and -10.
3. Do not drop inventory items during an active attack; 'put down bottle' wasted a turn and the axe hit anyway.
4. Do not follow stale goals like 'take egg at Up a Tree' while in a different underground area; check location first.
5. Never move in pitch black; verify light via inventory or the room description first and stop if darkness appears.
6. Do not claim 'turn on' without a prior 'take'; confirm the item is carried first, and re-verify in inventory.
7. Do not treat a prior +25 or total gain as proof that 'down' is safe; check the current observation.
8. Do not open containers or fiddle with items in a dangerous underground room without a purpose or an escape plan.
