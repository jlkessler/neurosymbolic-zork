LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Head to Behind House, open the window, then go west into the Kitchen at once; prior runs recorded +10 on Kitchen entry. This run reached the open window by step 5.
2. Take the lantern from the Living Room before any descent, and light it before moving in dark areas.
3. Push rug, open trap door, go down: +25 was recorded on Cellar arrival earlier. Confirm the score from that observation, not from the goal text.
4. If an observation says dark, pitch black, or grue, make your very next action lighting the lamp. Do not move first.
5. After opening the window, enter immediately. If the game blocks you, use look or inventory before trying untried exits.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain; check the score line after every transition.
7. Bank valuables in the trophy case by the known route before exploring further; earlier runs recorded Gallery art +4 and trophy case +6.
8. Treat aggregate goal text such as '+225 over attempts' as a hint only; check prerequisites against the current observation before repeating an action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in darkness: 'south' from the Cellar with no lit light ended in grue death, recorded -10.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; an earlier run ended in a knockout, recorded -10 and episode end.
3. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; an earlier run died from it.
4. Do not wander Forest, Forest Path and Clearing early; one run recorded 35 wasted steps there with no score before the window.
5. Ignore stale 'take egg' or Up a Tree goals unless a tree route has been verified in the current run.
6. Do not spend early steps on mailbox and leaflet detours; two of five steps this run gave 0 score and no progress.
7. Do not go down the trap door without a lit light source; it shuts behind you and blocks the way back.
8. Do not shuttle among Cellar, Troll Room and East Chasm, or drop and retake items; earlier loops gave 0 score.
