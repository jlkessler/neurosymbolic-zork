LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen; the +10 was recorded on that west move. Do this early, without detours.
2. Forest Path 'up' reaches Up a Tree; 'take egg' gave +5. 'put egg in case' in the Living Room gave another +5. Bank it promptly.
3. Before descending, take lantern and sword, turn on the lantern, and check inventory. The trap door shuts and bars behind you.
4. Push rug, open trap door, down: +25 was recorded on 'down' on arrival in the Cellar. Credit it to 'down', not 'up'.
5. Keep the lantern lit and carried. Read each new room's text for darkness before choosing an exit.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain. After each pickup or deposit, confirm the score change.
7. If a route stalls or an exit is blocked, try one untested exit or object rather than repeating the stalled action.
8. Earlier runs recorded Gallery 'take art' +4, case deposit +6 and maze 'take bag' +10. Treat these as unverified candidates and verify each score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not 'hit troll with sword' on arrival. This run's first swing was dodged and the counterblow killed the Actor; 'the sword defeats the troll' is unverified.
2. Do not follow the stale 'up' +25 goal text. The +25 came from Living Room 'down', and 'up' sent the Actor to the Attic.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen and Attic. Three round trips this run scored 0, and the Attic gives no points.
4. Skip mailbox, west-forest wandering and opening the sack. They scored 0 and delayed the lantern and trap door.
5. Do not drop and retake the egg. Go straight to the Living Room, open the case, and put the egg in.
6. Do not descend without a plan for the Troll Room. The trap door bars behind you, and the Actor entered it unprepared.
7. Do not blame grue or darkness without evidence. Read the terminal observation; this death came from the troll with the lantern lit.
8. Never type 'jump' at a chasm edge. Avoid pointless pickups (lunch, garlic, bottle) and loops between Cellar, East Chasm and Troll Room or in the maze.
