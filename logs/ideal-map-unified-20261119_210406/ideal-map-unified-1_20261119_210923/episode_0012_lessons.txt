LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go straight to Behind House, open the window, enter the Kitchen, and check for the +10 score line; the window opened this run.
2. Take lantern and sword in the Living Room, light the lantern, and open the trap door before descending; Cellar +25 was recorded earlier.
3. Before entering the Troll Room, carry the sword and a lit lantern; earlier armed combat defeated the troll.
4. Cellar south then east reaches the Gallery; 'take art' gave +4, and 'put painting in case' in the Living Room gave +6.
5. In the maze, west, west, west, up reached room #167; 'take bag' gave +10. Verify the score after each pickup.
6. At the Cyclops Room, saying 'odysseus' makes the cyclops flee and opens the east wall; then Strange Passage, west, up reached the Treasure Room +25.
7. If a route is blocked, try one untested command or exit, then re-read the observation before repeating anything.
8. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and bank treasures in the trophy case promptly.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in darkness without a lit lantern; an earlier run died to a grue after moving south from a dark Cellar.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; an earlier run was knocked out and the episode ended.
3. Never type 'jump' at a chasm edge; an earlier run died from it.
4. Do not wander Forest, Forest Path or Clearing before entering the Kitchen; earlier forest steps scored 0 and delayed progress.
5. Do not pursue stale 'take egg' or Up a Tree goals; no tree route was verified and repeats scored 0.
6. Do not loop northwest/southeast in the maze or shuttle Cellar, East Chasm and Troll Room; about 20 moves scored 0.
7. Do not make repeated Attic or Kitchen trips without a new item or goal; they wasted moves and scored nothing.
8. Do not open the mailbox or take detours before the window; this run's mailbox step scored 0, and North House cost extra moves.
