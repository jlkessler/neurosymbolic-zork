LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, go west into Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move; do it early.
2. Take the egg from the tree (+5) and the Gallery art (+4); both scored on pickup. Bank them in the case afterward.
3. Open the trophy case, then deposit treasures. Egg (+5) and painting (+6) deposits were each score-verified; check the score after each.
4. Living Room 'down' recorded +25 on first Cellar arrival. Prepare lantern, sword and inventory first; the trap door then bars behind you.
5. When navigation offers a confirmed route home with treasure, follow it. The return to Living Room was verified at step 132 and the painting deposit scored.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and read the observation before trusting goal text.
7. When a loop stalls after two or three zero-score visits, leave for an untried exit, such as the Studio or Gallery frontiers.
8. Keep the lantern lit and carry the sword while underground. Before leaving a banked-score area, check inventory for light and weapon.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not trust the stale 'up +25' goal. The +25 came from Living Room 'down'; repeating 'up' elsewhere scored 0 for most of the episode.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm, Chasm/East-West Passage or Loud Room/Deep Canyon. Dozens of such moves scored nothing and burned the step budget.
3. Do not fight the thief or light matches near him. Hitting him with the axe in the Dome Room produced the fatal -10.
4. Do not use 'wait' or vague commands like 'push all' near a visible armed character. React to the threat or leave.
5. Do not wander with a banked score and no new objective; revisiting known rooms (Round Room, Engravings Cave) repeatedly yielded no points.
6. Do not retry objects that already refused you, such as the platinum bar ('You can't be serious'), without a new prerequisite.
7. Do not take low-value detours such as the guidebook and Maintenance Room buttons while treasure or new exits remain unexplored.
8. Do not enter the Troll Room or Dome Room without confirming you can survive a hostile encounter; choose a different exit.
