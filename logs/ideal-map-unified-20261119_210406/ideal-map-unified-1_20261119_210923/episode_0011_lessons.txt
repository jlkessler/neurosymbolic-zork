LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go to Behind House, open window, west into Kitchen: +10 recorded at step 7. Skip the forest entirely.
2. Take lantern and sword in Living Room, push rug, light lantern, open trap door, go down: Cellar +25 recorded with light on.
3. Cellar south, then east reaches Gallery; 'take art' gave +4. Carry it to the Living Room case: 'put painting in case' gave +6.
4. Enter the Troll Room armed with the sword and lit lantern. Repeated 'hit troll with sword' knocked out, then killed, the troll; west leads into the maze.
5. In the maze, west, west, west, up reached room #167; 'take bag' gave +10. Verify the score line after each pickup.
6. At the Cyclops Room, saying 'odysse' made the cyclops flee and open the east wall. Then Strange Passage, west, up gave Treasure Room +25.
7. When a route is blocked, try an untested command or exit once, then re-check the observation. This unlocked the Cyclops Room exit.
8. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and watch for drops: taking the painting from the case cost -6.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in darkness without a lit lantern: an earlier run died to a grue after 'south' from a dark Cellar.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; an earlier run was knocked out and the episode ended. Armed combat worked this run.
3. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; an earlier run died from it.
4. Do not wander Forest, Forest Path or Clearing before the window; steps 2-5 this run scored 0 and delayed the Kitchen.
5. Ignore stale 'take egg' / Up a Tree goals; no tree route was verified and they were re-issued about eight times with 0 score.
6. Do not shuttle Cellar, East Chasm and Troll Room, or loop northwest/southeast in maze room #52; about 20 moves scored 0.
7. Do not drop and retake treasure, or take a deposited painting out of the case; that cost -6 and needed a redeposit.
8. Skip mailbox, sack, bottle, garlic and rope detours; none produced score. Leave the trap door open path and avoid hauling junk.
