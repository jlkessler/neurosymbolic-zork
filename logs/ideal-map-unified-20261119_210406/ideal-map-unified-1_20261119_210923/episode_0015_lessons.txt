5. Before descending, take a light source, turn it on and check inventory. The trap door barred behind the Actor.
6. If an observation says it is pitch black, stop and fix the light before any movement. Use only safe non-movement actions.
7. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and check the score after every pickup or deposit.
8. If a route stalls or an exit is blocked, test one untried exit or object rather than repeating the stalled action.

3. Do not 'put down' or drop treasure in the Living Room. It leaves the egg unbanked and wastes a step.
4. Do not follow the stale 'up' +25 goal text. The +25 came from Living Room 'down', not 'up'.
5. Skip the opening mailbox and the west-forest detour. Both scored 0 and delayed the productive route.
6. Do not trust older lesson claims about the troll or the Attic without evidence. This episode contained neither, so verify cause of death.
7. Do not leave the lit, safe house with treasure still unbanked and no inventory check. Verify what you carry before going down.
8. Do not treat the symbolic navigator's goal text as a command. Check the current observation and prerequisites before acting.
