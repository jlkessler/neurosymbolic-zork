LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window and enter; this route to the Kitchen coincided with a recorded +10 on the west move.
2. After the rug reveals the trap door, open it and descend; the descent coincided with +25 in this episode.
3. Push the rug in the Living Room when a trap door is not yet visible; it revealed one here.
4. Check each action's score delta and credit only moves where the score event is recorded.
5. Read the observation after each move, and stop to reassess when it warns of darkness or danger.
6. Take visible portable items from rooms you pass, such as the Kitchen's sack or bottle, before descending.
7. Before going underground, secure a light source and confirm that it works.
8. Deposit confirmed valuables in the Living Room trophy case, and check the score after each deposit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never take an unknown exit in pitch-black darkness; the south move from the Cellar led straight to a grue death.
2. Do not follow a stale goal into unexplored places after a grue warning; the Actor chased the egg goal and died.
3. Do not descend without a light; the trap door shut behind the Actor and left it in the dark.
4. Do not pass useful Kitchen items without examining or taking them; the sack and bottle were ignored.
5. Do not treat a score-action label as proof of a score cause; check the recorded event.
6. Do not move on after a danger warning without a safe fallback or a known route home.
7. Do not manipulate fragile treasures with tools or force; opening the egg with a leaflet earlier wasted turns and broke it.
8. Do not store non-treasure weapons in the trophy case expecting points; the sword scored 0.
