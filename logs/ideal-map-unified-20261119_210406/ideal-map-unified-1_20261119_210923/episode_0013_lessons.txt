LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, go west into Kitchen; the +10 was recorded on that west move. Do this first, with no detours.
2. In the Living Room, take lantern and sword, light the lantern, and check inventory shows both before descending. The trap door shuts and bars behind you.
3. Push rug, open trap door, go down: Cellar +25 was recorded. Prepare light and weapon first, because no retreat upward was available.
4. The sword defeats the troll. Enter the Troll Room carrying the sword and a lit lantern.
5. With light carried, Cellar south then east reaches the Gallery; 'take art' gave +4, and 'put painting in case' in the Living Room gave +6.
6. In the maze, west, west, west, up reached room #167; 'take bag' gave +10. Verify the score after each pickup.
7. At the Cyclops Room say 'odysseus': the cyclops flees and the east wall opens. Strange Passage, west, up reached the Treasure Room +25.
8. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain. Bank treasures in the trophy case promptly, and try one untested exit if blocked.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in darkness. This run lit the lantern but never took it, then died moving south from the pitch-black Cellar.
2. Do not trust symbolic goal text over the observation: the +25 came from Living Room 'down', yet the 'up' goal sent the Actor on Attic trips.
3. Do not shuttle Kitchen and Attic. Six round trips scored 0, and the Attic was dark.
4. Skip mailbox, North House and forest wandering before the window; they scored 0 and wasted early moves in this and earlier runs.
5. Do not pursue stale 'take egg' or Up a Tree goals; no tree route was verified and repeats scored 0.
6. Do not loop northwest/southeast in the maze or shuttle Cellar, East Chasm and Troll Room; about 20 moves scored 0.
7. Do not pick up lunch, garlic, bottle or sack without a stated use; those pickups scored 0 and delayed taking the lantern.
8. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed or type 'jump' at a chasm edge; earlier runs ended in a knockout and a death.
