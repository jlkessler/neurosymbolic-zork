LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open and enter newly discovered access points, such as windows, to reach unexplored interior rooms; one such entry preceded a +10 score event.
2. Take visible portable objects in new rooms and check the score delta; the egg and painting both scored when taken.
3. Deposit valuables in the Living Room trophy case; the egg deposit gave a confirmed +2.
4. Inspect rooms for movable furniture and hidden openings; moving the rug revealed a trap door that was then opened.
5. Descend through newly opened underground routes; going down into the Cellar coincided with +25 in this episode.
6. After taking a treasure underground, switch promptly to returning via the confirmed route to bank it.
7. Use the lantern before dark underground exploration; turn it on once taken, and keep it on while exploring.
8. Test exits in new rooms systematically, and record the room that each exit leads to.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not manipulate fragile treasures with tools or force; opening the egg with a leaflet and shaking it wasted turns and left it broken.
2. Avoid repeating open, close, and shake loops on one object after the first no-score result; change approach.
3. Do not wander the house perimeter aimlessly; note known entrances like the Kitchen window and use them directly.
4. Do not shuttle between Cellar and East Chasm without examining new exits; repeated back-and-forth earned nothing.
5. Do not store non-treasure weapons in the case expecting points; the sword deposit scored 0.
6. Do not carry an unbanked painting into further exploration; return to the Living Room right after the first valuable underground find.
7. Do not leave the Living Room trap door route unrecorded; it shut behind the Actor, so find alternate return paths first.
8. Do not credit actions for score unless the score event confirms them; many exploratory moves produced 0 delta.
