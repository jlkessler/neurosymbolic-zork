LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then enter west; the Kitchen entry recorded +10 and led on to the Living Room.
2. Push the rug and open the trap door before descending; both gave clear confirmations, and 'down' then recorded +25.
3. Treat the Cellar +25 as already earned and non-repeatable; spend later turns on new, safe surface frontiers instead.
4. Before any descent, run 'inventory' and confirm a light source is carried and lit; do not assume it from the plan.
5. If a lantern is found, take it first, then turn it on, and confirm 'now on' before going underground.
6. Read each new observation for danger cues, such as 'pitch black', 'grue' or armed creatures, before choosing an exit.
7. If the room turns dark on arrival, stop moving and use a safe action, such as lighting a lamp or going back up, before exploring.
8. Check the score response after each transition and credit a score gain only to the action whose observation shows it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black without light; going north from the dark Cellar after the grue warning ended the run with -10.
2. Do not descend underground without a verified lit light source; the Cellar was entered with nothing lit.
3. Do not follow a stale goal such as 'take egg at Up a Tree' while the current room is a different, dangerous area.
4. Do not treat 'down' as safe just because it scored +25 before; check the current observation and inventory first.
5. Avoid repeated entry into any room with a hostile creature; if one is seen, retreat to a safe room instead of shuttling.
6. Do not give or drop items to a hostile creature during an attack; this wastes a turn and the recorded outcome was a knockout.
7. Do not wander without a purpose; the early mailbox and north/east detour gained 0 score and delayed useful progress.
8. Do not open containers or fiddle with items in a dangerous underground room without a stated purpose or an escape route.
