LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west; the Kitchen entry recorded +10 and leads on to the Living Room.
2. Take the lantern, turn it on, and see 'now on' before the trap door; this run's descent was lit.
3. Push the rug and open the trap door first; both gave clear confirmations, and 'down' then recorded +25.
4. Treat the Cellar +25 as earned; the trap door closes behind you, so spend later turns on new, safe frontiers.
5. Take portable valuables like the Gallery art (+4 recorded once), then follow the home route toward the Living Room and verify any trophy-case score.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain; check the score response after each transition.
7. Read each observation for danger cues, such as darkness, grue, edges or armed creatures, before choosing an exit.
8. If a room turns dark, stop moving and light the lamp or retreat up before exploring.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; it killed the agent for -10 and ended the run.
2. Do not descend without a verified lit light source, and never move in pitch black; a grue is a known death risk.
3. Do not shuttle among Gallery, Studio, East Chasm and Cellar; about 30 loop steps gave 0 score. Change plan after one revisit.
4. Do not drop and retake the same item repeatedly; after the first +4, 'put down art' and 'take art' gave 0.
5. Do not follow a stale goal like 'take egg at Up a Tree' while in an unrelated underground room.
6. Do not reverse a navigate-to-home step while carrying a valuable; the Actor's south move undid the route and the painting was never banked.
7. Do not enter the troll room unarmed, and do not drop or give items to hostile creatures.
8. Do not wander or hoard low-value items (leaflet, manual, mailbox detour) without a purpose; when stalled, use look or inventory, not risky verbs.
