LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Go straight to Behind House, open the window, then west; Kitchen entry recorded +10 again, so skip forest exploration.
2. Push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded +25 on arrival in the Cellar; confirm score from that observation.
3. Take the lantern from the Living Room before descending; this run had no light source when the trap door shut.
4. If you arrive in darkness, your very next action must be lighting your lamp; do not move first.
5. Earlier runs recorded Gallery art +4 and trophy case +6; bank valuables by the known route before exploring further.
6. Treat 'pitch black' and 'likely to be eaten by a grue' as stop signals; reassess inventory before any move.
7. When stalled, use look or inventory, then try untried non-troll exits instead of revisiting known rooms.
8. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain; verify the score line after each transition.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black: 'south' from the Cellar with no light ended in grue death, recorded -10.
2. Never enter the Troll Room unarmed; an earlier run ended in a knockout, recorded -10 and episode end.
3. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm or any chasm edge; an earlier run died from it.
4. Do not spend early turns looping through Forest, Forest Path and Clearing; four moves gave 0 score before the window.
5. Do not let stale remote goals like 'take egg at Up a Tree' drive underground moves; no supporting route has been shown.
6. Do not shuttle among Cellar, Troll Room and East Chasm; about 10 to 14 steps earlier gave 0 score.
7. Do not go down the trap door without a lit light source; it shuts behind you and blocks the way back.
8. Do not drop and retake the same item or re-descend aimlessly after banking treasure; neither gave score.
