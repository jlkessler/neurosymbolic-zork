LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window and enter the Kitchen; the west move there recorded +10 again this episode.
2. Before any descent, run 'inventory' and confirm the lantern is carried and lit; lighting it in the room is not enough.
3. Take the lantern explicitly, then verify it appears in inventory; 'turn on lantern' worked while the lantern was still in the room.
4. Push the rug and open the trap door; both worked safely. Prepare light and an escape plan before using 'down'.
5. Treat 'pitch black' or a grue warning as an emergency: stop, check inventory and light, and avoid exits unless light is certain.
6. Credit score only to the recorded event: +10 came on the Kitchen entry and +25 on 'down', and the latter ended in danger.
7. Compare the Actor's stated beliefs, such as 'I have a lit lantern', against inventory and observations before acting on them.
8. Take portable Kitchen items such as the bottle, and examine them, before leaving; the bottle held water.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never descend unless the lantern is verified in inventory and on; this run apparently left it upstairs and died in the Cellar.
2. Never move in pitch black; the north move from the dark Cellar triggered a grue death and a -10 event.
3. Do not pursue a remote goal like 'take egg' from a dark room; stale goals drove the fatal blind move.
4. Do not repeat 'down' for +25 as a safe point source; the recorded gain came with a trap door shutting behind the Actor.
5. Do not waste dark turns on trivial actions like dropping the leaflet; it gave no light and no escape.
6. Do not trust a score-goal label or a prior total gain as proof of safety; check prerequisites and the current observation.
7. Do not shuttle between Behind House and the Kitchen without a purpose; those moves gave 0 and delayed preparation.
8. Do not leave the Kitchen or Living Room without taking light and utility items; the lantern and bottle were left unused.
