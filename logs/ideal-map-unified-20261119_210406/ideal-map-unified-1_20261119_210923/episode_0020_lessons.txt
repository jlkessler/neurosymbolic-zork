LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From West House go north, then east to Behind House directly. Skip Forest and repeated North House/West House shuttling.
2. Behind House: open window, then go west into Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move.
3. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then 'down'. The +25 was recorded on that move, once; the door bars behind you.
4. Before 'down', check inventory and confirm a lantern is carried and lit. Do not rely on a reported 'lantern on' message alone.
5. In pitch black, stay put: check inventory and fix the light first. Take no exit until the room description is visible.
6. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain. Treat inherited goal text like 'up +25' as unverified.
7. Bank treasures in the trophy case (egg, art, painting scored in earlier runs) and check the score after each deposit.
8. After two zero-score visits to a loop, take an untried exit. Avoid the thief and prefer unexplored exits.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move through exits in pitch darkness. East from the dark Cellar caused grue death and a -10 penalty.
2. Do not follow the stale 'up +25' goal. Kitchen-to-Attic 'up' scored 0 repeatedly; the +25 came from Living Room 'down'.
3. Do not descend the trap door without checking inventory for light. It crashes shut behind you and leaves no retreat.
4. Do not oscillate between adjacent rooms (West House/North House, Kitchen/Attic) without a new exit or item.
5. Do not spend early steps on low-value detours (mailbox, sack, leaflet) before the confirmed scoring route.
6. Do not wander Forest or other unmapped dark areas; this episode's final move landed in Forest during death.
7. Do not attack, wait near, or ignore the thief. Attacking him in the Dome Room was fatal earlier.
8. Do not enter Troll Room or Dome Room without a survival plan, or open the trophy case empty-handed.
