Historian episode review — episode 3
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 8 positive + 8 negative (configured per category: 8)

The Actor's early play was efficient. It opened the window and entered the Kitchen (+10 recorded on the west move), took the sack, pushed the rug, and opened the trap door. These were sensible, low-risk steps. The fatal error was descending without a light source: the +25 was recorded on 'down', but the move also shut the trap door and left it in pitch black. It never examined the Living Room or its inventory for light, and it left the bottle. In the dark it opened the sack and took garlic, which did not help. It then moved north blindly, was eaten by a grue, and took -10. The symbolic navigation steps worked. The stale 'take egg' goal drove the final move. Next time, secure and test a light before any descent, and treat the +25 as unrepeatable without it.
