LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move; do it early.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending. This episode's Cellar entry stayed safe and lit.
3. Open the trophy case, then put treasure in it. 'put painting in case' recorded +6; verify the score change.
4. A Gallery pickup recorded +4 on 'take art'. Take valuables when seen, then return home to bank them.
5. Living Room 'down' recorded +25 on arrival in the Cellar. Prepare light and inventory before this one-way trip.
6. When a navigation route home is offered and consistent with the observation, follow it. It worked cleanly from the Studio.
7. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and check the score after each pickup or deposit.
8. If a loop stalls, switch to a genuinely unexplored exit or object instead of oscillating between known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll Room again. The troll knocked the Actor out and the -10 ended the episode.
2. Do not repeat Cellar-East Chasm-Cellar loops. They scored 0 for many steps once the trap door was barred.
3. Do not trust the stale 'up +25' goal text. The +25 came from Living Room 'down'; check the observation first.
4. Do not wander after banking without a plan. Roughly half the steps were zero-score loops, so pick one new frontier.
5. Do not move in pitch darkness or without a lit lantern; earlier episodes recorded a grue death and -10.
6. Do not enter a room known to be hostile without evidence you can survive it. Choose another exit.
7. Do not skip inventory checks after pickups and before descending. Know what you carry and whether the light is on.
8. Skip low-value detours such as the mailbox, sack, and Attic unless the observation points to a score source.
