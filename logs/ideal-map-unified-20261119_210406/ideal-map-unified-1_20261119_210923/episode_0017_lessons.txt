LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then west into Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move; do it early.
2. Take the lantern and any sword before descending, and light the lantern immediately on arrival in darkness.
3. Open the trophy case, then put treasures in it. Egg (+5) and painting (+6) deposits were each score-verified.
4. Take valuables when seen: egg in tree (+5) and Gallery art (+4) both scored on pickup. Bank them afterward.
5. Living Room 'down' recorded +25 on first Cellar arrival. Prepare light and inventory first; it is a one-way trip.
6. When symbolic navigation offers a consistent route home, follow it. Studio to Kitchen to Living Room worked cleanly.
7. Credit score only to the action whose observation shows the gain, and check the score after each pickup or deposit.
8. When a loop stalls, pick a genuinely unexplored exit or object, and stop retrying known zero-score rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not use vague catch-all commands like 'push all' near a hostile NPC. The thief attacked and the -10 ended the episode.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar-East Chasm-Cellar. About 30 steps scored 0 once the trap door was barred behind the Actor.
3. Do not trust the stale 'up +25' goal text. The +25 came from Living Room 'down'; read the observation first.
4. Do not use 'wait' in a room with a visible armed character. React to the threat or leave.
5. Do not enter the Troll Room without a weapon and evidence you can survive it. Choose a different exit.
6. Do not go underground without checking inventory. Know whether you carry the lantern and a weapon.
7. Do not move in pitch darkness or without a lit lantern; earlier episodes recorded a grue death and -10.
8. Skip low-value detours such as the mailbox, leaflet, and tree-and-forest wandering once a clear score source is known.
