LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go 'west'. Entering the Kitchen recorded +10 again, so do it early.
2. From Forest Path, 'up' reaches Up a Tree, where 'take egg' recorded +5. Check such spots for portable valuables.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Use the exact 'put X in case' form.
4. Living Room: push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded +25. The +25 came from 'down', not 'up'.
5. Before any descent, run 'inventory' and confirm the lantern is carried and lit. Re-check the light if the arrival text mentions darkness.
6. Bank treasure in the case before descending, as done with the egg, then verify score before leaving the Living Room.
7. When a room is lit, take untried exits and note new rooms. Earlier lit runs reached East Chasm, Gallery and Studio, and 'take art' recorded +4.
8. Compare the score before and after each action, and credit a gain only to the action whose score event recorded it.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move when the text says 'pitch black'. Going 'south' from the Cellar caused a grue death (-10) this episode.
2. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It was fatal (-10) in an earlier run.
3. Don't trust 'lantern is now on' alone. The Cellar still reported pitch black, so stop and diagnose the light before moving.
4. Never generate the stale 'up' +25 goal. Kitchen 'up' is the Attic and scores 0; the +25 came from Living Room 'down'.
5. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Attic, or Cellar and East Chasm. These loops scored 0 in earlier runs.
6. Don't take junk such as the sack, bottle, manual, coil or knife without a stated need. Earlier runs recorded 0 for each.
7. Don't 'put down' or drop a treasure. Only 'put X in case' banks it, and 'put down painting' recorded 0.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the command. This episode's first note said 'north' while the move was west.
