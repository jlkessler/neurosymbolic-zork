LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the lantern first. Run 'inventory' to confirm it is carried and lit before any descent, and keep it intact all episode.
2. Pick up portable valuables in unusual rooms: Up a Tree 'take egg' recorded +5 and Gallery 'take art' recorded +4.
3. At Behind House, open the window and go 'west'. The Kitchen entry recorded +10, so do it early.
4. Bank treasure in the Living Room case with exact 'put X in case' commands. 'put egg in case' recorded +5; verify the score after each deposit.
5. Living Room 'down' recorded +25 only after the trapdoor was open. Treat it as valid only with a carried, lit lantern, and label it 'down'.
6. Read arrival text for darkness or danger after every move. With light available, prefer untried exits and log each new room.
7. After banking, if about 10 moves score 0, leave that area for an unexplored exit instead of re-probing known rooms.
8. Credit gains only to recorded score events. Make state notes match the actual command, room, and inventory, and avoid claiming 'only action' without checking.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw, drop, or break the lantern. 'throw water at lantern' smashed the lamp and extinguished it just before the death (-10).
2. Never move when the text says 'pitch black' or the light is out. The west move from East Chasm in the dark was fatal.
3. Don't loop 'open bottle' and 'close bottle'. Dozens of cycles in the Cellar and Studio scored 0 and burned turns.
4. Don't shuttle Cellar and East Chasm or Gallery and Studio. These loops took hundreds of steps and scored 0.
5. Don't repeatedly go north from the Cellar into the troll room. The troll attacked and nothing scored; it was fatal in an earlier run.
6. Never generate or follow the stale 'up' +25 goal. The +25 came from Living Room 'down', and the mislabel drove the aimless route.
7. Don't use 'down' from the Living Room without inventory showing a carried, lit lantern. Earlier deaths came from descending unlit.
8. Don't take junk (sack, bottle, manual) without a stated need, and don't drop treasure. Only 'put X in case' banks it.
