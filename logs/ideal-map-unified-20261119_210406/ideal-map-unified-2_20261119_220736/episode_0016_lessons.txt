LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the lantern first, before treasure hunting. Run 'inventory' and confirm it is carried before any descent; this episode had no lantern at all.
2. Check Up a Tree via Forest Path 'up'; 'take egg' there recorded +5. Look for portable valuables in unusual rooms.
3. At Behind House, open the window, then go 'west'. The Kitchen entry recorded +10, so do it early.
4. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Use the exact 'put X in case' form.
5. Bank treasure and verify the score before descending. The egg was banked first this episode, so score was secured before the risk.
6. Living Room 'down' recorded +25 after push rug and open trap. Treat 'down' as valid only with a carried, lit lantern.
7. After each move, read the arrival text for darkness or danger before acting. If the room is lit, take untried exits and log new rooms.
8. Compare score before and after each action and credit a gain only to the recorded score event. Write state notes that match the actual command and room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move when the text says 'pitch black'. Going south from the Cellar caused a grue death (-10); diagnose the light source first.
2. Don't use 'down' from the Living Room without inventory showing a carried, lit lantern. This episode descended empty-handed into darkness.
3. Never generate the stale 'up' +25 goal; the +25 came from Living Room 'down'. Don't let that mislabeled goal drive the route.
4. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It was fatal (-10) in an earlier run.
5. Don't assume an exit is safe in the dark. The note 'only south is safe' was unsupported and preceded death.
6. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Attic, or Cellar and East Chasm. These loops scored 0 in earlier runs.
7. Don't take junk (sack, bottle, manual, coil, knife) without a stated need. Earlier runs recorded 0 for each.
8. Don't drop or 'put down' a treasure. Only 'put X in case' banks it, and 'put down painting' recorded 0.
