LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go 'west'. Entering the Kitchen recorded +10 again, so do it early.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before going 'down'. Lit exploration of Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio was safe.
3. From Forest Path, 'up' reaches Up a Tree, where 'take egg' recorded +5. Check such spots for portable valuables.
4. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Use the exact 'put X in case' form.
5. Living Room: push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded +25 again this episode. Do it only with a lit lantern in hand.
6. Bank treasure in the case before descending, as done with the egg. Then check inventory and score before leaving.
7. From the Cellar, go south to East Chasm, then east to the Gallery. 'take art' there recorded +4. Prefer untried exits from lit rooms.
8. Carrying a painting home: Gallery north to Studio, up to Kitchen, west to Living Room. Then 'open case' and 'put painting in case'.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It was fatal (-10) in an earlier run and remains unsafe.
2. Never move in pitch black. Going 'south' from the unlit Cellar ended in a grue death (-10). Light the lantern before any move.
3. Never descend the trap door without a lit lantern. The door crashes shut behind you and there is no retreat.
4. Never generate the stale 'up' +25 goal. Kitchen 'up' is the Attic and scores 0. Ignore any goal text that suggests it.
5. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Attic, or Cellar and East Chasm. These loops wasted steps in earlier runs and scored 0.
6. Don't take junk such as the sack, bottle, manual, coil or knife without a stated need. Earlier runs recorded 0 for each.
7. Don't 'put down' or drop a treasure. Only 'put X in case' banks it, and 'put down painting' recorded 0.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the action, such as 'moving north' while going west. Check the note against the command.
