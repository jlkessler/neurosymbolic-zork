LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. 'open window' then 'west' from Behind House entered the Kitchen and recorded +10. Repeat this early surface route, and take the egg first if it is available.
2. Living Room: 'push rug', 'open trap', then 'down' recorded +25 once. Treat it as a one-time event, never a repeat goal.
3. Take a light source and run 'inventory' to confirm it before going down. The trap door shuts behind you.
4. Keep the lantern in hand at all times. Darkness was the direct precursor of both deaths.
5. Earlier 'take art' (+4) and 'put treasures in case' (+6) scored. Carry found valuables home and deposit them.
6. After about 10 scoreless moves, name one untried exit or object. 'examine' carried items before looping.
7. Have each state note name the true room and darkness status. Check it before choosing any movement verb.
8. Credit points only to recorded score events, and remember that death subtracts 10 from the final score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. 'south' from the dark Cellar met a grue: -10, death, episode over.
2. Don't descend without a confirmed light source. This run reached the Cellar with nothing lit and had no safe action left.
3. Don't drop, stash, or leave the lantern. Dropping it in the Studio led to dark moves and death in a prior run.
4. Don't shuttle among the Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio and Troll room. Earlier runs spent about 350 moves there for zero points.
5. Don't adopt an 'up' or trap-door revisit goal. The +25 was a one-time 'down', and the goal text is stale.
6. Don't re-enter the Troll room. Past visits gave no points and the notes kept flagging it as blocked.
7. Don't probe dark side rooms like the Attic for no reason. The up-and-down detour cost two moves for nothing.
8. Don't burn turns on open, close, shake or put with the sack, garlic or lunch. None of it has scored.
