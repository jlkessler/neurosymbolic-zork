Historian episode review — episode 14
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 8 positive + 8 negative (configured per category: 8)

The Actor scored +35 net in 16 steps but died in the Cellar. Its strongest decisions were 'take egg' in Up a Tree (+5), opening the window and going west to the Kitchen (+10), and then 'open case' and 'put egg in case' (+5). It banked the egg before descending, which was sound. The decisive error was going 'down' with no lantern. The trap door shut behind it, and in pitch black it moved 'south'. The grue killed it (-10). The +25 on 'down' is recorded, but the Actor should have lit a lantern first. Missed opportunities: taking and lighting a lantern, and exploring lit rooms such as the Gallery. Its state notes were sometimes inconsistent with its actions, for example 'moving north' while going west. Symbolic navigation home worked well. Next episode: no light means no descent, and never move blind.
