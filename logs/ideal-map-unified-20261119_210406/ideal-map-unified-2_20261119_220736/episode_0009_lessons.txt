LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then 'west' into the Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move.
2. Living Room 'down' recorded +25. Trust recorded events over any stored 'up' goal, which is stale.
3. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. In new lit rooms, take portable items and compare the score before and after.
4. Putting the painting in the Living Room trophy case recorded +6. Deposit carried treasures there, then verify the score.
5. Up a Tree 'take egg' recorded +5. Explore outdoor rooms beyond the house for new items and rooms.
6. In any dark arrival room, light the lantern and confirm with inventory before choosing an exit.
7. In each lit room, try every unvisited exit once, mark the room finished, then head to a new room.
8. After 10 moves with no score or new room, abandon the loop and pick an untried exit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while the room is dark. The final move north from East Chasm ended in grue death (-10).
2. Ignore stale 'up' and return-home goals. The trap door is barred after 'down', and repeating them wasted over 200 steps.
3. Don't shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. The repeated cycles recorded 0 each time.
4. Avoid the Troll Room: it cost -10 earlier and killed run 2. Don't give it items or fight it.
5. Don't give items to the troll. Giving the sword recorded 0 and preceded death.
6. Don't drop and retake lunch or pepper hoping for score. Those actions recorded 0.
7. Don't open and close the sack repeatedly. Four toggles in the Cellar scored nothing.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the action, like 'light lantern' beside a move into Forest. Verify with inventory.
