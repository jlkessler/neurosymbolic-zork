LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window then go west. The +10 was recorded on the west move into the Kitchen, not on opening.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern and turn it on before any 'down'. This run did so and descended with light.
3. Push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded +25 on the 'down' move. Repeat it only with the lantern lit.
4. Before each move, reread the observation for exits and danger. Retreat immediately from any room that attacks you.
5. Compare the score before and after every action. Credit only recorded events: +10 on the west move, +25 on 'down'.
6. Reject goals whose action conflicts with the record. The +25 came from 'down', so the 'up +25' goal is stale.
7. After taking the lantern, run 'inventory' once to confirm it. This run relied only on the 'Taken' message.
8. Keep the Living Room sequence tight: take lantern, light it, push rug, open trap door, descend. It took five actions with no wasted moves.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. The troll attacked, and the final north move recorded -10 and ended the episode.
2. Don't shuttle between the Cellar and Troll Room. Four round trips scored 0 and invited axe attacks.
3. Don't pursue the stale 'up +25' goal. Kitchen 'up' led to a dark Attic with 0 score.
4. Don't move in pitch black. The Attic was dark with no light in hand. Light first or leave by the way you came.
5. Don't treat a lit lantern as safety. It prevents darkness, not hostile creatures. Reread the observation after each move.
6. Don't open the sack or other containers before getting the lantern. This run found nothing of recorded value there.
7. Don't detour via North House on the way to Behind House. It added moves with no recorded value.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict observations, such as 'trapped' or unexplained plans to re-enter the Troll Room. Base them on the last observation.
