Historian episode review — episode 2
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 8 positive + 8 negative (configured per category: 8)

The Actor's best decisions were the efficient window route and the trap-door setup. Opening the Behind House window and going west gave a recorded +10 on entering the Kitchen. Pushing the rug, opening the trap door and going down gave a recorded +25. Navigation transitions were all confirmed, so the failures were the Actor's own choices. It wasted four early actions on the mailbox, the leaflet and a North House detour, and none of them scored. Its biggest miss was passing through the Living Room without taking the lantern or checking inventory. It then descended into a dark Cellar, wasted a turn putting down the leaflet, and moved south in the dark. That move ended the episode in death, with a -10 recorded. The stale 'up +25' goal was misleading, because the recorded +25 came from 'down'. Next time: go directly to the lantern, confirm it in inventory, turn it on before 'down', and if the room is dark, allow only 'turn on lantern'.
