LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window, then go west. The +10 was recorded on the west move into the Kitchen.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern, push rug, open trap door, then 'down'. The +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Turn on the lantern as soon as it is in hand, ideally before 'down'. Light it first thing if you arrive in pitch black.
4. Use only recorded score events as evidence: +10 on the west move into the Kitchen, +25 on 'down'. Do not credit other actions.
5. Treat the 'up +25' goal as stale. The +25 came from 'down' in the Living Room, and Kitchen 'up' scored 0.
6. Once lit, record every exit in each new room and try only unvisited ones. East Chasm's exits other than north are untested.
7. Run 'inventory' after taking items and before descending, so notes about light and items rest on confirmed state.
8. Go straight from the window to the Living Room trap door. The shortest route gave +35 in about 14 steps.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't enter the Troll Room. The troll blocked all exits there, and the final north move from the Cellar recorded -10 and ended the episode.
2. Don't shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, and Troll Room. About 17 moves of round trips scored 0. After one visit, mark a room as done.
3. Don't follow the stale 'up' goal to the Attic. It scored 0 and wasted two moves in darkness.
4. Don't spend early moves on the mailbox, leaflet, or North House. All scored 0 and delayed the window and Kitchen.
5. Don't move in darkness. If an observation says pitch black, light the lantern or stop, and never step into an unseen exit.
6. Don't descend through the trap door unprepared. It crashed shut and was barred behind the Actor, so retreat is impossible.
7. Don't re-enter a room after your own note says it blocks all exits. A move back there is not exploration.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the last observation, such as 'Cellar remains dark' while the lantern is on.
