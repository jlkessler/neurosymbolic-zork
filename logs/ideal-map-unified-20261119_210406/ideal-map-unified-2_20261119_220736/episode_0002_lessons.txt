LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then go west. Entering the Kitchen after this recorded +10.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern first, run 'inventory' to confirm it, and 'turn on lantern' before any 'down'.
3. The sequence push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded +25 once. Do it only with a lit lantern in hand.
4. If an observation says pitch black, the only permitted action is 'turn on lantern'. Never move, drop or shuffle items.
5. Take the direct route: window, Kitchen, Living Room, lantern. Skip side trips, since every extra action risks nothing gained.
6. Compare the score before and after each action, and credit only recorded score events to the action that caused them.
7. Check a goal's wording against the observed action. 'Up +25' conflicted with the recorded 'down' event, so treat such goals as stale.
8. Before any one-way transition, such as a door that crashes shut behind you, confirm light and inventory. Once underground, bank treasures and avoid the thief.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in the dark Cellar without light. The south move there was fatal (-10) and has repeated across runs.
2. Never descend through the trap door without the lantern in inventory and lit. The door shuts behind you and blocks retreat.
3. Don't pursue the stale 'up +25' goal. The recorded +25 came from 'down' in the Living Room.
4. Don't open the mailbox or take the leaflet. It scored nothing and delayed reaching the lantern.
5. Don't detour through North House on the way to Behind House. It added moves with no recorded value.
6. Don't shuffle inventory in the dark, such as dropping the leaflet or putting items in the sack. Light first, or do nothing else.
7. Don't make Kitchen-Attic round trips. The Attic is dark and has no recorded value.
8. Don't treat a score gain on entering a dark place as proof it is safe. Light the lantern before any other action.
