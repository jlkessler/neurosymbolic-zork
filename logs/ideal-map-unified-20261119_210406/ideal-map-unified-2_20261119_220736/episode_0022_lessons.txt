LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on Kitchen entry. Repeat it early, then check the score.
2. 'take egg' at Up a Tree recorded +5, and 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Bank carried treasure before descending.
3. 'push rug', 'open trap', then 'down' recorded +25 in the Living Room. Do this only after securing light and banking treasure.
4. Before any descent, run 'inventory' and confirm a light source is carried. Take one from the house if it's missing.
5. Light the lamp the moment it is in hand, and verify the room description is no longer pitch black before moving.
6. Examine or take portable items in the Kitchen and Living Room, such as the sack and bottle, before leaving the house.
7. Credit points only to recorded events: egg +5, Kitchen entry +10, case deposit +5, trap-door 'down' +25. Keep state notes consistent with the real room.
8. After about 10 scoreless moves, name one specific untried exit or object, and try 'examine' or 'read' on carried items.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. 'south' from the dark Cellar met a grue: death, -10, episode over.
2. Don't descend the trap door without a lit light source. The door bars behind you and there is no way back.
3. Never 'give troll' anything, and don't go north from the Cellar into the Troll room. That route cost -10 before.
4. Don't follow navigate_score_goal 'up' blindly. The +25 was the one-time Living Room 'down', not a repeatable 'up'.
5. Don't treat navigate_to_home as viable once underground. The trap door is barred, so choose a specific untried exit instead.
6. Don't let state notes contradict the action, such as saying 'north' while moving west. Write the actual intent and room each turn.
7. Don't shuttle between Gallery, Studio, East Chasm and Cellar. Earlier runs spent about 55 moves there with no new room or score.
8. Don't skip exploring above ground. Only a few surface rooms were seen, so check unvisited exits before spending the one-way descent.
