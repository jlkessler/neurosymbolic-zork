LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the Behind House window, then enter west. That sequence produced a recorded +10 on entering the Kitchen.
2. In the Living Room, move the rug and open the trap door before 'down'; this route gave a recorded +25.
3. Before descending anywhere, take the lantern in the Living Room and turn it on. Confirm it is in inventory.
4. If a room is described as pitch black, light the lantern first. No other action is acceptable.
5. Retreat immediately from dark rooms you can't light, as done twice at the Attic, instead of exploring them.
6. Check the score before and after each action, and credit only recorded score events to the action that caused them.
7. Prefer verified scoring actions over stale goals. Check the room's exits and the action text against the goal.
8. Plan toward securing treasures in the Living Room, and avoid the thief once underground.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room without light. The south move from the dark Cellar was fatal (-10).
2. Don't go down the trap door without a lit lantern. The door shuts behind you and you can't retreat.
3. Don't follow stale goals blindly. 'Up +25' was wrong; 'down' from the Living Room was the scoring move.
4. Don't spend early moves on the mailbox: opening it, taking the leaflet, and putting it back scored nothing.
5. Avoid pointless exploration loops like West House to North House to Behind House. Go straight to known productive actions.
6. Don't waste turns in darkness on inventory shuffling (put/drop lunch, put garlic in sack). Light first or leave.
7. Don't repeat the Kitchen-Attic round trip when the Attic is dark and has no recorded value.
8. Don't ignore advice to carry the lantern. Check inventory before leaving the Living Room.
