LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree ('take egg' recorded +5), then go straight to Behind House and the Kitchen without detours.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on Kitchen entry. Repeat it early each game.
3. In the Living Room, 'open case' then 'put egg in case' recorded +5. Check the score after every deposit.
4. Before 'down', take the lantern, light it, and confirm it in inventory. 'down' recorded +25 once; the lit lantern is the only safe way below.
5. If the lantern dims or the room goes dark, stop and fix light first. Treat each lit turn as a limited budget.
6. Pick up portable valuables in new rooms and bank them: Gallery 'take art' recorded +4, and the case deposit +6.
7. After about 10 moves with no score, commit to an untried exit or a new room instead of retracing mapped ones.
8. Credit gains only to recorded score events. Label the +25 as Living Room 'down', and keep state notes matching real room, light and inventory.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while it is pitch black. 'west' from the dark East Chasm ended in a grue death (-10).
2. Don't burn lantern turns shuttling Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. About 300 moves there scored 0 and the lamp ran nearly out.
3. Never follow navigate_score_goal 'up'. The +25 came from Living Room 'down' and is already banked; the goal only produced scoreless loops.
4. Don't go north from the Cellar into the Troll room. It scored 0 and is a known knockout risk (-10 in earlier runs).
5. Don't descend without a lit lantern in inventory. The trap door shuts behind you and the Cellar is dark.
6. Never drop, stash, or put the lantern in the sack; a thief wandered by right after. Keep it carried and lit.
7. Don't loop 'open sack'/'close sack' or drop lunch and garlic. These burn turns, and none of them scored.
8. After banking, don't wander known rooms. Check light and inventory, then try one unvisited exit or return to bank.
