Historian episode review — episode 15
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 8 positive + 8 negative (configured per category: 8)

The Actor played the early game efficiently, scoring 35 after a final -10 death. Its best decisions were taking the egg at Up a Tree (+5), opening the window and entering the Kitchen (+10), and banking the egg with 'open case' then 'put egg in case' (+5). It also pushed the rug and opened the trap door before the Living Room 'down', which recorded +25. It did not follow the stale 'up' goal text. Symbolic navigation handled the walk home adequately but took a roundabout route. The failure came after the descent. 'turn on lantern' returned 'now on', yet the Cellar reported pitch black. The Actor ignored that and moved 'south', which ended in a grue death. Its first state note said 'north' while it moved west. Next time, treat any 'pitch black' text as a stop sign: check inventory, the light and the lantern state, and do not move until the room is lit.
