LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then 'west' into the Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move at step 4.
2. Living Room: take lantern, push rug, open trap door, then 'down'. The +25 was recorded on 'down'; it is a one-time move.
3. After arriving in a pitch-black room, spend the first turn lighting the lantern and reading the new room text before any exit.
4. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. In new lit rooms, take portable items and compare the score before and after.
5. Judge goals by recorded score events. Ignore any 'up' goal: it recorded 0 at Kitchen, and the +25 came from 'down'.
6. In each new lit room, try every unvisited exit once, then mark the room finished and move to a new frontier.
7. Run 'inventory' after 'turn on lantern' and before leaving each dark arrival room, so the light source is verified.
8. After 10 moves with no score or new room, leave the loop and try an exit or room not yet visited.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never take exits while the observation says pitch black; Cellar east and West from East Chasm both ended in grue deaths in earlier runs.
2. Don't enter or linger in the Troll Room. It cost -10 and ended this episode, and earlier it killed run 2.
3. Don't shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. About 60 steps there scored nothing after the +4.
4. Don't drop and retake treasure, or take the manual, hoping for score. Those actions recorded 0.
5. Don't chase 'return to Living Room' after 'down'. The trap door is barred, and repeated tries wasted dozens of steps.
6. Don't open with North House or other detours. Steps 1-2 delayed the route that scored.
7. Don't give items to the troll or interact with it. It recorded 0 and the Troll Room ended the run.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the action, such as 'turning on lantern' beside 'take sword'. Confirm the lantern is lit through inventory.
