LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on Kitchen entry. Repeat it early each game.
2. Take the lantern, 'push rug', 'open trap', then 'down' recorded +25. Light the lantern immediately in the dark Cellar.
3. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Pick up portable valuables in new rooms and check the score change.
4. Explore above-ground exits (Up a Tree egg earlier recorded +5) before descending, because the trap door crashes shut behind you.
5. After about 10 scoreless moves, name one specific untried exit or object in the state note, and try 'examine' or 'read' on carried items.
6. Carry treasures back to the Living Room trophy case and check the score after each deposit; bank before wandering further.
7. Credit points only to recorded events: Kitchen entry +10, Living Room 'down' +25, Gallery 'take art' +4. Keep state notes matching real room, light and inventory.
8. Keep the lantern carried and lit, and check light each few moves. If the room goes dark, fix light before moving.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never 'give troll painting' or any treasure to the troll. It recorded -10, ate the painting, knocked you out and ended the episode.
2. Don't go north from the Cellar into the Troll room. Three visits scored 0 and the last ended the run.
3. Don't shuttle Gallery, Studio, East Chasm and Cellar. About 55 moves found no new room and no score.
4. Never follow navigate_score_goal 'up'. The +25 was the one-time Living Room 'down', and 'up' only produced detours.
5. Don't revisit the Studio hoping for more. Its only exit leads back south, and 'take manual' scored 0.
6. Don't carry treasure underground with no verified route home. The trap door shut and the painting was never banked.
7. Don't blindly obey navigate_to_home when it routes north into the Troll room. Stop and choose a safe untried exit instead.
8. Don't skip above-ground exploration. Only North House and Behind House were visited; try unvisited surface exits before spending moves underground.
