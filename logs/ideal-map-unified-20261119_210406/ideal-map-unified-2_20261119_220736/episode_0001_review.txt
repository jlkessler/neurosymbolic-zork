Historian episode review — episode 1
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 8 positive + 8 negative (configured per category: 8)

The Actor's strongest play was opening the Behind House window and entering the Kitchen (+10 recorded on that move), then moving the rug, opening the trap door and going down (+25 recorded). It also backed out of the dark Attic twice instead of exploring it.

It made two major errors. It passed through the Living Room without taking the lantern, then went down into the Cellar unlit. In the dark it spent five actions shuffling lunch and garlic, then moved south and was eaten by a grue (-10, episode over). Early moves were also wasted: the mailbox sequence, the North House loop, and the stale 'up +25' goal, which misdirected it. 'Down' from the Living Room was the actual scoring move. Next time: take the lantern and light it before 'down', and if a room is dark, light the lantern before any other action. Bank treasures and avoid the thief.
