LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go 'west'. Entering the Kitchen recorded +10, so do this early.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before going down. Lit exploration of Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio was safe.
3. Living Room: push rug, open trap, then 'down' recorded +25 once. Don't expect it again; use the time on new exits.
4. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Take portable valuables in new lit rooms and compare the score afterward.
5. Carrying a painting home: Gallery north to Studio, up to Kitchen, west to Living Room. This route is confirmed and worked.
6. In the Living Room with the case open and a treasure in hand, immediately use the exact command 'put painting in case'.
7. From the Cellar, take south to East Chasm, then east to the Gallery. Use untried exits from lit rooms rather than revisiting known rooms.
8. After 'open case' returns 'Opened', the next action should be depositing the treasure. Then check inventory and score before leaving.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It was fatal (-10) in an earlier run and remains blocked and unsafe.
2. Never generate the stale 'up' +25 goal. The +25 was Living Room 'down', already collected. Kitchen 'up' is the Attic and scores 0.
3. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Attic, or Cellar and East Chasm. These loops cost about 8 steps this run and scored 0.
4. Don't re-enter the Attic to light the lantern. Turn it on right after taking it, before any move.
5. Don't take junk such as the sack, bottle, manual, coil or knife without a stated need. Earlier runs recorded 0 for each.
6. Don't 'put down' or drop a treasure. Only 'put painting in case' counts, and 'put down painting' recorded 0.
7. Don't end the episode carrying an unbanked treasure. Before spending steps elsewhere, deposit what you hold.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the action, such as 'moving north to the Cellar' while heading east to the Gallery.
