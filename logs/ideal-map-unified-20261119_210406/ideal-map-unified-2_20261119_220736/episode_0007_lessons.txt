LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, go west into the Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move.
2. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then 'down'. The +25 was recorded on 'down'; the door is barred afterward, so do it once.
3. Before 'down', 'take lantern', light it, and confirm with 'inventory'. The Cellar was pitch black despite an earlier 'lantern is now on'.
4. If an observation says pitch black, spend the turn on inventory or lantern checks, not on exits.
5. Compare stored goals with recorded score events. Stale 'up' scored 0 at Kitchen; trust the 'down' evidence and the current observation.
6. In new lit rooms, list exits and try each unvisited one once, then mark the room finished.
7. Take portable items in new lit rooms and check the score; Gallery 'take art' previously recorded +4.
8. After 10 moves without a score or new room, change frontier; don't loop known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never take any exit in darkness. Cellar east recorded -10 and a grue death; West from East Chasm did the same earlier.
2. Don't write 'lantern on' or 'safe' in state notes without confirming it in inventory or room text; this episode's notes contradicted each other.
3. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Living Room, as steps 7-13 did, or Studio, Gallery, Cellar, and East Chasm. Those moves scored 0.
4. Don't spend early moves on mailbox, leaflet, or North House; they scored 0 and delayed the real route.
5. Don't re-issue the stale 'up' goal. Kitchen 'up' leads to the Attic with 0 gain.
6. Don't enter the Troll Room or the Studio; the Troll Room killed run 2 and the Studio only looped.
7. Don't open or handle the sack, lunch, garlic, or pepper. None of these produced score.
8. Don't descend with an unverified light source; the trap door bars behind you, so check inventory before 'down'.
