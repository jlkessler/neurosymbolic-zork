LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg early. 'take egg' (+5) and 'put egg in case' (+5) were recorded; get light and egg before the trap door.
2. 'open window' then 'west' from Behind House entered the Kitchen and recorded +10. Repeat this early surface route.
3. Before any 'down' or underground move, run 'inventory' and confirm the lantern is held and lit. Treat unlit descents as high risk.
4. Order goals: lantern and egg first, then trap door. The +25 'down' scored once and is not repeatable.
5. Carry found valuables home and deposit them: 'take art' (+4) and 'put treasures in case' (+6) were both recorded.
6. Once the lantern is lit, try one untried exit or object after about 10 scoreless moves, and note it.
7. Each state note should name the true room, darkness status and lantern status. Check them before choosing any movement verb.
8. If a room is dark, spend the turn on light or non-movement actions such as 'inventory' or 'examine'. Do not walk blind.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a dark room. 'south' from the dark Cellar met a grue: -10, death, episode over.
2. Don't put down the lantern, especially in the dark. It was dropped in the Cellar one move before the fatal step.
3. Don't adopt an 'up' or trap-door revisit goal. The +25 was a one-time 'down'; the stale goal was reissued about 20 times.
4. Don't shuttle among Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. About 340 moves there recorded zero points; avoid 2-room loops.
5. Don't re-enter the Troll room. Repeated visits gave no points and the notes kept calling it blocked.
6. Don't spend turns taking or dropping pepper, garlic or lunch. None of it scored.
7. Don't make Attic or dark side-room detours without a reason. They cost moves and risk darkness.
8. Don't descend without a confirmed lantern, and don't trust a note claiming 'lantern in hand'. Verify with 'inventory'.
