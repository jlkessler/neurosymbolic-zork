LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then 'west'; the +10 was recorded on entering the Kitchen. Do this early.
2. Take the lantern, turn it on and keep it carried before any 'down'. Lit descents here were never fatal.
3. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Take portable items in new lit rooms and compare the score afterwards.
4. Use the exact command 'put painting in case'. 'put down painting' only drops it and recorded 0.
5. Studio 'up' leads to the Kitchen, then 'west' to the Living Room. Use this confirmed route to bring treasures home.
6. Try untested exits from lit rooms, such as the Studio, East Chasm and Gallery, before revisiting known rooms.
7. Try Up a Tree 'take egg' (+5 in earlier runs), then bank it in the case, before spending steps underground.
8. After a score gain, check inventory. Deposit treasures in the trophy case before leaving the Living Room again.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It recorded -10 and ended this episode after two earlier safe retreats.
2. Never generate the stale 'up' +25 goal. It is Living Room 'down', already collected. It caused the Kitchen/Attic loop that wasted roughly 35 steps.
3. Don't shuttle Kitchen, Attic, Behind House and Living Room, or Cellar and East Chasm. These loops recorded 0 each time.
4. Don't take the manual, coil, bottle, sack or knife without a stated need. All of these recorded 0.
5. Don't 'put down' or drop a treasure in the Living Room, then descend again. Leaving the painting unbanked wasted the trip.
6. Don't repeat a probe that already failed. The Troll Room was entered three times and was blocked each time.
7. Don't treat a stalled goal as a reason to re-run 'up' or 'down'. Pick an unexplored exit or a deposit instead.
8. Don't write state notes that contradict the action, such as 'the Cellar is dark' while carrying a lit lantern.
