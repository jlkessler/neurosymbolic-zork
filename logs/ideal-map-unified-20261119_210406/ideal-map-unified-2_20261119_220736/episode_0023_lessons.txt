LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Early surface sequence works: 'take egg' at Up a Tree (+5), then window and west into the Kitchen (+10), then bank the egg (+5).
2. Take a light source before descending, and confirm it with 'inventory'. This run carried the lantern into the Cellar, unlike the last one.
3. 'take art' in the Gallery gave +4, and 'put treasures in case' in the Living Room gave +6. Carry found valuables back and deposit them.
4. Trap-door 'down' gave +25 once, after the egg was banked. Treat it as a one-time event and never as a repeatable goal.
5. Keep the lantern in hand at all times. Dropping or stashing it left the Studio and Cellar pitch black.
6. When the lamp text says dim or nearly out, stop idle shuttling and spend remaining light on one untried exit or object.
7. After about 10 scoreless moves, name one specific untried exit or object, and 'examine' carried items. Do not repeat a known loop.
8. Credit points only to recorded score events, and check that state notes name the true room and darkness status each turn.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. 'south' from the dark Cellar met a grue: -10, death, episode over.
2. Don't drop, stash, or leave the lantern. 'put down lantern' in the Studio started a chain of dark moves ending in death.
3. Don't shuttle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio and Troll room. About 350 moves there produced no new room and no points.
4. Don't act on navigate_score_goal 'up' revisits. The +25 was a one-time 'down'. The goal stalled more than 15 times with zero gain.
5. Don't re-enter the Troll room from the Cellar repeatedly. Every visit produced no points, and the notes kept saying it was blocked.
6. Don't burn turns on open, close, shake, put and take with the sack, garlic or lunch. None of it scored anything.
7. Don't waste lamp fuel on idle loops. The lamp dimmed, and the resulting darkness preceded the death.
8. Don't skip untried exits above ground or in side rooms. The run ended with unexplored options that its own notes named.
