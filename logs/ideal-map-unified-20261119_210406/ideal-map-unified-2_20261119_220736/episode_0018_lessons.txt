LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree ('take egg' recorded +5), then go straight to the Living Room instead of detouring.
2. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 on Kitchen entry. Do it early.
3. Bank treasure with 'open case' then exact 'put X in case'. 'put egg in case' recorded +5; check the score afterward.
4. Before descending, push the rug, open the trap door, take the lantern, and turn it on. 'down' then recorded +25 once.
5. When the text says dark, retreat at once. Going down from the dark Attic avoided the grue; carry the lantern lit.
6. Pick up portable valuables in unusual rooms and bank them: Gallery 'take art' recorded +4, and the painting in the case +6.
7. After about 10 moves with 0 score, leave for an unvisited exit, such as Studio 'up', instead of re-probing known rooms.
8. Credit gains only to recorded score events. Label the +25 as Living Room 'down', and make state notes match the actual room and inventory.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the troll room. Troll attacks scored nothing, and this run ended -10 on that move.
2. Never generate or follow a navigate_score_goal 'up'. The +25 came from Living Room 'down'; the mislabel drove the aimless route.
3. Don't shuttle Cellar and East Chasm, or Gallery and Studio. Six crossings here scored 0; leave once a room is mapped.
4. Never throw, drop, or break the lantern. 'throw water at lantern' extinguished the light in an earlier run, and the dark move after it was fatal.
5. Never move when text says pitch black or the light is out. Stop and fix the light first.
6. Don't loop 'open bottle' and 'close bottle'. Dozens of cycles scored 0 and burned turns.
7. Don't enter dark rooms like the Attic with the lantern left behind. Check inventory before each stair, ladder, or descent.
8. While carrying treasure, don't detour to the mailbox or South House. Never drop or throw treasure; only 'put X in case' banks it.
