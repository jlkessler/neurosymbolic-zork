LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then 'west' into the Kitchen. The +10 was recorded on that west move.
2. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded +25. Treat the stored 'up' +25 goal as stale and use 'down'.
3. Before descending, take the lantern, turn it on, and confirm with inventory. Lit-and-carried matters more than lit-in-room.
4. If a new room says pitch black, stop. Fix light first (inventory, light source) before choosing any exit.
5. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. In lit new rooms, take portable items and compare the score before and after.
6. Putting the painting in the Living Room trophy case recorded +6. Deposit carried treasures there, then check the score.
7. Up a Tree 'take egg' recorded +5. Explore outdoor rooms beyond the house for items and new rooms.
8. The home return from underground was verified earlier, so return-home goals are valid. Carry treasures up and deposit them. Don't treat the trap door as a dead end.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while dark. Both the earlier death and this one (south from the Cellar, -10) came from moving in the dark.
2. Don't act on the stale 'up' +25 goal. It is really Living Room 'down'. Never revisit it from the Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery or Studio.
3. Don't cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio, or shuttle Kitchen and Living Room. Cycles recorded 0 each time.
4. Don't take the sack or lunch without a reason. Don't drop and retake them, or toggle the sack open and closed. These scored 0.
5. Avoid the Troll Room: it cost -10 earlier and preceded a death. Don't fight it or give it items.
6. Don't open the trophy case when carrying nothing to deposit. Opening it recorded 0.
7. Don't let state notes contradict the action, like 'light lantern' beside a move east. Verify with inventory.
8. After 10 moves with no score or new room, abandon the loop and try an untried exit, but only if lit.
