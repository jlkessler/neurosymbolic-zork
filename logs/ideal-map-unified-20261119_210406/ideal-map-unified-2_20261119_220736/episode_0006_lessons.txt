LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, open the window and go west. The +10 was recorded on the west move into the Kitchen.
2. In the Living Room, take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, then go 'down'. The +25 was recorded on 'down'.
3. Light the lantern and confirm it with 'inventory' or a room description before leaving the Cellar. Darkness preceded the grue death.
4. Take visible portable items in new rooms and check the score. 'take art' in the Gallery recorded +4.
5. List every exit of each new room and try each unvisited one once. Mark the room finished when all exits are tried.
6. Credit score only to recorded events: Kitchen west +10, Living Room down +25, Gallery take art +4.
7. Write state notes from the latest observation. If the lantern is lit, never write 'dark'; if unsure, check inventory.
8. After 10 moves without a score or new room, stop shuttling and try a different frontier exit, such as East Chasm's other exits, with light.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move west from East Chasm, or into any unseen exit, without confirmed light. That move recorded -10 and a grue death.
2. Don't shuttle between two rooms, like Studio and Gallery or Cellar and East Chasm. About 290 such moves scored 0.
3. Don't go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. The troll blocked the room, so there is no gain there.
4. Don't treat the stale 'up' +25 goal as valid. The +25 came from Living Room 'down', and Kitchen 'up' scored 0.
5. Don't re-enter the Studio or Gallery expecting new results. Their exits and items are already known.
6. Don't spend moves dropping or taking junk items like lunch or pepper. None of them scored.
7. Don't detour to the Attic or mailbox. Both previously scored 0 and cost early moves.
8. Don't ignore a score-0 streak. If the same exits keep repeating, change strategy instead of repeating routes.
