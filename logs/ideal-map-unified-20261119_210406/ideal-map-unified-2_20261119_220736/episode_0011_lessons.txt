LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: open window, then 'west'. The +10 was recorded on that west move into the Kitchen.
2. Up a Tree 'take egg' recorded +5. Open the trophy case and 'put egg in case' recorded another +5.
3. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, then 'down' recorded +25. The trap door shuts behind you.
4. Before 'down': 'take lantern', confirm it in inventory, turn it on, and only then descend.
5. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Putting the painting in the trophy case recorded +6. Take portable items in lit new rooms and compare scores.
6. If a room says pitch black, make no exit move. Check inventory and fix light first.
7. Follow the symbolic home route when it is confirmed. This run's five-move route back to the Living Room had no deviations.
8. Bank carried treasures in the trophy case before descending, then check the score to confirm the deposit.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move while dark. 'South' from the pitch-black Cellar cost -10 and killed the Actor. Earlier deaths also came from moving in the dark.
2. Don't generate the stale 'up' +25 goal. It is Living Room 'down', already done. Never pursue it from the Cellar or elsewhere.
3. Don't push rug, open trap door or go 'down' with no lantern in inventory. A note saying 'lantern needed' is not having it.
4. Don't assume you can retreat after descending. The trap door shuts behind you, and no underground return to the house is verified.
5. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Living Room, or cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. These loops recorded 0.
6. Don't take the sack or lunch without a reason. Don't drop and retake items, or toggle the sack open and closed. These scored 0.
7. Avoid the Troll Room. It cost -10 earlier and preceded a death. Don't fight it or give it items.
8. Don't open the trophy case when carrying nothing to deposit. Don't write plans that contradict the action taken.
