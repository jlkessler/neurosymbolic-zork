LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, then immediately go west; this episode recorded +10 on the west move into the Kitchen.
2. Test fixtures one step at a time, but credit score only to the action where it changes; +25 appeared on 'down'.
3. Before using the trap door, check inventory for a light source and light it before descending.
4. If arrival text says pitch black, make no compass move until light is available or a safe non-movement action is chosen.
5. Use plain compass moves for transitions and note each score delta, so reward and risk are attributed to the correct action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not move in pitch black; 'south' from the Cellar was followed by grue death and a -10 score penalty.
2. Do not descend the trap door without a lit light source; it crashed shut and the Cellar was pitch black.
3. Do not assume a safe route back to the Living Room after a door shuts behind you; verify it before exploring.
4. Do not shuttle between two rooms with no score; after about 10 such steps, change exit or action (earlier candidate, unseen this episode).
5. Do not enter rooms with hostile occupants unarmed or re-enter after combat damage (earlier candidate; not tested this episode).
