LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Enter an opened window or door immediately; entering the window gave a recorded +10 on the move into the Kitchen.
2. Take portable items and check the score line at once; 'take egg' gave a confirmed +5 at Up a Tree.
3. Test room fixtures such as the rug and trap door; push rug, open trap and down led underground, with +25 recorded.
4. Prefer plain compass moves, which confirmed transitions every time, over 'go around' moves that bounced between known forest rooms.
5. Before each move, list this room's untried exits and pick one. After 20 steps with no score and no new rooms, change area.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle among Forest Path, Clearing, Behind House, West House and North House once they show zero score and no new rooms.
2. Do not cycle take leaflet and put leaflet in mailbox; it repeated many times with only 'Taken.' and 'Done.' and no score.
3. Do not push carried items such as the egg, canary or nest through the grating; keep them held unless a score event supports it.
4. Do not move in pitch black after the trap door shuts; confirm a light source works first. A grue killed the Actor (-10) after a blind north and south.
5. Do not go underground without a verified route back; the trap door shut behind the Actor and the return to the Living Room was never confirmed.
