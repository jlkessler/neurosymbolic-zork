It took the lantern, then did push rug, open trap, down. The +25 was recorded on that final down move, not on the rug or door. Weak points: it turned the lantern on only after the trap door shut in the dark. It never tried 'up' to test a way back, so the return is still unverified. Steps 10-23 were a no-score shuttle between Cellar, East Chasm and the Troll room. The evidence shows a troll axe killed the Actor (-10), not a grue, and the lantern was on. It re-entered the troll room after an axe-hit warning, then issued a pointless 'take walls'. Next time: light the lantern before descending, test one return exit, and leave a room once it shows a threat.
