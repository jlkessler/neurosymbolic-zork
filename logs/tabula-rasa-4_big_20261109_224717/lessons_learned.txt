LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, then go west; +10 was recorded on the west move into the Kitchen.
2. Take and light the lantern before using the trap door; the Cellar entries with light caused no dark-room trouble, and +25 appeared on 'down'.
3. After taking a treasure-looking item, return to the Living Room, open the case and put it in; painting +6 and egg +5 were recorded.
4. Check each action's score delta separately; take art +4 and take egg +5 were separate pickups, not credited to movement.
5. Reuse confirmed routes home before long trips; Studio up to Kitchen, then west, reached the Living Room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not attack the troll with the knife; 'hit troll with knife' produced death and -10 on the final step.
2. Do not shuttle between Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio without score; roughly 20 steps went into zero-score cycles.
3. Do not revisit the Troll Room repeatedly after it blocks progress; the Actor entered it four times with no gain.
4. Do not start combat while an unused weapon is only noted; the sword was seen but never taken before the fight.
5. Do not make compass moves in pitch black without light; the earlier dark-Cellar death warning is retained but was not tested here.
