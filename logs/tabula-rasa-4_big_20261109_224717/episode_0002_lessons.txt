LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take obviously portable items early and check the score line right after; the first 'take egg' gave a confirmed +5.
2. Read observation text for new nouns and exits, such as the nest, leaves and the broken-item messages, before choosing the next action.
3. Use confirmed room transitions to build a mental map, then deliberately pick exits not yet taken.
4. After a score event, record where and how it happened, then move on to unexplored areas instead of staying nearby.
5. Examine objects in the start area, like the house exterior, to look for ways in before leaving the area.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat up/down or north/south room pairs after a pair has returned zero score and no new text.
2. Do not cycle put/take on the same item and container; 'Done.' and 'Taken.' with no score change means stop.
3. Do not drop or throw carried valuables in arbitrary places; the egg and canary read as 'broken' after drops, though the cause is unconfirmed.
4. Do not spend 500 steps on a goal with no progress, such as finding the Living Room, without trying a different approach.
5. Do not keep re-walking the same forest rooms; make a new exit, examine, or open attempt every few moves.
