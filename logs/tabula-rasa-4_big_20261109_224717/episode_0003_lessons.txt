LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take obviously portable items and check the score line immediately; 'take egg' gave a confirmed +5 at Up a Tree.
2. Probe house entrances such as the window; 'open window' gave confirmed text, so follow up by actually trying to enter.
3. Before each move, list exits of the current room not yet taken this episode and choose one of those.
4. After any score event, record the room and action, then head to unvisited areas instead of lingering.
5. Prefer plain compass moves, which confirmed transitions every time, over 'go around' moves that landed in different rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat north/south, up/down or east/west pairs between Forest Path, Clearing and Up a Tree after they return zero score and no new rooms.
2. Do not cycle 'put down leaves' and 'take leaves'; 'Dropped.' and 'Taken.' with no score change happened about ten times.
3. Do not use 'go around forest' or 'go around trees' as exploration; they are unreliable and mostly bounced between known forest rooms.
4. Do not open a window or door and then walk away; after 'open window' succeeds, try entering it before leaving.
5. Do not drop or throw carried valuables casually; the egg read as broken in earlier episodes, so keep them held until a safe place is found.
