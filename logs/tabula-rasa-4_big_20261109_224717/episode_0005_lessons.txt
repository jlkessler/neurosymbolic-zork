LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. After opening a window or door, enter it immediately; 'open window' then 'west' gave a recorded +10 into the Kitchen.
2. Take the lantern before going underground, and turn it on before descending, not after.
3. Test room fixtures one at a time, then check the score; push rug, open trap, down was followed by +25 on 'down'.
4. Use plain compass moves; they gave confirmed transitions every time here.
5. Right after arriving underground, test one return exit such as 'up' before exploring further.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle between two rooms, such as Cellar and East Chasm, with no score; after about 10 steps of this, try a different exit or action.
2. Do not re-enter a room where combat damage was reported; the troll room's axe hit was followed by death.
3. Do not issue nonsensical actions like 'take walls' in danger; use them only if they plausibly escape or solve the threat.
4. Do not descend with the lantern unlit; the trap door shut and the Cellar was pitch black.
5. Do not cycle take/put leaflet, 'go around', or put items in the grating; none of these ever scored.
