LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From Behind House, open the window and go west; 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10. Repeat this opening immediately, without detours.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then 'down'; the +25 was recorded on 'down'. Do this early.
3. Before descending, check inventory and confirm the lantern is carried and lit; the Cellar was still reported pitch black despite 'lantern is now on'.
4. On entering a room, read the text, list the exits, and try each untried exit or verb (look, up, examine) once.
5. Take visible valuables in new rooms, like the Gallery art (+4, prior episode), and keep them carried until banked.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never take a movement command in a pitch-black room; 'south' from the dark Cellar led to a grue death (-10, game over). Check light or 'look' first.
2. Do not shuttle West House/North House or put and re-take the leaflet; about 8 opening steps produced no score.
3. Drop the stale Maze 'take coins' goal when the route is unknown; it steered the early actions off-plan.
4. Avoid prior-episode fatal guesses: 'jump' at East Chasm, 'give troll painting' (-10, ended the game), and entering the Troll room.
5. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio (about 150 steps, no score, prior episode). After two repeats, change verb; try Studio 'look' and 'up'.
