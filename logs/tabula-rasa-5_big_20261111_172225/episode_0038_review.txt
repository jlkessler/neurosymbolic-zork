Historian episode review — episode 38
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

Confirmed this episode: the Actor scored +10 for 'west' into the Kitchen after opening the window, and +25 for 'down' after pushing the rug and opening the trap door. Those were its strongest decisions, and it also took the sword, lunch and garlic. The first ~8 steps were wasted shuttling West House/North House and putting the leaflet in the mailbox and taking it back. It never pursued the stale Maze 'take coins' goal, which is just as well, but that goal still shaped its early wandering.

The fatal error was 'south' in the pitch-black Cellar. The lantern had been turned on in the Living Room, yet the Cellar was still reported dark. The Actor never checked inventory or used 'look'. Its note said it was going north to the Troll room, which contradicts the action, so the reasoning was unreliable. 'South' ended in a grue death (-10 at the terminal transition). Next episode: verify light before descending and never move blindly. Navigation was mostly sound.
