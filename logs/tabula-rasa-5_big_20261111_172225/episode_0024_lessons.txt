LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10. Reuse this early, then head for the Living Room trap door.
2. Open the trap door and go 'down' from the Living Room with the lit lantern: +25 recorded at the Cellar.
3. Gallery 'take art' recorded +4. Return Studio, up, Kitchen, west, then 'put painting in case' for +6 before roaming.
4. Bank treasures immediately; this run deposited by about step 21, so the 35 points survived the later failure.
5. If about 20 moves bring no score change, mark the goal stale and try an unvisited exit instead of a known room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room. It killed the Actor again here (-10, 45 to 35), after an earlier death there.
2. Treat the Maze 'take coins' goal as unreachable. About 270 steps of chasing it never reached the Maze and gained nothing.
3. Don't loop Living Room, Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio, Kitchen and Attic. Repeated cycles showed zero score gain after step 21.
4. Don't take an exit your own notes call blocked or dangerous just because other options feel exhausted; explore forest or house-exterior frontiers instead.
5. Never jump at East Chasm. Never drop or extinguish the lantern, or wait, in the dark; earlier runs died doing these.
