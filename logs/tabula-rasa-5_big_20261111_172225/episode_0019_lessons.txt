LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Egg from Up a Tree, open Behind House window, enter Kitchen: all scored (+5, +10). Open the case first, then 'put egg in case' gave +5.
2. Take the lantern and turn it on before descending, as done this episode; confirm with inventory rather than assuming.
3. Chain prerequisites from observations: pushing the rug revealed the trap door, 'open trap' succeeded, and 'down' was followed by +25.
4. Picking up Gallery art scored +4 on pickup. Keep such valuables in inventory and carry them to the Living Room trophy case.
5. When carrying a treasure, let symbolic navigation route home; it moved Up a Tree to Kitchen to Living Room without detours.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't re-enter the Troll room or issue odd commands like 'take walls' there; the troll's axe killed the Actor (-10) with no weapon or plan.
2. Don't shuttle Cellar, Troll, East Chasm and Gallery without new information; about 25 moves gained nothing. Also avoid early West/North House back-and-forth.
3. Don't 'put down' or drop art to test scoring; it only drops the item. Scoring deposit needs the Living Room case.
4. Drop stale goals like Maze 'take coins' when the Maze is unreached and the underground route is unverified.
5. Don't descend without a verified return path: the trap door shut behind the Actor, and Cellar return stayed unverified. Verify inventory, not state notes.
