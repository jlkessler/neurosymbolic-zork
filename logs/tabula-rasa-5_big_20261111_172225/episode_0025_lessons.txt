LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: 'open window', then 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10. Go there directly from the start instead of wandering the forest.
2. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, go 'down' recorded +25 at the Cellar. Repeat early, but only with a lit lantern in hand.
3. Earlier runs recorded Gallery 'take art' +4 and 'put painting in case' +6. Treat these as candidates and retry once safely lit.
4. Deposit treasures in the trophy case as soon as they are carried; that is the only banking route evidenced so far.
5. If about 20 moves bring no score change, mark the goal stale and try an unvisited exit rather than a known room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go down the trap door until 'take lantern' succeeds and inventory shows it. This run turned it on but recorded no take, then arrived in pitch black.
2. In darkness, don't open containers, wait, or walk blindly. This run opened the sack, then moved south and was eaten by the grue (-10).
3. Never go north from the Cellar into the Troll Room; earlier runs died there.
4. Treat the Maze 'take coins' goal as unreachable. It drove most of this run's actions and never reached the Maze or scored.
5. Never jump at East Chasm, and don't loop Living Room, Cellar, Chasm, Gallery, Studio and Kitchen; earlier loops gained no score.
