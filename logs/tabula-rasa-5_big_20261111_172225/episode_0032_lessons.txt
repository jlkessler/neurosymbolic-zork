LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. At Behind House, 'open window' then 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10. Do this early when returning to the house.
2. Living Room: 'push rug', 'open trap', then 'down' recorded +25. Do this only after the preparation steps below.
3. Before descending, take the sword and lantern and confirm both with 'inventory'. This run never recorded 'take lantern' before the dark Cellar.
4. Take the egg at Up a Tree (+5), then 'put egg in case' in the Living Room (+5). Bank treasure before exploring.
5. At Gallery, 'take art' recorded +4. Check each new room for treasures and plan the carry-home route before collecting.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch black. 'south' in the dark Cellar ended in grue death (-10). Verify the light source and inventory first, or stay put.
2. Never interact with the thief ('give', 'take', attack). An earlier thief encounter at East Chasm killed us (-10).
3. Drop the stale 'take coins' at Maze goal. It was never reached and pulled this run into aimless wandering.
4. Do not shuttle Kitchen/Living Room/Attic/Behind House or use 'go around' and 'jump'. About 12 steps scored nothing; pick unexplored exits instead.
5. Stop sack and bottle open/close or take loops and East Chasm/Gallery/Studio/Cellar cycling. Leave a room after two visits without a score event.
