LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree, then open the window at Behind House and go west; both recorded gains (+5, +10).
2. In the Living Room, open the case and put the egg in once; the first deposit recorded +5.
3. Take the lantern before descending, and check inventory for any weapon in the house before going below.
4. After a trap-door descent, note which exits are untried (Cellar south, west ramp) and test one before any fight.
5. After each scoring action, re-check the score and the current goal; drop stale goals tied to rooms not yet visited.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't re-take and re-put the same egg; the take (-5) and put (+5) cancelled out, wasting steps.
2. Don't enter the Troll Room unarmed; it was entered three times, and the third entry ended in death (-10).
3. Don't shuttle Kitchen and Living Room with no goal; about four round trips scored nothing.
4. Don't pursue 'take coins' or Maze goals when the Maze is unvisited and far away; those goals were stale.
5. Avoid futile commands like 'open egg with lantern' and early West/North House bouncing; they cost moves with no score.
