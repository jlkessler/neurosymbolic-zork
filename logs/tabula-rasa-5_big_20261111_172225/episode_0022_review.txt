Historian episode review — episode 22
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's strongest decisions came after it reached Behind House. It opened the window and entered the Kitchen (+10 recorded on that move). In the Living Room it took the lantern, turned it on, pushed the rug, opened the trap door and descended. The descent carries the recorded +25, and the lit lantern fixes the earlier dark-descent failure. Symbolic navigation handled the opening route, so it earns no credit for that.

The weaknesses were all in the Cellar. It toggled north and south between the Cellar and the Troll Room five times with no score and no new information, then went north once more. The Troll Room's troll knocked it out for -10, ending the episode at 25. The stale 'take coins' goal was active during this stretch, though the evidence doesn't show it caused the moves. It also left the Kitchen and Living Room with no further inventory checks or item pickups, and never tried the Cellar's other exits.

Next time: light the lantern, then explore untested exits. Don't re-enter the Troll Room.
