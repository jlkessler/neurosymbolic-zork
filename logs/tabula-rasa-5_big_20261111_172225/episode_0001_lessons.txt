LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Early on, pick up reachable items and use score events to find what works; the egg, art and case deposits all produced recorded gains.
2. After a treasure pickup, carry it to the display case; repeated 'put egg in case' events show deposits can be rewarded.
3. Move on to unexplored rooms when a location shows nothing new; score came from fresh areas such as the Gallery.
4. Check lantern status before going underground and respond to 'dimmer' or 'nearly out' warnings by heading back toward light.
5. Record each new room and its exits so a verified route home exists before long underground sequences.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not repeat open/close/shake nest cycles after several zero-score results; the loop yielded nothing in dozens of steps.
2. Do not wander the maze rooms 67/68/69/70 for hundreds of steps without a mapping plan; confirmed movement cycles produced no score.
3. Do not ignore lamp warnings ('dimmer', 'nearly out'); the episode ended in grue death with a -10 score event.
4. Do not shuffle junk items (bottle, leaflet, lunch, pepper) between inventory and nest without a clue that justifies it.
5. Do not give or place items with a thief present without reason; handing garlic over gained nothing.
