LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree (+5), then 'put egg in case' in the Living Room (+5). Bank treasure before exploring.
2. Behind House: open window, then 'west' into Kitchen recorded +10. Test the window first when returning to the house.
3. Living Room: push rug, open trap door, go 'down' recorded +25. Take sword and lantern first; the trap door then shuts behind you.
4. Turn on the lantern immediately on arriving in the dark Cellar; it worked and prevented grue trouble.
5. At Gallery, 'take art' recorded +4. Check for treasures in each new room, and plan the carry-home route before collecting.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never interact with the thief ('give', 'take', attack). 'give thief garlic' then 'take garlic' at East Chasm ended in death (-10).
2. Do not shuttle East Chasm/Gallery/Studio/Cellar. Over 100 steps scored nothing; leave a room after two visits without a score event.
3. Stop open/close sack loops, garlic/manual drop-and-retake, and 'look around X' commands. None produced score.
4. Never emit 'take coins' at Maze or follow that stale goal; it was never reached and drove the looping.
5. Avoid 'north' from Cellar without troll preparation, and avoid 'jump' and 'go around'. Pick unexplored exits other than north instead.
