LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open with take egg (+5), west into Kitchen (+10), put egg in case (+5), trap door down (+25). All four were recorded this episode.
2. From Studio, test 'up' and every other untried exit first. Confirm painting banking by a score event and inventory, not by assumption.
3. Take Gallery art once (+4) and keep it. Deposit it in the case only after a verified route back to the Living Room.
4. Prefer unvisited rooms and unverified exits. Once an edge repeats twice with no score, switch goal and leave that room cluster.
5. Light the lantern before going down. Examine room features when the text hints at them, and check score and inventory after each action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from Cellar into the Troll Room. The troll kills, and earlier Troll Room visits scored nothing.
2. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. About 130 steps there scored 0; treat any three-time repeated move as a loop.
3. Do not ping-pong Maze rooms 63-70, 167, 65 and 66. About 85 steps after the coins gave no score and no new rooms.
4. Do not take the rusty knife or skeleton, or attack the thief. The result was a ghost curse, a lost sword and death at -10.
5. Do not set the Maze 'take coins' goal, and do not repeat drop/take pairs on the same item. Change approach after one failure.
