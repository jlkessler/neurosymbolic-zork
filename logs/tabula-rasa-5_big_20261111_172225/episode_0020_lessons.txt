LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg, open the window, enter the Kitchen, then open the case and put the egg in: +5, +10 and +5 were all recorded.
2. Light the lantern before descending. Pushing the rug, opening the trap door and going down was followed by +25; repeat that sequence.
3. Gallery art scored +4 on pickup. Carry it home and use 'put painting in case' in the Living Room, which gave +6.
4. Verify return routes by travelling them: Gallery→Studio→up→Kitchen→Living Room worked. The trap door reopened, so 'barred' notes were not final.
5. Let symbolic navigation handle known routes home while carrying treasure. Take over only to open the case, deposit, and check score.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never try 'jump' at East Chasm or similar edges: it ended the episode with -10 from 55 to 45.
2. Don't shuffle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio without new information. About 65 post-banking moves scored nothing; choose an untested exit or item.
3. Discard stale 'take coins' goals when the Maze is unreached and no route to it exists. They drove many aimless moves.
4. Don't give treasure to the thief or drop it to test mechanics. Keep valuables until the case deposit is confirmed.
5. Don't trust summary notes over observations: check inventory, score and room text, and distinguish verified outcomes from guesses before acting.
