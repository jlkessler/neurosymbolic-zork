LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then open the Behind House window and go west to the Kitchen (+10 recorded).
2. In the Living Room with the egg, put it in the opened trophy case, then re-read the score to confirm any gain.
3. Before any descent, read the leaflet and inspect Kitchen items and unused exits (west, up, chimney); this episode left them unexamined.
4. Treat earlier-episode score claims, like the Living Room 'down' +25, as candidates; verify prerequisites and the actual score change before relying on them.
5. After each score event, drop stale goals for places never reached, such as the Maze 'take coins' goal.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move in pitch-black rooms or without a lit light source; a prior grue death makes darkness the top risk.
2. Don't descend without verifying a return route; no route back from the Cellar is confirmed.
3. Don't wander the forest or backtrack Forest Path↔Up a Tree before pursuing a known score goal; four moves passed before the first score.
4. Don't end with the egg undeposited: this episode opened the case, then used a recovery_default 'turn on lantern' and stopped at no_safe_actions.
5. Don't repeat unverified claims (sword, Gallery art, Cellar round trips, lantern status); confirm each in the current observation first.
