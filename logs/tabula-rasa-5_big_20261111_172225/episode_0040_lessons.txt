LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then bank it with 'put egg in case' in the Living Room (+5 recorded) before descending.
2. At Behind House, open the window, then go 'west' into the Kitchen; +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it without detours.
3. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go 'down'; +25 was recorded on 'down'. Do this early.
4. Take and light the lantern before descending. After arriving anywhere dark, check inventory or look before any move.
5. Take visible valuables in new rooms, such as the Gallery art (+4 recorded in an earlier run), and bank them. Confirm each gain against its action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. Going 'south' from the dark Cellar recorded -10 (grue death) and ended the episode at step 19.
2. Do not set or follow the stale Maze 'take coins' goal; it is unreachable and has repeatedly misdirected the Actor's plans.
3. Do not enter the Troll room or give items to the troll; both led to deaths in earlier runs.
4. Avoid cycling Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio, and do not jump at East Chasm. After two repeats, change verb or leave the area.
5. Do not drop the lantern or toggle sack and lantern states. Do not pick up junk such as garlic or lunch; none of it scored.
