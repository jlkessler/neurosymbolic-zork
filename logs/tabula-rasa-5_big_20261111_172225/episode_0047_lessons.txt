LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen. The move recorded +10 and is a reliable early score step.
2. Take the egg from Up a Tree (+5 confirmed). After any treasure pickup, go straight to the Living Room and put it in the case.
3. Use symbolic navigation for Living Room returns. It completed Tree to Living Room in five confirmed moves. On arrival, immediately put the treasure in the case and check the score.
4. Take the lantern and turn it on before entering dark rooms such as the Attic. Do this before the first dark room.
5. After banking, prefer unvisited above-ground exits and new rooms. Try the Kitchen staircase/Attic with the lantern lit, and stop once the score stays flat.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never type 'jump' at East Chasm. It cost 10 points and ended an earlier episode. Avoid untested risky verbs near drops.
2. Ignore the Maze 'take coins' goal. No route was ever verified, and it steered this whole episode with no coins found.
3. Do not wander or pick up items while carrying an unbanked treasure. The egg was still in inventory at termination. Deposit first.
4. Do not loop Forest/Clearing/Behind House, Attic up/down, or Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio. Any edge repeated with no score means go elsewhere.
5. Do not take leaflet, garlic, sword or lunch, or open containers for no reason. None scored, and the actions displaced real exploration. Never go north from Cellar.
