LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the Up a Tree egg, open the Behind House window, and enter the Kitchen; both scored here (+5, +10).
2. Use 'put egg in case' after opening the case, then verify with score or inventory; 'put down' only drops items.
3. Before descending, 'take lantern', turn it on, and check inventory to confirm it is carried, since 'on' alone proved nothing.
4. Test each new exit or object (mailbox, rug, trap door) once, then act on what the observation reveals.
5. Treat remembered score actions as candidates; verify the current room, inventory, and observation before committing to them.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never issue a movement command after 'pitch black'; the first dark move in the Cellar ended in a grue death (-10).
2. Don't descend through a trap door without a plan for light and a way back; no Cellar return was verified.
3. Don't shuttle between the same two rooms (West House↔North House, Kitchen↔Living Room) without new information or a purpose.
4. Don't pursue stale goals like 'take coins' in the Maze when it is unreached and the route is unverified.
5. Don't trust state notes: 'lantern on' was not 'lantern carried', and skipped items such as the sword went unnoticed.
