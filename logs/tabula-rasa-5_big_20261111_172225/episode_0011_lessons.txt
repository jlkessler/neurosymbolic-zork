LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree, open the Behind House window, go west, and bank it in the case: +5, +10, +5 recorded.
2. Move the rug, open the trap door, and go down: +25 recorded on 'down'. Take the lantern first and light it in the dark.
3. The trap door shut behind the player after descending. Treat it as one-way and prepare gear before going down.
4. Carry the Gallery art (+4) back to the case and put it in rather than dropping it underground.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and replace goals tied to places never reached, such as the Maze.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't 'jump' at East Chasm or similar edges: it killed the player (-10). Examine the surroundings and try other exits first.
2. Don't oscillate Cellar and Troll Room: about seven round trips scored nothing. After one blocked or hostile visit, try a different exit or item.
3. Don't follow stale 'take coins'/Maze goals from Living Room or Cellar; the Maze was never visited and the goal misdirected exploration.
4. Don't take and drop the same item repeatedly; the art went take/drop twice, and a third drop in the Troll Room left it unbanked.
5. Don't descend with only a lantern: no sword or other weapon was taken, and the troll attacked. Read the leaflet and list the exits.
