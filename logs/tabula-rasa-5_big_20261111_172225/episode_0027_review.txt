Historian episode review — episode 27
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor's opening was strong. It banked 55 points quickly: egg +5, Kitchen +10, Cellar +25, art +4, and 'put painting in case' +6, plus a second recorded egg deposit of +5. It returned home by step 35, so treasure banking worked. Its decisions after that were poor. About 370 steps produced no score, mostly cycling Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. It kept following the Maze 'take coins' goal even though its own notes called the route blocked. It also ran filler actions: repeated open/close sack, and taking and dropping the manual, garlic and lunch. The worst decision was dropping the lantern in East Chasm and then moving north in the dark. That led to the grue death (-10). Navigation transitions were confirmed and not the problem; the repeated Maze goal came from the symbolic layer, but the Actor never overrode it. Next time, keep the lantern, mark stalled goals stale, and prefer unvisited exits.
