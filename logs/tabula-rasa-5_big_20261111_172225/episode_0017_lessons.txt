LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then open the Behind House window and go west to the Kitchen (+10 recorded).
2. In the Living Room, put the egg inside the opened case, not just 'put down'; then check inventory and score to confirm any gain.
3. Before descending, take the lantern, light it, and confirm via inventory that it is carried; consider the skipped sword too.
4. Read the leaflet and examine untried Kitchen exits (west, up, chimney) before committing to the trapdoor.
5. Treat remembered score claims as candidates and verify the score change; drop stale goals for unreached places like Maze 'take coins'.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in pitch-black rooms: after 'pitch black' appears, a grue killed the Actor on the second dark move (-10).
2. Don't descend without a verified return route; the trap door shut and no route back from the Cellar is confirmed.
3. Don't wander the forest or oscillate Forest Path↔Up a Tree before pursuing a known score goal.
4. Don't leave the egg on the floor or go underground with treasure unbanked; 'put down' only drops it.
5. Don't trust state notes without evidence: 'lantern on' did not mean it was carried, and 'egg taken' was wrong.
