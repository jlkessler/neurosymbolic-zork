LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House: 'open window' then 'west' recorded +10 at the Kitchen. Do this early and directly, without wandering the forest.
2. Living Room: open the trap door and go 'down' for +25 at the Cellar. Carry the lantern, lit, before going.
3. Up a Tree 'take egg' gave +5 and Living Room 'put egg in case' +5. Bank treasures promptly after collecting them.
4. Gallery 'take art' gave +4, and 'put painting in case' in the Living Room gave +6. Carry the art straight home.
5. After banking, if about 20 moves pass with no score, mark the goal stale and try an unvisited exit, such as the Attic or Forest paths.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Treat the Maze 'take coins' goal as unreachable. It was reissued about 15 times, never reached, and ignoring it cost nothing.
2. Don't shuttle among Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio. About 370 steps after step 35 scored nothing. Cap revisits to a room at two.
3. Never drop the lantern. Dropping it in East Chasm and then moving north in the dark caused the grue death (-10). In darkness, don't move or fiddle.
4. Avoid filler actions: repeated open/close sack, taking and dropping the manual, garlic and lunch. None of it scored.
5. Don't go north from the Cellar toward the Troll Room. An earlier run died there and this run's notes call the route blocked.
