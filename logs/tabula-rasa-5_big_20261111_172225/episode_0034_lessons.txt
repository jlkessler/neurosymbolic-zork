LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take a light source and confirm it is in inventory before descending into any dark area; verify it is lit.
2. Pick up visible portable valuables when safe; taking the Gallery art gave a recorded +4.
3. After a score event, note the exact room and action that produced it, and test only compatible follow-ups.
4. When a route closes behind you, read exit text and inventory, then test untried exits once each.
5. Break stagnation with a different kind of action, such as examine, inventory or an untried exit, instead of another movement.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not cycle between the same rooms more than twice without new score, inventory, or map evidence.
2. Do not toggle drop/take on the same item (leaflet, painting); it burned turns and gained nothing.
3. Do not linger or manipulate inventory in the Troll room while the troll is attacking; leave or avoid it.
4. Do not pursue stale goals, such as a remembered Maze 'take coins', before confirming the current room and route.
5. Do not move through dark rooms; stop and fix lighting first, since darkness risks death.
