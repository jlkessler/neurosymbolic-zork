LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the Up a Tree egg, open the Behind House window, go west: +5 and +10 were recorded. Then bank the egg in the case (+5).
2. Take and light the lantern before descending; this episode it was carried and lit. Also find a weapon first, since none was taken.
3. Push the rug, open the trap door, go down: +25 recorded on 'down'. The door shut behind you, so treat it as one-way.
4. Take the Gallery art (+4 recorded). Keep it in inventory and plan a route back; it was dropped unbanked this time.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and discard goals for unreached places, such as the Maze 'take coins' goal.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't re-enter the Troll Room: 'north' from Cellar ended the episode with -10 and a troll attack. Earlier entries scored nothing.
2. Don't shuttle Cellar, Troll Room, East Chasm and Gallery for no new information; about 20 moves yielded only +4.
3. Don't chase stale Maze 'take coins' goals; the Maze was never reached. Check the current exits and the score before each move.
4. Don't drop treasure like the art away from the Living Room case ('put down art' scored nothing). Unbanked items are at risk.
5. Don't descend one-way without a plan: the trap door shut and no return route was found. Check the weapon, light and exits first.
