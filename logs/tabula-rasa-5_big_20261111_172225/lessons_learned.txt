LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree and 'put egg in case' in the Living Room before descending; both recorded +5 here.
2. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen; this recorded +10 again.
3. In the Living Room, open the trap door and go down; 'down' recorded +25. Then take the art in the Gallery (+4).
4. After taking the art, go Gallery north to Studio and try 'up' at once; it led to Kitchen, then Living Room.
5. Once back in the Living Room with art, try 'put art in case' and check the score; this was untested here.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not cycle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio; it cost most of the episode with zero new rooms or score. Pick an untried exit or 'look'.
2. Ignore the Maze 'take coins' goal; it was proposed about 20 times and never reached. Treat it as unreachable and explore elsewhere.
3. Do not spend moves on put down or take of lunch, pepper or manual; none of them scored.
4. After returning home, do not go back down the trap door without a new plan; explore untried above-ground exits first.
5. Avoid north from Cellar into the Troll room and never give items to the troll. The final 'north' from Gallery ended the episode at -10; cause unverified, so 'look' first.
