LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg at Up a Tree (+5 recorded), open the trophy case, then 'put egg in case' (+5 recorded) in the Living Room.
2. At Behind House, open the window and go 'west'; +10 was recorded on that move. Repeat it without detours.
3. In the Living Room: push rug, open trap, go 'down'. +25 was recorded on that move; do it early.
4. Before 'down', take the lantern, check inventory to confirm it is carried, then turn it on. Re-check on arrival in the Cellar.
5. After the Cellar arrival, use 'look' or 'inventory' first. If the room is still dark, do not move; try fixing the light instead.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. Moving 'north' from the dark Cellar recorded -10 (grue death), ending this episode at step 34.
2. Do not follow the stale Maze 'take coins' goal; it is unreachable and drove about 12 aimless forest moves this run.
3. Do not assume 'turn on lantern' lights the next room. This run's Cellar stayed dark, so the lantern likely was not carried.
4. Avoid looping Forest, Forest Path, Clearing and CanyView. After two repeats of a loop, change area or verb.
5. Do not enter the Troll room, give items to the troll, or jump at East Chasm; each caused a death earlier.
