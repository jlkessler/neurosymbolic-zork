LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and enter the Kitchen (+10 recorded), then put the egg in the trophy case (+5 recorded).
2. Before 'down' at the Living Room, take lantern and sword, light the lantern, push the rug, and open the trap door; 'down' recorded +25.
3. Arm yourself before meeting the troll: the sword knocked it out this run, and a follow-up attack killed it.
4. Take visible loose items in new rooms (coins gave +10 recorded) and compare the score after each take.
5. When two or three rooms alternate with no score, check inventory and room text, then pick an unexplored exit instead of repeating the pair.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle among maze rooms 64/167/67/68/70 and the Dead End; about 60 steps there scored nothing after the coins.
2. Do not keep exploring underground while carrying unbanked coins; establish and verify a route to the Living Room first.
3. Do not descend the trap door without a return plan: it shut behind the Actor and the way back was never verified.
4. Do not fight or linger beside the thief while carrying treasure; the run ended there with a recorded -10.
5. Do not repeat zero-effect commands like drop/take axe or 'look around knife/thief'; try a distinct verb or exit.
