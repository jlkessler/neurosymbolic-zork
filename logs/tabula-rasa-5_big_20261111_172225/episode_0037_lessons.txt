LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From Behind House, open the window and go in; the move west into the Kitchen recorded +10. Repeat this opening first.
2. Examine room features early: moving the rug and opening the trap door preceded the recorded +25 on 'down'. Follow new passages immediately.
3. Take visible valuables in new rooms, as with the Gallery art (+4 recorded), and keep them carried.
4. On entering a room, read its text, list every exit, and try each untried exit or verb (up, down, examine) once before leaving.
5. When a symbolic goal stalls for 10+ commands, stop moving and test local interactions on room features instead of re-walking known rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio; about 150 steps there produced no score. After two repeats, change verb or exit.
2. Never enter the Troll room again or give it a valuable; 'give troll painting' killed the Actor (-10) and ended the episode.
3. Treat 'jump' at the East Chasm as lethal (prior-episode evidence, not retested here); avoid risky guesses with no supporting clue.
4. Drop stale remembered goals like Maze 'take coins' when the route is unknown or blocked; they steered the early actions off-plan.
5. Do not drop and re-take the leaflet or pick up manual-type clutter; the toggling produced no score and wasted steps.
