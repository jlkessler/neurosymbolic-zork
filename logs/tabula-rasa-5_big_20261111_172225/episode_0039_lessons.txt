LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. From Behind House, open the window and go west; 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10. Repeat this opening without detours.
2. In the Living Room, push the rug, open the trap door, then go 'down'; +25 was recorded on 'down'. Do this early.
3. Take visible valuables in new rooms, such as the Gallery art (+4 recorded this run), and keep them in inventory.
4. Before leaving a lit room, 'look' and check inventory to confirm the lantern is carried and actually lighting the room.
5. Verify each recorded score event against the action that produced it, and repeat only actions with a confirmed positive delta.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move or drop the lantern in a pitch-black room. This run, dropping it in the dark Troll room and then moving 'south' led to a grue death (-10).
2. Do not enter the Troll room again; the Cellar's north exit led there and an axe attack was recorded.
3. Ignore the stale Maze 'take coins' goal; it was re-issued about 20 times and drew the Actor into loops with no score.
4. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Studio; about 350 steps earned zero. After two repeats, change verb or leave the area.
5. Stop toggling sack and lantern (put/take/open/close) and picking up junk like garlic and lunch; none of it scored and it wasted dozens of steps.
