LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open with take egg (+5), window west (+10), put egg in case (+5), then trap door 'down' (+25). All were recorded; repeat without detours.
2. Light the lantern before descending. Push and open room features (rug, trap door, case) when a scene hints at them; this exposed the +25 route.
3. The trap door shut behind the Actor and no return route was found. Bank treasures in the case before going down.
4. Gallery 'take art' scored +4 once. Take valuables once and keep them; do not drop and re-take them to 'test' scoring.
5. When stalled, pick an exit never used (Studio's other exits, Attic, new Cellar options) rather than a known room; new rooms are the only source of fresh points.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not re-enter Troll Room from Cellar. About eight visits scored nothing, and the last 'north' knocked the Actor out and ended the game at -10.
2. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery and Troll Room. About 150 steps there scored 0; after two repeats of an edge, change goal.
3. Do not follow the 'take coins' Maze goal when the route is blocked; it was reissued about ten times and never became reachable.
4. Do not repeat put down/take on the same item (leaflet five times, art once). Each pair wasted steps and changed nothing.
5. Never pick up the rusty knife or hit things with it (-10 earlier). Avoid Maze ping-pong rooms 67-70 and 167.
