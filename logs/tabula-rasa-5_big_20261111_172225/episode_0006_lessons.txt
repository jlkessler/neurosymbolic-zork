LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10 recorded on entry), and bank the egg in the case (+5 recorded) before going underground.
2. Before 'down': take lantern and sword, push rug, open trap door ('down' recorded +25). Light the lantern at once in the dark Cellar.
3. Arm yourself before the Troll room: the sword knocked the troll out and the axe killed it. Then list its exits and test untried ones.
4. Take loose valuables (coins gave +10 recorded) and compare score after each take, then immediately plan a verified route to bank them.
5. If rooms cycle about ten steps with no score, stop and pick an untried exit or a mapped route back, such as Maze 70 east to the Troll room.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not loop Maze 64/167/67/68/69/70 and the Dead End; 60+ steps after the coins scored nothing.
2. Never switch the lantern off in the maze or dark; the run ended in darkness (grue text, -10 recorded) right after the thief appeared.
3. Do not explore underground with unbanked coins; the trapdoor shut and the return to the Living Room was never verified.
4. Skip zero-effect commands: 'look around X', wait, and repeated drop/take. Do not take the skeleton; it brought a ghost curse.
5. When the thief appears, leave by a known mapped exit with the lantern on, not a blind unmapped move while carrying treasure.
