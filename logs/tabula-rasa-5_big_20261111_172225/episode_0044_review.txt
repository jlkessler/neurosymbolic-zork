Historian episode review — episode 44
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

The Actor opened well: the recorded events show take egg +5, west into the Kitchen +10, put egg in case +5 and trap door down +25. It also took the Gallery art for +4. Navigation was clean, with no deviations and every transition confirmed, so the failures were the Actor's choices. After the coins (+10 in room 167) it spent roughly the last 85 steps looping among Maze rooms 67, 68, 69, 70, 167, 65 and 66 with no score and no new rooms. Its alternating explore/investigate goals never produced a new exit. It then took the rusty knife, took the skeleton (a ghost cursed it), and hit the thief with the sword. Taking the sword back ended the game at -10. Note that the briefing places the death in the Troll Room and the final score at 39, while the episode log shows a Maze death and 55. The log also records the painting deposit twice (+6 each), which I cannot fully reconcile with the briefing. Next episode: leave the Maze, test Studio 'up' and other untried exits, and stop any edge once it has repeated twice.
