Historian episode review — episode 23
================================================

Review source: llm
Lessons: 5 positive + 5 negative (configured per category: 5)

Early play was effective. The Actor reached the Kitchen (+10) and descended with the lantern (+25). It then took the art (+4) and put the painting in the case (+6), banking treasure by about step 21. After that it made no progress. Roughly 370 steps were spent cycling Living Room, Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio and Kitchen, reopening the trap door about 20 times. A stale 'take coins'/Maze goal, repeated about 10 times, never reached the Maze. The lamp dimmed and a thief appeared. The Actor then dropped the lantern in the Cellar, waited in the dark, moved, and a grue killed it (-10, ending at 35). That death is what the trajectory shows. The briefing's troll-room death is unconfirmed here, and the trajectory is truncated. Most moves were goal-driven navigation, but the lantern drop was the Actor's own error. Next time, keep the light on, abandon any goal after one failed attempt, and probe unexplored exits instead of looping.
