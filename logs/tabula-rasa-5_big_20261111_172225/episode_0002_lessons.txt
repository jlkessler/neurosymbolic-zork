LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Repeat the opening that scored: take egg in the tree, enter the Kitchen from Behind House, put the egg in the case, then go down.
2. Deposit carried treasures in the trophy case promptly; 'put egg in case' gave +5 and 'take art' gave +4, so repeat both.
3. Take items in newly visited rooms and watch the score line immediately; the Gallery pickup scored, so check each fresh room before moving on.
4. When the symbolic layer reports stagnation, change action type (examine, read, inventory, look) or take an exit never tried, instead of repeating moves.
5. On entering dark underground areas, note lamp warnings and verify a route back to the Living Room before going deeper with valuables.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not cycle Cellar, East Chasm, Gallery, Studio and Troll rooms; about 200 zero-score moves in this loop wasted the episode.
2. Do not toggle open/close sack or move the painting in and out of the sack repeatedly; every attempt scored zero.
3. Do not use 'jump' near chasms or edges; it caused the -10 score event and the death that ended the episode.
4. Do not keep exploring after 'lamp dimmer' notes without a plan to get back to light; the Actor noted the warning and ignored it.
5. Do not enter one-way underground areas, such as after the trap door shut, without a plan for returning treasures to the Living Room.
