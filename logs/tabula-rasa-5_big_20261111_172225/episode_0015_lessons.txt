LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then open the Behind House window and go west into the Kitchen (+10).
2. Open the trophy case and put the egg in it immediately; this episode recorded +5 for that deposit.
3. Rug, open trap door, down scored +25 and is repeatable early, but the door shut behind the player with no return route verified.
4. Before any one-way descent, inspect the Kitchen and Living Room for useful items and unused exits.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and drop stale goals for places never reached, such as the Maze 'take coins' goal.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move around in pitch-black rooms: Cellar to Troll Room and then south ended in a grue death (-10) after two moves.
2. Don't descend through the one-way trap door without light or a verified way back; the Cellar was pitch black.
3. Don't open the episode with forest wandering when a known score goal exists; four moves passed before the first score.
4. Don't leave Kitchen table items (sack, bottle) and other exits unchecked before committing to a dangerous area.
5. Don't trust unverified claims about repeated Cellar<->Troll trips, a sword, or art in the Gallery; none appear in this episode's evidence.
