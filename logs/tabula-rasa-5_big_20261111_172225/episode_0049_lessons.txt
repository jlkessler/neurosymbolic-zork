LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen. This sequence recorded +10 in repeated episodes.
2. Take the egg from Up a Tree and 'put egg in case' in the Living Room before any descent. Banking it first was never achieved this episode.
3. Before leaving the Living Room for the cellar, take the lantern and light it. Moving the rug and opening the trap door led down, recorded +25.
4. In the Gallery, take the art, then try the unexplored exits (Studio and beyond), including 'up', before backtracking.
5. When a stagnation warning appears, try an untested exit or verb in the current room. Do not repeat the last two moves.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar. Troll Room entries ended in a -10 death. Never use 'give' on the troll or 'hit troll'. Treat Maze 'take coins' as unreachable.
2. Do not descend the trap door with an unbanked treasure. It barred behind the Actor and no way home was ever verified.
3. Do not cycle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio. About 65 steps passed here with zero new rooms or score; repeated edges mean choose a new exit.
4. Do not spend opening steps on mailbox, leaflet, sack, garlic or sword takes. None scored. Go straight to the egg and window.
5. Do not follow the 'return route' goal by retracing edges already confirmed dead. Test untried directions like 'up' and 'down' in each room instead.
