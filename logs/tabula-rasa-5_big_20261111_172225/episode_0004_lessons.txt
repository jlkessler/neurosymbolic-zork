LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, enter the Kitchen, and bank the egg in the trophy case; the recorded gains were +10 and +5.
2. Before using 'down' at the Living Room, move the rug and open the trap door; this recorded +25.
3. Take and light the lantern before going underground, as this run did, so dark rooms stay visible.
4. Check the Gallery and Studio first for takeable items; 'take art' gave a recorded +4.
5. When two rooms alternate with no score, try a new verb or untried exit instead of repeating the move.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not enter the Troll room unarmed or repeatedly; the living troll blocked exits, hit the Actor, and killed it, costing -10.
2. Do not shuttle Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio for dozens of steps; about 80 steps there scored nothing.
3. Do not drop and re-take the art; the repeats scored zero and it was never carried to the trophy case.
4. Do not pick up items such as the manual while a hostile troll is present; the action ended the run.
5. Do not descend the trap door without a return plan: it shut behind the Actor, and the route home was never verified.
