LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree, open the Behind House window, go west: +5 and +10 were recorded this episode.
2. Push the rug, open the trap door, go down: +25 recorded on 'down'. The door shut behind the player, so treat it as one-way.
3. Take the Gallery art (+4 recorded), then carry it back for banking rather than dropping it underground.
4. Use 'put in case' at the Living Room with treasures before descending. This episode only dropped the egg on the floor, unbanked.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and replace goals tied to places never reached, such as the Maze.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't move around in pitch darkness: a grue killed the player (-10) on 'north' from East Chasm. Secure a light source before going underground.
2. Don't descend unprepared: no lantern or weapon was taken, and the trap door shut with no known return route to the Living Room.
3. Don't pursue stale 'take coins'/Maze goals: the Maze was never reached, and the goal drove the Actor to wander Cellar, East Chasm and Gallery.
4. Don't use 'put down' or 'drop' for treasure in the Living Room. It scores nothing and leaves the item unbanked.
5. Don't chase unverified exits while carrying treasure; check available exits and the score first. East Chasm/Gallery shuttling gave no new points.
