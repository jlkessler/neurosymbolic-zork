LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window, enter the Kitchen (+10 recorded on entry), then open the case and put the egg in (+5 recorded). Confirmed again.
2. Take the lantern, push the rug, open the trap door, then 'down' (+25 recorded). Turn the lantern on immediately in the dark Cellar.
3. Take the sword as well as the lantern before descending; earlier evidence says the sword is needed against the troll. Check inventory before the Troll room.
4. On entering a room, read its listed exits and try an untried one instead of retracing. Record each confirmed transition so a return route exists.
5. Take loose valuables and compare the score after each take. Bank them only when a verified route home exists.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Do not shuttle Cellar/Troll room north-south; about eight such moves scored nothing and ended in death. Leave by a different exit or fight armed.
2. Never 'open sack' or 'take garlic' while the troll is attacking. Non-defensive item actions there ended in death (-10 recorded).
3. Do not bounce Kitchen/Living Room with no new goal; about six moves were wasted. Move only toward a stated target.
4. Never switch the lantern off underground, and never loop the Maze 60+ steps (reported in earlier runs; not seen this episode).
5. Skip zero-effect commands such as 'look around X', wait, and repeated drop/take. Do not take the skeleton; earlier evidence says it brings a ghost curse.
