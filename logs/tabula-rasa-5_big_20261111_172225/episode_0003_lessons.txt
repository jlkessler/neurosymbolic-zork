LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House and enter the Kitchen, then bank the egg in the open trophy case before descending; this gave +10 and +5.
2. Light the lantern immediately on entering a dark area; the Cellar and Troll room were explored safely while it stayed lit.
3. Check score after each fresh pickup: coins in the skeleton room gave +10, so examine and take items in newly reached rooms.
4. When a fight stalls, keep retrying with the wielded weapon; repeated 'hit troll with sword' eventually killed the troll without losing the run.
5. When position repeats, switch to untried exits or a different action type (inventory, examine, read) rather than re-walking known room pairs.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never throw or break your light source; 'throw sword at light' smashed the lamp, and moving in the dark caused the grue death (-10).
2. Do not wander Maze rooms 67-70, 167 and 64 in loops; about 70 moves there produced only the coins (+10).
3. Do not drop and re-take the sword or axe, or use 'look around sword'; each repeat scored zero and wasted steps.
4. Do not carry the coins through dangerous dark areas: they were never banked, and death ended the chance to deposit them.
5. Do not descend through the trap door without a plan for return: it shut behind the Actor and no route home was found.
