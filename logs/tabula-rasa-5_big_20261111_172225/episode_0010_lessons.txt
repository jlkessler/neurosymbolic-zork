LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree, open the Behind House window, go west, and bank it in the case: +5, +10, +5 recorded.
2. Move the rug and open the trap door, then go down: +25 recorded on 'down'. Take the lantern first and light it in the dark.
3. Treat trap-door descents as one-way: the door shut behind the player. Prepare gear and weapon before going below.
4. After picking up scoring items like the +4 art, carry them home and bank them instead of dropping them.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and current goal. Replace goals tied to far-away or unvisited rooms.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Don't go underground with only a lantern. No weapon was taken, and the episode ended in death (-10) after hostile encounters.
2. Don't oscillate Cellar and Troll Room with no new action; four round trips scored nothing. Try an untried exit or use an item.
3. Don't follow stale 'take coins'/Maze goals while in the Living Room or Cellar; the Maze was never visited.
4. Don't take and drop the same item repeatedly: the art was taken, dropped, retaken and dropped again, leaving it unbanked in the Gallery.
5. Don't issue vague commands like 'look around thief' next to a hostile character; the second one coincided with the -10 death.
