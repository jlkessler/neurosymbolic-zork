LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the egg in Up a Tree (+5 recorded), then open the Behind House window and go west for the Kitchen (+10). Skip the forest detour.
2. After opening the trophy case, immediately put the egg in it and re-read the score; this episode stopped before depositing.
3. Rug, trap door, down scored +25 earlier. The door shut behind the player and no return route is verified, so prepare light and exits first.
4. Take the Gallery art (+4 recorded) and carry it back to the case instead of leaving it behind.
5. After each score event, re-read the score and drop stale goals for unreached places, such as the Maze 'take coins' goal.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never repeat Cellar<->Troll Room trips: they led to the troll knocking the agent out and -10. No sword was ever taken.
2. Don't oscillate among Cellar, Troll Room, East Chasm and Gallery without new information; about 20 moves yielded only +4.
3. Don't drop the art in the Gallery: 'put down art' scored nothing and left it unbanked. Treasure counts only once it is in the case.
4. Don't open the mailbox or wander the forest when a known goal exists; five moves passed before the first score.
5. Don't end with exits untried: check Kitchen and Living Room exits and the table items before no_safe_actions. Don't descend one-way without a weapon.
