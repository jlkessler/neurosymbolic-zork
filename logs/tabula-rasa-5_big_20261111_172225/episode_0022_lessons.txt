LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Take the lantern, turn it on, then push rug, open trap door, go down. This sequence recorded +25 on descent, with light confirmed.
2. Verified route to Behind House: west, go around trees, west, go around forest, west. Then open window and go west for Kitchen +10.
3. Gallery art gave +4 on pickup; 'put painting in case' banked +6. Return via Studio, up to Kitchen, west to Living Room; deposit immediately.
4. Treat the lantern as a required tool, not a treasure. Check inventory and light before any descent or dark room.
5. Compare the score after each risky or scoring action. Egg +5 and case +5 appear in earlier records after the Kitchen entry.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never move in a pitch-black room. An earlier run went south in the dark Cellar and a grue killed it, -10.
2. Never 'jump' at East Chasm or similar edges. An earlier run died that way, -10 from 55 to 45.
3. Don't toggle Cellar and Troll Room. Five round trips scored nothing, and the next north entry meant a troll knockout, -10 (35 to 25).
4. Distrust history goals like 'down' or 'take coins'; they ignore light and threats. Reassess surroundings before obeying them.
5. Don't carry unbanked treasure near the thief; the painting was stolen once. Bank it before roaming, and skip forest detours.
