LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Behind House with window open: 'west' into the Kitchen recorded +10 once. Don't expect repeat points from re-entering; look for new sources afterward.
2. Up a Tree 'take egg' gave +5 and Living Room 'put egg in case' +5. Bank a treasure promptly, then leave it in the case.
3. An earlier run recorded +25 going 'down' through the Living Room trap door to the Cellar. Unverified this episode; carry a lit lantern if retrying.
4. In dark rooms like the Attic, retreat at once. No grue death occurred this run, so keep that discipline.
5. After about 20 moves without score, commit to an unvisited exit or item rather than revisiting known rooms, and compare score before and after each action.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Mark Maze 'take coins' stale after one failed attempt. It was reissued about 13 times this run with zero progress and about 250 wasted steps.
2. Avoid 2-cycles such as Kitchen↔Living Room, Kitchen↔Behind House and Clearing↔Forest Path. Visit a room at most twice without a new score event.
3. Never take the egg back out of the trophy case. It cost -5, the redeposit gave +5, and it was done twice for net zero.
4. Skip filler: open/close window, Attic in-and-out, and 'go around forest/trees' loops through Forest rooms #76-78. None scored.
5. Never drop the lantern; an earlier run died to a grue (-10) after moving north in the dark. In darkness, don't wander or fiddle.
