LESSONS LEARNED
================================
POSITIVES TO REPEAT
1. Open the window at Behind House, then go west into the Kitchen. The move recorded +10, and the same sequence recorded it again this episode.
2. Take the egg from Up a Tree (+5), return to the Living Room, and 'put egg in case' (+5 confirmed). Bank before exploring.
3. Use symbolic navigation for Living Room returns. It took the egg home in a few confirmed moves, with no detours.
4. Take the lantern and turn it on before entering dark rooms like the Attic. The Actor retreated from the dark Attic without it.
5. After banking, head for unvisited above-ground exits and new rooms. Stop any area that gives no score or new room within a few moves.

NEGATIVES TO AVOID
1. Never go north from the Cellar (Troll room). Three entries ended in a -10 terminal attack. Never type 'jump' near drops, and treat the Maze 'take coins' goal as unverified.
2. Do not go below the trap door while carrying an unbanked treasure. The door shut behind the Actor, and the return route was never verified.
3. Do not take and drop the same item repeatedly. The art was picked up and dropped several times with no score and never banked.
4. Do not loop Cellar/East Chasm/Gallery/Studio or Kitchen/Living Room. Any edge repeated with no new room or score means go somewhere else.
5. Do not spend opening steps on mailbox and leaflet fiddling, or on needless takes (sword, garlic, sack). None of them scored. Prioritize the egg and window.
